## What Happens If We Swap the Orientation?

For real boundary spectrum,

$$
m_B(z)=\sum_\gamma\frac{m_\gamma}{\gamma-z}
$$

is Herglotz in the upper half-plane:

$$
\operatorname{Im}m_B(z)\ge0,\qquad z\in\mathbb C_+.
$$

If we reverse orientation, then

$$
-m_B
$$

is anti-Herglotz in `C_+`, or Herglotz after swapping the preferred half-plane.
This is harmless. It is the same geometry with time reversed.

Thus a **global negative sign** does not break mathematics. It changes the
chosen direction of time/current/phase.

What breaks is **not negative orientation**. What breaks is **indefiniteness**.
An off-line FE cluster puts zeros at

$$
\gamma+i\delta,\quad -\gamma+i\delta,
\quad
\gamma-i\delta,\quad -\gamma-i\delta.
$$

No choice of upper versus lower half-plane removes all interior atoms. If
`C_+` is future, the upper atoms break analyticity. If `C_-` is future, the
lower atoms break analyticity. So off-line zeros are not a wrong orientation;
they are broken K.
