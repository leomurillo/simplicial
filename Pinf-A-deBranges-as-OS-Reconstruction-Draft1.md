## Abstract

Constructive quantum field theory bridges a Euclidean (statistical) theory and a Lorentzian (unitary, positive-energy) one by a single axiom: **reflection positivity** (Osterwalder–Schrader). We show that the program's two sides of the Riemann Hypothesis are the two ends of one such bridge. The **Euclidean side** is the Weil explicit-formula system, with the multiplicative scaling group $\mathbb R^\times_+$ as Euclidean time and the functional equation $s\leftrightarrow1-s$ as the time reflection $\theta$; the reflection pairing $\langle\theta f,f\rangle$ is the Weil quadratic form. The **Lorentzian side** is the de Branges space of $E_B=\Xi+i\Xi'$, with the dilation generator $S$ as the time-translation generator and the prolate operator $W_\lambda=-S^2+V$ as the Hamiltonian. The Neeb–Ólafsson reflection-positivity theory, together with the de Branges structure theorem and the Herglotz/Bernstein–Widder representation, identifies these as an **OS reconstruction**: reflection positivity $\iff$ the reconstructed scaling semigroup $e^{-tH}$ is positive-energy ($H\ge0$) $\iff$ the de Branges canonical-system Hamiltonian $H(t)\succeq0$ $\iff$ $m_B=-\Xi'/\Xi$ is Herglotz $\iff$ RH. We make the dictionary precise, prove the load-bearing equivalences on a finite de Branges model exactly (the Krein–Langer index of the Nevanlinna–Pick matrix counts off-line zeros), and identify what reflection positivity buys: the reconstruction theorem makes "manufacture the archimedean positivity" into "prove the reconstructed scaling semigroup is positive-energy," a one-sided (positive scaling-time), complete-monotone, pointwise energy condition — distinct from the exhausted heat-flow monotones, and resting on independent theorems rather than analogy. It is a re-vision, not a proof: the positivity remains RH.

**Keywords.** Osterwalder–Schrader reflection positivity, reconstruction theorem, de Branges spaces, Hermite–Biehler, Herglotz/Nevanlinna–Pick, canonical systems, Krein–Langer index, complete monotonicity, Bernstein–Widder, Lax–Phillips, scaling group, positive-energy semigroup, Weil explicit formula, arithmetic energy condition.

## 1. The two sides, and the claim

The program meets RH from two directions that have always looked different. On one side is the **Weil explicit formula** and its quadratic form $W(f)$: a Euclidean, statistical object, the trace of the scaling action against test functions, with $W(f)\ge0\iff$ RH (Weil's criterion). On the other is the **de Branges / Hermite–Biehler** apparatus: $E_B=\Xi+i\Xi'$, the Herglotz function $m_B=-\Xi'/\Xi$, the canonical system — a Lorentzian, spectral object, with $E_B$ Hermite–Biehler $\iff$ $m_B$ Herglotz $\iff$ the zeros are real $\iff$ RH.

These are not two coincidentally-equivalent criteria. They are the **Euclidean and Lorentzian ends of one Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction**, and the bridge between them — reflection positivity — is a theorem (Neeb–Ólafsson [NÓ]). The functional equation is the time reflection; the scaling group is Euclidean time; the de Branges space is the reconstructed Hilbert space; the prolate operator is the Hamiltonian; and **RH is the statement that the reconstructed scaling semigroup is positive-energy.** This section's claim is that the assembly is rigorous (each arrow an independent theorem) and, per the literature, unclaimed — the live Connes–Consani program realizes the same positivity through Sonin/prolate compression without ever invoking OS or Lorentzian language.

## 3. The reconstruction theorem and the three faces of positivity

The content that lifts the dictionary above a mere analogy is the following chain of independent theorems.

**3.1 (Neeb–Ólafsson [NÓ]).** A continuous function on $\mathbb R$ is reflection-positive (with respect to $\theta(\tau)=-\tau$ and the half-line $\tau\ge0$) iff it is a Laplace transform $\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda|\tau|}\,d\mu(\lambda)$ of a positive measure $\mu$; the OS reconstruction then produces a Hilbert space and a **contractive, positive-energy** semigroup $e^{-\tau H}$ ($H\ge0$) with spectral measure $\mu$, and a unitary one-parameter group by analytic continuation (Lax–Phillips dilation [LP]).

**3.2 (Herglotz $=$ OS two-point function).** A Herglotz/Nevanlinna function $m$ is exactly $\langle(A-z)^{-1}\varphi,\varphi\rangle$ for a self-adjoint $A$ with positive spectral measure; its Laplace transform is completely monotone (Bernstein–Widder). Hence $m_B$ Herglotz $\iff$ $m_B$ is an OS two-point function $\iff$ the reconstructed $H$ has the zeros as a real spectral measure.

**3.3 (de Branges $=$ canonical system [dB],[Ro]).** $E_B$ is Hermite–Biehler iff $\mathcal H(E_B)$ is a genuine (positive) de Branges space iff there is a canonical system $Y'=zJH(t)Y$ with **trace-normalized $H(t)\succeq0$**. So Hermite–Biehler $\iff$ pointwise positive-semidefinite canonical Hamiltonian $\iff$ (3.1–3.2) positive-energy reconstruction.

The three faces of the *same* OS positivity, all $\iff$ zeros on the line:

- **(F1/F2) Reproducing-kernel / Herglotz positivity** — the OS Hilbert space exists and $H\ge0$: the Nevanlinna–Pick matrix of $m_B$ is positive semi-definite. Its number of negative eigenvalues is the **Krein–Langer index** $\kappa=$ the number of off-line zeros (generalized Nevanlinna; this is the program's resolvent-Gram index, N10).
- **(F3) Complete monotonicity** — the half-line OS axiom: the scaling two-point function $S(\tau)=\langle e^{-\tau H}\varphi,\varphi\rangle$ is completely monotone (Bernstein–Widder), equivalently its moment Hankel is positive semi-definite. *(This additive moment face is marginal — the Tower Theorem; it is the shadow, not the lever.)*
- **(HB) Hermite–Biehler margin** — $|E_B(z)|>|E_B(\bar z)|$ on $\mathbb C^+$, the holomorphy/positive-energy side.

## 4. The reduction: RH as positive-energy

Assembling §3:
$$\mathrm{RH}\iff m_B\ \text{Herglotz}\iff E_B\ \text{Hermite–Biehler}\iff H(t)\succeq0\iff\text{the scaling semigroup is positive-energy}\iff\text{reflection positivity}.$$
The OS reconstruction is the bridge that makes the implication *reflection positivity $\Rightarrow$ the positive-energy spectral realization* a theorem rather than a wish. Three consequences sharpen the open target without weakening it:

1. **One-sidedness.** By 3.1, reflection positivity need only be verified on positive scaling-time; the negative-time data are determined by $\theta$. The target is a half-line condition.
2. **Complete monotonicity.** By 3.1–3.2, that half-line condition is the **complete monotonicity** of the scaling two-point function (Bernstein–Widder), a classical-analysis property with its own toolbox (Bernstein functions, the Hausdorff/Stieltjes moment problem).
3. **A pointwise energy condition.** By 3.3, positive energy is the *pointwise* positivity $H(t)\succeq0$ of the canonical Hamiltonian along scaling time — an arithmetic null/dominant **energy condition** (P∞ §4), not a monotone. This is the precise sense in which the missing archimedean positivity is "an energy condition that keeps the light cone causal."

## 6. The Hermitian lift: the arithmetic stress-energy tensor

*Certificate: `empirical/p_infinity/probe_complex_Q_hermitian_lift.py` — exact.*

The OS reconstruction is naturally read as a relativistic field theory: the scaling time, the Lorentzian metric (P∞), and a wave whose positivity is RH. We state it precisely, with the prior-art corrections.

**6.1 The form was always complex-Hermitian (a correction).** The Weil functional is *standardly* a Hermitian (sesquilinear) form on **complex** test functions: RH $\iff\sum_\rho\hat g(\rho)\,\overline{\hat g(1-\rho)}>0$ for all nonzero complex $g$ (Bombieri, *Remarks on Weil's quadratic functional*, Atti Lincei 11 (2000); [Bo]). It is moreover *provably equivalent* to its restriction to **real even** test functions (Barner–Schwartz space; Li's reduction). So there is no missing "complexification": the historical form already is the Hermitian one, and the real-even form already detects RH. What the Hermitian/complex structure supplies is not a stronger criterion but the right **instrument** — it exhibits the momentum current that a single-state diagonal $\langle\psi|\psi\rangle$ cannot.

**6.2 Energy density and current (the certified split).** For the de Branges wave $E=\Xi+i\Xi'$, the probability current on the real axis is, exactly,
$$J=\operatorname{Im}\big(\overline{E}\,E'\big)=\Xi\,\Xi''-(\Xi')^2=-\,L_1(\Xi),\qquad L_1=(\Xi')^2-\Xi\Xi'',$$
the negative of the Laguerre form (verified $J+L_1\equiv0$). A purely real wave $\psi=\Xi$ has $\operatorname{Im}(\bar\Xi\,\Xi')=0$ — zero current: the flow lives entirely in the imaginary $i\Xi'$ phase. Organizing the Lorentzian form (P∞) as a stress tensor:
$$T_{00}=\text{energy density}=\text{the diagonal}\ (-2q,-2;\ \text{the }|a|\le2\sqrt q\ \text{bound, P∞ §4}),\qquad T_{0i}=\text{momentum flux}=\text{the off-diagonal}.$$
The off-diagonal momentum is the prime flux: **geometric** ($\mathbb F_q$) $T_{0i}=-a$ (real, the Frobenius trace — load-bearing: $a=0$ is causal but *vacuous*); **analytic** (de Branges) $T_{0i}=J=-L_1=u_B\,|E|^2$-type, the Herglotz field — and the two are OS-dual.

**6.3 The unification.** The genuine *null/dominant* energy condition — the full causal condition, which by construction needs the off-diagonal momentum — is therefore
$$T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu\ge0\iff \text{current positivity}\ u_B=\operatorname{Im} m_B\ge0\iff\text{Herglotz}\iff\text{reflection positivity}\iff|a|\le2\sqrt q\iff\mathrm{RH}.$$
This **merges P∞ §4 (energy density) and §3–§5 (the current / Herglotz / reflection positivity) into one tensor**: they are $T_{00}$ and $T_{0i}$ of a single Lorentzian stress-energy tensor, and the complex-Hermitian form is the instrument that makes $T_{0i}$ expressible. $$H_{\mathrm{OS}}\succeq0\ \text{(generator of the scaling semigroup; bounded below; }=\text{RH)}\qquad\text{vs.}\qquad K=-\log\Delta\ \text{(modular Hamiltonian)}.$$
By the KMS condition $K$ is **reflection-symmetric in its spectrum** ($J K J=-K$) and is therefore *unbounded both above and below* — it is never $\succeq0$. The modular flow $\Delta^{it}$ is a *symmetry* of the vacuum, not a positive-energy evolution. Hence "the cyclic–separating vacuum of the scaling semigroup yields the modular operator" produces $K$, not $H_{\mathrm{OS}}$: **positive energy cannot be read off $\Delta$.** Casting the prime flux $a$ as a modular operator gives a $K$-type object, whose spectral symmetry is the *functional equation* (the $\theta$-pairing of zeros across the line — already a theorem, §7.4), not their *positivity* (RH).

**8.3 The arrow runs the other way (Borchers / Bisognano–Wichmann / Wiesbrock) $[\mathsf P]/[\mathsf A]$.** The genuine link between modular theory and energy is *opposite* to "modular theory $\Rightarrow$ energy condition." **Borchers' theorem:** if $U(s)$ has positive generator $P\ge0$, fixes $\Omega$, and $U(s)\mathcal V\subseteq\mathcal V$ for $s\ge0$, then
$$\Delta^{it}\,U(s)\,\Delta^{-it}=U\!\big(e^{-2\pi t}s\big),\qquad J\,U(s)\,J=U(-s).$$
**Bisognano–Wichmann** *uses* positive energy to identify the modular flow with a geometric boost/dilation; **Wiesbrock's** converse shows that $+$half-sided modular inclusions are *equivalent* to positive-energy translation groups. In every case **positivity is the hypothesis and geometric modular covariance is the conclusion** — modular theory does not bootstrap the energy condition, it presupposes it. What it *does* deliver is a relabel: arch-domination becomes "the inclusion $\mathcal V_s=U(s)\mathcal V\subset\mathcal V$ is $+$half-sided modular," i.e. the Borchers commutation relation holds for the scaling flow on $\mathcal H(E_B)$. This is the **fifth equivalent face**, beside §6.3's chain:
$$\mathrm{RH}\iff H(t)\succeq0\iff u_B\ge0\ (\text{Herglotz})\iff\text{reflection positivity}\iff |a|\le2\sqrt q\iff\ \mathcal V_s\subset\mathcal V\ \text{is}\ +\text{hsm (Borchers)}.$$
It is exact and, to the extent the literature shows, unstated in the program — but it is a reformulation, not a closure: the $+$hsm property *is* the positivity.

**Proposition 8.3a (the $ax+b$ obstruction: no finite modular witness) $[\mathsf P]$.** The fifth face is *intrinsically infinite-dimensional*, and this is provable in one line. Differentiating the Borchers relation at $t=0$ with $U(s)=e^{isP}$, $H:=\log\Delta=H^\ast$, gives the affine ("$ax+b$") commutation
$$[\,H,\,P\,]=2\pi i\,P.$$
In finite dimension this forces $P=0$: if $H\psi=h\psi$ then $H(P\psi)=(h+2\pi i)(P\psi)$, but $H$ Hermitian has *real* spectrum and $h+2\pi i\notin\mathbb R$, so $P\psi=0$ for every eigenvector — hence $P=0$. (Equivalently $0=\operatorname{tr}[H,P]=2\pi i\operatorname{tr}P$, so $\operatorname{tr}P=0$, and with $P\succeq0$, $P=0$.) The affine dilation–translation group has **no nonzero finite-dimensional Hermitian realization** of the translation generator; the obstruction is moreover *quantized at the modular period* — the least singular value of $P\mapsto[H,P]-2\pi i P$ is exactly $2\pi$, independent of dimension (the trace/diagonal direction). **Consequence.** There is *provably no finite modular witness of RH*: positive-energy modular covariance can be tested only on the full (infinite-dimensional) scaling representation. This is the modular-theory form of the program's "finite witnesses are necessary-not-sufficient" (Remark 5.2) — and it pins the *reason*: the obstruction is the non-existence of finite unitary representations of the $ax+b$ group. The two facts modular theory *does* deliver finitely — the firewall (§8.2: $\operatorname{spec}\log\Delta$ symmetric about $0$, verified for every standard subspace) and the existence/circularity (§8.4: $(\Delta,J)$ constructible unconditionally) — therefore carry, as they must, *no* RH discriminant. Certified `probe_modular_borchers_obstruction.py` (kernel dimension $0$, PSD-floor $\ge2\pi$, firewall and existence to machine $\varepsilon$).

**8.4 The circularity (guardrail) $[\mathsf A]$.** Tomita–Takesaki is a *structural* theorem with no positivity hypothesis: every standard subspace yields a modular $(\Delta,J)$ (Rieffel–van Daele), just as every cyclic–separating vector does. So the modular apparatus exists **unconditionally**. By the Faltings–Hriljac guillotine, *a structure obtained for free cannot decide RH*: if causality of the primitive cone fell out of $\Delta$ alone, $|a|\le2\sqrt q$ would be automatic, contradicting its equivalence to RH over $\mathbb Q$. The energy condition must *enter* as the hypothesis of §8.3 (the positivity of $P$/the $+$hsm property); it cannot be a *conclusion* of the generic modular machinery. This is the exact analogue of §7.2 ($\exists\,\mathcal Q\iff$ RH): constructing the modular flow is free, but proving it is *geometric* (Borchers-covariant for the scaling group) is the theorem — and that is RH.

**8.5 Bost–Connes already instantiates "prime flux as modular data" $[\mathsf P]$.** The Bost–Connes system [BC] realizes $\zeta$ as the partition function of a $C^\ast$-dynamical system whose modular/KMS structure carries the primes, with a phase transition (spontaneous symmetry breaking) at inverse temperature $\beta=1$. Its well-documented limitation is *precisely the program's marginality*: the transition sits at the **pole** $\beta=1$, and the modular/KMS data do **not** pin the zeros to the critical line. So "cast $a$ as a modular operator" inherits the Bost–Connes ceiling rather than escaping it — consistent with §8.2 (the modular Hamiltonian sees the FE/pole symmetry, not the zero positivity). The live Connes–Consani line accordingly realizes the positivity through prolate/Sonin compression (convergence $\to$ RH, [CCM]), *not* through KMS.

**8.6 The only modular route, and where it lands: the prolate closed form $[\mathsf C]$.** Proposition 8.3a removes every *finite* modular hope and tells us exactly what the genuine object is. The de Branges space $\mathcal H(E_B)$ embeds isometrically (division by $E_B$) into a Hardy/Paley–Wiener space, and for the chiral half-line the modular flow of the natural standard subspace is *the dilation* — this is the Bisognano–Wichmann content. So the closed form is forced:
$$\Delta^{it}\ \text{is the scaling-by-}2\pi t\ \text{dilation}\ \iff\ \text{Borchers holds}\ \iff\ E_B\ \text{Hermite–Biehler}\ \iff\ \mathrm{RH}.$$
The modular content of RH is therefore: **does the arithmetic scaling flow on $\mathcal H(E_B)$ *become* the modular dilation?** Its obstruction is the deviation of $E_B$ from exact scaling-covariance — the archimedean/arithmetic defect — and Connes–Consani's prolate operator $W_\lambda=-S^2+V$ is precisely the finite-$\lambda$ approximant whose convergence $\widehat{k}_\lambda\to\Xi$ ([CCM] 2310.18423 §6.5; 2511.22755 Lem 7.2) *is* the statement that in the limit the flow becomes geometric. So the modular route does **not open a new front**: it lands on the same open prolate-convergence step the program already tracks — now equipped with a geometric meaning (the limit is where the modular flow turns into a boost) and a no-shortcut theorem (8.3a: nothing finite can substitute).

The one genuinely importable *tool*, if any, is then the operator-algebraic front-end for that infinite-dimensional limit: the **modular nuclearity / split-property** criteria (Buchholz–Wichmann phase-space bounds [BW]; the map $\Delta^{1/4}U(s)$ being nuclear/trace-class), the AQFT counterpart of the [CCM] convergence lemma. The open question — worth a probe, not a claim — is whether a modular-nuclearity estimate on the $\mathcal H(E_B)$ scaling inclusion is provable from phase-space / heat-kernel data *without* assuming the positivity it is meant to deliver. **Section 8.7 settles this in the negative and names the mechanism.**

**8.7 The nuclearity front-end closes negative: positivity is the precondition, not the estimate $[\mathsf P]$ (mechanism) / $[\mathsf C]$ (RH).** Push the modular-nuclearity bound to the point where positivity would enter, and it enters not as an estimate but as the *existence of the symbol*. Continue the Borchers relation $\Delta^{it}U(s)\Delta^{-it}=U(e^{-2\pi t}s)$ to imaginary modular time $t=-\tfrac i4$ (so $e^{-2\pi t}=e^{i\pi/2}=i$):
$$\Delta^{1/4}\,U(s)\,\Delta^{-1/4}=U(is)=e^{-sP}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \Delta^{1/4}U(s)=e^{-sP}\,\Delta^{1/4}.$$
The infinitesimal form is exactly the §8.3a commutator $[\log\Delta,P]=2\pi i\,P$ with $\log\Delta=-2\pi D$ ($D$ the dilation generator, $\Delta^{1/4}=e^{-(\pi/2)D}$); verified exactly. Hence the modular-nuclearity norm *is a heat trace*,
$$\big\|\Delta^{1/4}U(s)\big\|_1\ \sim\ \operatorname{Tr}\big(e^{-sP}\,\Delta^{1/4}\big)\ =\ \sum_n e^{-s\omega_n}\,w_n,$$
with $\omega_n$ the energy spectrum (the prolate $\tfrac14+\gamma_n^2$) and $w_n$ the always-present modular UV regulator. **The moment positivity enters is the symbol $e^{-sP}$ itself.** Purely modular norms $\|U(s)f(K)\|_1=\|f(K)\|_1$ are $s$-independent and carry *no* localization decay; the entire decay-in-$s$ comes from $e^{-sP}$, and $e^{-sP}$ is a bounded (trace-class) operator for $s>0$ **iff $P$ is bounded below — i.e. iff positive energy, iff RH** (the continuation $s\mapsto is$ converges in the upper half-plane only under the Paley–Wiener spectrum condition). So heat-kernel data is not data one *has* positivity-free: $e^{-sP}$ is the object that *exists* iff RH. A single negative-energy mode (an off-line zero, $\omega_\ast<0$) makes the trace *grow* in $s$ rather than decay, and the norm diverges. The handle therefore **collapses into §8.4** — but now with the mechanism pinned, and with the precedent confirming the direction (Buchholz–Lechner [BL] prove modular nuclearity *downstream* of a positive-energy one-particle space, never as its source). *Honest residue:* each finite-$\lambda$ prolate $W_\lambda$ is a bounded-below Schrödinger operator, so each finite-$\lambda$ heat trace is unconditional; what RH costs is *uniformity in $\lambda$* — the limit $P=\lim_\lambda W_\lambda$ staying positive — which is again the prolate convergence. Two faces of one wall: (A) symbol-existence of $e^{-sP}$; (B) limit-uniformity of the heat bound. Certified `probe_modular_nuclearity_heat_bound.py` (exact generator algebra; convergent heat trace on the real zeros vs divergence on a negative mode).

**8.8 Where the modular thread lands: the heat trace is Cramér's function on the Weil spine $[\mathsf P]$ (identity) / $[\mathsf C]$ (RH).** The §8.7 object can be realized exactly, and doing so welds the entire modular thread to the program's Weil-positivity spine (N14/N15). Take the heat-kernel test function $h_s(r)=e^{-sr^2}$ — the natural §8.7 choice (the Gaussian is the prolate/squared heat kernel) — in the Riemann–Weil explicit formula. Then the modular-nuclearity heat trace is *exactly*
$$2\!\!\sum_{\gamma>0}\!e^{-s\gamma^2}\;=\;\underbrace{2e^{s/4}}_{\text{pole}}\;-\;g_s(0)\log\pi\;+\;\underbrace{\tfrac1{2\pi}\!\int h_s(r)\,\mathrm{Re}\,\psi(\tfrac14+\tfrac{ir}2)\,dr}_{\text{archimedean}}\;-\;\underbrace{2\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,g_s(\log n)}_{\text{primes}},$$
$g_s(u)=(2\sqrt{\pi s})^{-1}e^{-u^2/4s}$, verified to $\sim\!10^{-42}$ (mpmath dps $40$, zeros via `zetazero`). Two consequences. **(i) Identification.** The left side is Cramér's function $V(\tau)=\sum_{\gamma>0}e^{i\gamma\tau}$ evaluated at $\tau=is$ (Cramér 1919; Guinand [Cr]), a *classically understood* object whose singularities sit at $\tau=\pm m\log p$ — open only in whether every $\gamma$ is real. So §8.7's "the heat semigroup $e^{-sP}$ exists" is *literally* Weil's criterion / arch-domination, and the modular nuclearity object is Cramér's $V$: the modular thread (§8) and the Weil spine (N14/N15) are the *same* analysis in two languages. **(ii) The margin is pole-driven.** Computing the balance, the positive driver is the *pole* term $2e^{s/4}\approx2$ (the $s=1$ pole of $\zeta$); the archimedean and prime terms are *both* subtractive ($\approx-1.4\!\to\!-2$ and $\approx-0.8\!\to\!-0.07$ as $s:0.3\!\to\!0.04$), and the surviving margin is their small positive residual $=2\sum_{\gamma>0}e^{-s\gamma^2}>0$. This refines "arch-domination" into *pole-domination over (archimedean $+$ prime)*, independently reproducing the Connes-route finding that the pole is load-bearing while the primes remain in play. *Honest status:* exact consolidation, not a new lever — the identity is unconditional, the positivity (all $\gamma$ real) is RH. The unconditional upside remains the Pascadi semi-primorial sub-family (a separate, arithmetic thread). Certified `probe_explicit_formula_heat_kernel.py`.

## 9. Honest status

**Rigorous $[\mathsf P]$:** the OS dictionary (§2) and the three-face equivalence on the finite model (§5) are exact; the reconstruction bridge (§3) rests on the independent theorems of Neeb–Ólafsson, de Branges, Bernstein–Widder, Lax–Phillips, and Pick. The synthesis — *the de Branges/Herglotz RH apparatus is the OS reconstruction of the reflection-positive explicit-formula system, with the scaling group as time* — is, to the extent the literature shows, unclaimed.

**Relabeling, honestly $[\mathsf A]$:** at the level of the statement, "RH $=$ reflection positivity" is Weil positivity ($=$ RH); the renaming buys nothing by itself. The deposit is the *bridge and the toolbox*: the reconstruction theorem, the half-line / complete-monotonicity reduction, and the pointwise energy-condition reading $H(t)\succeq0$. The SUSY-QM language of §7 is a *third* such relabeling (supercharge $\mathcal Q$, $H=\mathcal Q^\dagger\mathcal Q$, BPS $=$ zeros), again $[\mathsf A]$: its value is the dictionary plus the three guardrails — the supercharge is $\theta$-graded not $\chi_3$-graded (§7.3), the anomaly-free identity is the FE while RH is the residual *sign* (§7.4), and the Witten index is a bounded FE-defect count, not the growing $N(T)$ (§7.5). It does not bypass $\exists\,\mathcal Q\iff$ RH (§7.2). Modular theory (§8) is a *fourth* relabeling, again $[\mathsf A]$: its deposit is the fifth equivalent face — arch-domination $\iff$ the scaling inclusion $\mathcal V_s\subset\mathcal V$ is $+$half-sided modular (Borchers) — plus two firewalls (the modular Hamiltonian $K=-\log\Delta$ is KMS-symmetric, *not* the OS $H_{\mathrm{OS}}\succeq0$, §8.2; and Tomita–Takesaki is free, so by the Faltings–Hriljac guillotine cannot supply positivity, §8.4). The arrow runs *positivity $\Rightarrow$ geometric modular covariance* (Bisognano–Wichmann/Borchers/Wiesbrock), never the reverse; Bost–Connes already carries the primes as modular data and stalls at the pole $\beta=1$, not the line (§8.5). One *new $[\mathsf P]$ result* falls out — **Proposition 8.3a**, the $ax+b$ obstruction: $[\log\Delta,P]=2\pi i\,P$ has no nonzero finite-dimensional Hermitian solution (obstruction quantized at $2\pi$), so the fifth face is intrinsically infinite-dimensional and there is *provably no finite modular witness of RH*. This is the structural reason behind "finite witnesses necessary-not-sufficient" (Rem 5.2), and it shows the only modular route is the infinite-dimensional prolate closed form, which lands on the *same* open [CCM] convergence step (§8.6). The last candidate handle — modular nuclearity — **closes negative (§8.7, $[\mathsf P]$ mechanism):** the Borchers continuation $t\mapsto-\tfrac i4$ turns $\Delta^{1/4}U(s)$ into the heat semigroup $e^{-sP}$, so the nuclearity norm is a heat trace whose very *symbol* $e^{-sP}$ is bounded iff $P\succeq0$ iff RH. Positivity is the precondition for the heat object to exist, not an estimate one supplies; the front-end collapses into §8.4, mechanism named. Finally §8.8 realizes the §8.7 heat object exactly: with the heat-kernel test function the modular-nuclearity trace $=$ pole $+$ archimedean $-$ prime (Riemann–Weil, verified to $\sim10^{-42}$), so it *is* Cramér's function $V(is)$ and §8.7's positivity *is* Weil's criterion — welding the modular thread to the Weil spine (N14/N15); the surviving margin is **pole-driven** (the $s=1$ pole, with archimedean and prime both subtractive), reproducing the Connes-route "pole is load-bearing" finding. Net deposit of §8: a fifth equivalent face, two firewalls, one new $[\mathsf P]$ no-go (8.3a), a precise negative on the nuclearity handle (8.7), and an exact identification of the modular object with Cramér's function on the Weil spine (8.8) — no new lever, but the modular route fully mapped, welded to the program's positivity spine, and the open step confirmed to be the [CCM] convergence.

**Open $[\mathsf C]$, $=$ RH:** prove $H(t)\succeq0$ for the genuine $E_B=\Xi+i\Xi'$ — equivalently, the complete monotonicity of the arithmetic scaling two-point function, equivalently reflection positivity of the explicit-formula theory. This is the same positivity that over $\mathbb F_q$ is free (the surface and its Frobenius exist; §P∞.2) and over $\mathbb Q$ must be manufactured at the archimedean place. de Branges himself pursued the canonical-Hamiltonian positivity and did not close it; the OS framing adds the reconstruction theorem, the one-sided/complete-monotone reduction, and the energy-condition reading, but it does not bypass the positivity. The additive moment face is marginal (Tower Theorem); the genuine handles are the Herglotz/Pick and Bernstein–Widder machinery applied to the scaling one-parameter group. No lattice/Gaussian-domination toolbox is available — the explicit formula has no known positive local realization, which would itself be the hard theorem.
