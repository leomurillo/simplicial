### Structural mechanism — fully identified

From `Arch-Structural-Theorems-Draft1.md`, Theorem 1 (arch atomic structure) + Corollary 2 (arch H-positivity, J-negativity) provide the structural setup. The joint prime cumulant decomposes as

$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N) = \mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N) - \mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N).$$

Interpreted as signed measures: $\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}} = \sigma^{\mathrm{total}} - \sigma^{\mathrm{arch}}$, where

- $\sigma^{\mathrm{arch}}$ is **positive atomic on $(-\infty, 0)$** (Theorem 1, unconditional).
- $\sigma^{\mathrm{total}}$ is **positive atomic on $(0, \infty)$ under GRH** (atoms at $\gamma_\rho^{-2} > 0$), with possible complex/negative-axis atoms if off-line zeros exist.
- $\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}}$ is therefore a signed measure with positive part on $(0, \infty)$ (the zero atoms) and negative part on $(-\infty, 0)$ (exactly $-\sigma^{\mathrm{arch}}$).

For test vector $v$ with $q(s) = \sum v_i s^i$:
$$v^T J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}} v = \int s\, q(s)^2 \, d\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}}(s).$$

**On $(0, \infty)$:** $s > 0$ and $d\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}} \ge 0$ → integrand $\ge 0$.
**On $(-\infty, 0)$:** $s < 0$ and $d\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}} = -d\sigma^{\mathrm{arch}} \le 0$ → $s \cdot d\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}} \ge 0$ → integrand $\ge 0$.

**Both pieces contribute non-negatively to $v^T J_n^{\mathrm{prime}} v$. Hence $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N) \succeq 0$ structurally — conditional on the joint total being Stieltjes-positive on $(0, \infty)$ (which is RH/GRH).**

This is the conditional proof. T8.4's analytical job is to make this proof *less conditional* by deriving the support structure of $\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}}$ from L-function machinery (T8 multiplicative orthogonality + T9.4 Helmert-Gauss decomposition) rather than from the assumption that all zeros are on the critical line.

## 2. Target theorems

The paper should establish (in priority order):

### Theorem A (Core: Stieltjes positivity of the prime-side joint cumulant)

For every primorial $N \ge 30$ and every $n \ge 0$,
$$J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N) := (\mu_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N))_{0 \le i, j \le n} \succeq 0,$$
where $\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N) := \mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N) - \mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)$ with $\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}$ given by the explicit closed-form of `Arch-Structural-Theorems-Draft1.md` Theorem 1.

The proof should be **as unconditional as possible**, with explicit identification of what assumptions are used. Acceptable proof levels (in decreasing order of strength; aim for the strongest the analysis supports):

1. **Fully unconditional** — derived from T8 multiplicative-Fourier structure + T9.4 Helmert-Gauss decomposition + classical character-orthogonality, without any zero-location assumption.
2. **Conditional on zero-density bounds** — derived using known bounds on off-line zero density (Bombieri, Heath-Brown, etc.) at primorial scale, but not assuming GRH itself.
3. **Conditional on GRH$_N$** — fully structural via `Arch-Structural-Theorems-Draft1.md` §7's signed-measure argument; valuable as the rigorous version of the empirical observation.

Even (3) is a real result, because it makes the relationship "GRH$_N$ ⟹ Apex-J side" precise via Hankel-positivity machinery. Aim for (1) or (2) if the analysis supports them; settle for (3) if the unconditional path requires inputs beyond T8 + T9.4.

### Theorem B (Multiplicative-Fourier-orthogonality lever)

Using T8 §2.4 (multiplicative selection rule $\chi_1 \chi_2 \cdots \chi_n = \psi$) and T8 §3.1 (cyclic-factor decomposition of $\widehat{(\mathbb{Z}/N)^\times}$), express the joint prime cumulant at primorial $N$ as
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N) = \sum_{\chi \in \hat{X}_N} \mu_k^{\mathrm{prime}, \chi},$$
with per-character contributions $\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime}, \chi}$ derived from $L'(\chi)/L(\chi)$ via the bilateral apex of T8 Corollary 4.2 (the now-rigorous version with explicit $G_\times/G$ and $\varphi(N)/N$ factors).

State the per-character decomposition explicitly so that the JOINT structure (summed over $\chi \in \hat{X}_N$) admits character-orthogonality-driven cancellation.

### Theorem C (Helmert-Gauss-sum expansion of the prime side)

Apply T9.4 Corollary 5.1 (the boxed identity $\Pi_k F = R_k \varphi(N) \sum_{n: \gcd(n,N)=1} \Lambda(n) w(n) \langle h_k, \chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$) to the prime cumulants. The result is a finite explicit sum over residues coprime to $N$, with kernel the inverse-residue Helmert-Fourier coefficient.

For the shifted Hankel $J^{\mathrm{prime}}_n$ specifically, the same decomposition applies to the shifted-cumulant index $k+1$ instead of $k$. State the analog of Corollary 5.1 for the shifted case.

### Theorem D (Stieltjes-positivity reduction)

The Stieltjes-positivity question $v^T J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}} v \ge 0$ reduces (via Theorems B + C) to an inequality of the form
$$\sum_{n: \gcd(n, N) = 1} \Lambda(n) \, K_v(n, N) \ge 0$$
for an explicit kernel $K_v(n, N)$ depending on the test vector $v$ and the Helmert-Fourier coefficients of inverse residues mod $N$. The kernel $K_v(n, N)$ should be exhibited in closed form.

This is the explicit-formula structural form of the Stieltjes-positivity question. If $K_v(n, N) \ge 0$ pointwise for every test $v$, the theorem follows unconditionally. If not, identify the conditions on $v$ and $N$ under which positivity holds; this is the conditional analytical bound.

### Theorem E (Optional: extension to higher primorials)

If the proof technique at $N \in \{30, 210\}$ scales naturally to $N = 2310$ or beyond, state the result. The Helmert-Gauss machinery should scale uniformly across primorial $N$, so this should fall out — but verify the constants are not pathologically degenerate at large $N$.
