# Universal Information-Investment Modeling: Theorem Mass

Created: 2026-05-14

This is the north-star attractor for the program, not yet a theorem.  Its job
is to keep the local proof work pointed at the same target while preventing
publication language from outrunning the evidence.

## Program Attractor

Prediction and generative engines invest finite information resources into an
unknown behavior geometry.  The conjectural optimal investment rule is:

> Choose finite categorical and quotient-character discretizations that
> linearize the relevant Fisher curvature, carry, and pullback defects while
> minimizing model rank, energy, latency, and sample complexity under the task
> distribution.

In short: curvature-linearization is the discretization problem.

This is especially relevant when the true shape is not known in advance:
semantic maps, world models, behavior manifolds, or any learned space where a
smooth coordinate story is available only after the fact.  The finite arithmetic
skeleton is valuable only if it predicts where compression, closure, leakage,
and control should occur.

## Current Translation

The strongest safe public phrasing is:

> Structured categorical behavior spaces admit finite arithmetic coordinates
> for Fisher curvature and off-centroid coupling.  These coordinates provide a
> candidate discretization principle for efficient prediction engines.

The unsafe phrasing is:

> We have proved the universal optimal discretization of all generative
> engines.

## Disconfirmation Tests

1. **No compression gain:** conductor or quotient-character coordinates predict
   no lower-rank or lower-latency representation than generic bases.
2. **No leakage gain:** update projection suppresses measured leakage but does
   not preserve or improve task loss under fair controls.
3. **No pullback stability:** learned activations destroy the finite skeleton;
   behavior-side Fisher structure does not survive the network map.
4. **No family transfer:** C7/C12 and local-row results do not replicate across
   unrelated structured tasks or model families.
5. **No resource correlation:** closure deficit, reciprocal-loci rigidity, or
   carry residuals do not correlate with energy, rank, latency, or sample
   complexity.

## Metrics To Attach

- Fisher curvature linearization error.
- reciprocal-density forbidden-mode energy.
- support leakage and full filtered leakage, separately.
- closure deficit by row and arity.
- descent-balance cost: missing lower conductor/divisor levels in the
  zero-sum transfer ladder.
- pullback defect: intrinsic cubic vs activation Jacobian/Hessian terms.
- model resource proxies: parameter rank, activation rank, wall-clock latency,
  energy estimate, sample count, and validation loss.
