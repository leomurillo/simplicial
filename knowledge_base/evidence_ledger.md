This ledger tags evidence by what it can actually support. The strongest
pattern is not "everything is proven"; it is a stratified pattern:

- exact finite arithmetic is strongest for the core algebra;
- real transformer probes support behavior-side information geometry and CRT splitting;
- optimizer pilots show leakage control but not yet general-purpose optimization;
- cubic/Bures and primorial branches are downstream and must keep proof,
  trajectory audit, support certificate, and analytic conjecture separated;
- FEP claims remain bridge claims.

## Evidence Hygiene Rules

1. Do not cite asymmetry alone as arithmetic evidence; bigram control makes it mixed.
2. Do not cite C7/C12 behavior probes as training-time evidence.
3. Do not cite `K_CH` as `Ext_CH` evidence.
4. Do not cite support-level closure as filtered closure.
5. When using local safetensors, record provenance: local path, date, model, layer, prompt set, and whether external/public replication exists.
6. Do not cite months aggregate conductor energy alone as CRT-specific routing; use the null-calibrated high-conductor tilt language.
7. Do not cite the retired `Z/12Z` versus `Z/18Z` FFT proxy for Constraint 3; use the trajectory-level cubic Noether audit.
8. Do not cite quantum-lift residual audits as proof of gauge symmetry inside transformers; they are exploratory pre-measurement geometry diagnostics.
9. Quarantined or superseded artifacts are not evidence until their claims are extracted into this ledger or the claim graph.
