# Simplicial Research Knowledge Base

Created: 2026-05-14
Last updated: 2026-05-15

This folder is the skeptical control layer for the research program. Its job is
not to validate the big narrative. Its job is to make every claim traceable,
ranked, falsifiable, and publication-ready only when the local evidence level
allows it.

## Evidence Tags

- `Proof`: proved in the manuscripts, modulo normal peer-review checking.
- `Exact computation`: finite enumeration or deterministic symbolic computation, useful as proof support but still requiring proof extraction if the claim is theorem-facing.
- `Empirical`: real model, corpus, or training run evidence.
- `Both`: supported by a proof-level statement plus finite computation or empirical evidence.
- `Gap`: an open problem, conjectural bridge, or unproven interpretation.
- `Risk`: a known contradiction, confound, transfer hazard, or overclaiming pressure.

## Socratic Rule

Every node should have at least one disconfirmation question. Strong claims are
allowed, but only after their failure modes are named. The private graph may be
messy and redundant; publication artifacts should be spare, scoped, and free of
the discovery log.
