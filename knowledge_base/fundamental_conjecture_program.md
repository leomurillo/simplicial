# Fundamental Conjecture Program: Arithmetic Simplectic Mechanics

Created: 2026-05-14
Last updated: 2026-05-15

This note compresses the current bridge work into one theorem program.  It is
not a manifesto for public language.  It is the private north-star for deciding
which conjectures deserve to be upgraded into fundamental theorems.

The proposed name for the mathematical spine is:

> **Arithmetic Simplectic Mechanics**

The proposed AI-facing name is:

> **Conductor Dynamics on Categorical Simplices**

The point is not that every categorical model literally follows a clean
Nambu-Fisher equation.  The point is sharper and more testable: when a finite
algebraic output structure is present, the Fisher geometry of behavior has a
canonical arithmetic skeleton, and learning/update dynamics can be decomposed
into conductor-closed motion plus measurable carry residuals.

## Master Conjecture

**FC0. Arithmetic simplectic mechanics master conjecture.**  Let \(R\) be a
finite quotient ring or finite abelian output algebra, and let
\(\Delta_R^\circ\) be the categorical simplex with Fisher volume
\(\Omega_R^F\).  For structured behavior distributions whose tangent forcing
has stable quotient-character support, the tuple

```text
(\Delta_R^\circ, \Omega_R^F, \mathcal A_R, K^{CH}, \operatorname{Ext}_{CH})
```

is the canonical finite phase-space skeleton.  Any Fisher-small learned update
or behavior flow decomposes, after projection to the behavior simplex, into:

1. a conductor-closed Nambu-Fisher current generated by macrovariables in the
   finite conductor algebra;
2. a metric cubic residual measured by \(K^{CH}\);
3. a filtered carry residual measured by local
   \(\operatorname{Ext}_{CH}(R_{\mathfrak p})\) classes and their
   hyper-adjacency lifts.

The resource-optimal finite model is the one minimizing the
information-investment functional \(\mathcal J_\infty\): Fisher distortion plus
closure leakage plus pullback defect plus resource cost.

**Scope hypotheses.**  Structured finite label algebra; positive categorical
states; either near-centroid control or reciprocal-density support control;
smooth learned map with generic behavior rank; fixed resource proxy.

**Primary falsifier.**  Conductor and quotient-character coordinates fail to
predict compression, leakage, pullback stability, optimizer behavior, or sample
complexity better than generic Fourier/random bases under fair controls.

## The Ideal Theorem Spine

The cleanest route is not to start with AI or FEP.  It is:

1. **FC2 first:** done as a family theorem.  The cohomological source is the
   filtered adjacency extension class; the remaining work is classification of
   nonzero rows, Fisher-jet kernels, and coefficient-level higher-arity tables.
2. **FC1 second:** closed at bounded finite-jet theorem scope, with the
   `F4[u]/(u^2)` Noether atlas delivered as an exact finite certificate.
3. **FC3 third, now active:** FC3-A/B/C are launched as bounded split-ledger
   information-investment theorem candidates.  They use B1/B2/B3/B4 for the
   generated-subgroup and normalizer-premium proof spine, import the first
   `F4[u]/(u^2)` local Noether-atlas carry ledger from FC1/FC2, and extend the
   full/support/H4/H3/remaining pricing rule across the current FC2 local row
   classifier.  FC3-C adds admissible task-dependent scalar weights and the
   bounded mixed-row A0/J_infty statement while keeping full filtered carry,
   support projection, Fisher-shadow visibility, and hidden residual mass
   distinct.
4. **FC4 fourth:** show that learned maps internalize or break the same
   conductor structure through pullback.  This is where real models matter.
5. **FC5 fifth:** convert the geometry into optimizer guarantees and
   ablations.
6. **FC6 sixth:** take the thermodynamic and continuum limits.
7. **FC8 as a downstream physics branch:** keep the Bures completion theorem
   precise and bounded; do not let it imply QFT, gravity, or transformer gauge
   actions.
8. **FC9 as a downstream RH branch:** keep the primorial program conditional
   until the finite-place and Archimedean proof obligations are actually
   closed.
9. **FC7 only after variables are formalized:** treat FEP/blanket language as
   a corollary candidate, not the engine.

This order keeps the strongest exact finite arithmetic at the root, then lets
physics, AI, and thermodynamics inherit the same obstruction ledger.

## Language Boundary

Safe private thesis:

> Categorical AI may be well modeled as conductor dynamics on arithmetic
> simplectic phase spaces whenever the output behavior has stable finite
> algebraic structure.

Unsafe public thesis for now:

> AI training is already proved to be arithmetic simplectic mechanics.

Also unsafe:

> The Bures lift derives a complete Standard Model or the primorial program has
> proved RH.

The theorem program should make the first sentence precise.  The second
sentence and the downstream physics/RH variants should remain forbidden until
their explicit graph blockers close.
