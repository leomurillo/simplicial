# B0 Cyclic Balance-Transfer Descent Theorem

Created: 2026-05-14

This note promotes the cyclic part of B0 from prototype to theorem target.

## Theorem

Let \(C_N\) be the cyclic output algebra, and identify additive-character
labels with \(k\in{\mathbb Z}/N{\mathbb Z}\).  For a nonempty reciprocal
Fourier support \(S\subset \widehat{C_N}\setminus\{0\}\), define

```text
M(S) = lcm { cond(k) : k in S }.
```

The B0 balance-transfer descent closure is

```text
D_N(S) = { k != 0 : cond(k) divides M(S) }.
```

Equivalently, \(D_N(S)\cup\{0\}\) is the unique subgroup of
\(\widehat{C_N}\) generated by \(S\).

## Proof Sketch

In a cyclic group, a character label \(k\) has conductor

```text
cond(k) = N / gcd(N,k).
```

If the active conductors have least common multiple \(M\), then the subgroup
they generate is the unique subgroup of order \(M\).  Its nonzero elements are
exactly the labels whose conductors divide \(M\).  Therefore
\(D_N(S)\cup\{0\}\) is closed under character addition and is the smallest
subgroup containing \(S\).

If a positive reciprocal density \(m_p=1/p\) has Fourier support in \(S\), then
it lies in the finite function algebra with Fourier support in
\(D_N(S)\cup\{0\}\).  That algebra is closed under pointwise products and
inversion of nowhere-zero functions, so \(p\propto 1/m_p\) and \(p-p_*\) also
have Fourier support inside \(D_N(S)\cup\{0\}\).  Thus projection error,
reciprocal error, and closure leakage vanish on \(D_N(S)\).

Finally, B0's descent-balance rule requires all lower conductor/divisor levels
inside the generated subgroup.  Any candidate support with zero reciprocal
projection for generic \(S\) contains \(S\); any candidate support containing
\(S\) with zero descent-balance cost contains \(D_N(S)\).  Hence \(D_N(S)\) is
the unique minimum-resource support with zero structural B0 cost.

## A0 Reading

This is the first clean B0 theorem family.  It says that for cyclic structured
labels, optimal finite investment is not "take the largest observed modes" and
not "buy the full space."  It is:

> buy exactly the balance-transfer subgroup generated by the active
> translation/stretch spectrum.

Prime rows are controls because the generated subgroup is the full nontrivial
prime family.  Prime-power rows give nested descent ladders.  Mixed-prime rows
show how prime families combine through the lcm conductor.
