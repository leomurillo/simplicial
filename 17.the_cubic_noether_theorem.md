## Abstract

We supply the missing conservation–symmetry dictionary at third cumulant order. On the categorical simplex equipped with the Fisher volume form \(\Omega_R^F\), the cubic information action and its autonomous Noether energy are the direct higher-order analogues of the classical quadratic action and energy. Their stationary paths reproduce the Amari–Chentsov force law \(F = K_{CH}(v,v)\). Every vector field that preserves the volume, commutes with the macro-Hamiltonians, and respects the CRT-tensor and real-cone conditions then generates a cubic conserved charge via the Nambu-Fisher bracket.

The jerk \(\dddot\eta = \nabla^{(e)}T = T^{(4)}\) appears as the natural pointer to higher-order (quartic) momentum. We prove that this higher momentum cannot be consistently included at the classical level without violating real-cone preservation or CRT-tensor compatibility. With this cubic Noether theorem in hand, the cubic information-geometric closure problem is resolved: the force is cubic, the only admissible symmetries on the real simplex are the Cartan subalgebra \(\mathfrak{u}(1)^2\), the only conserved cubic charges are the two abelian ones, and the integrability locus \(\{\nabla^{(e)}T = 0\}\) does not coincide with the Cartan obstruction locus even when higher cumulants are considered. Quantization remains forced by representation theory and by variational completeness; the leakage rate of the density-matrix lift is the jerk \(\nabla^{(e)}T\).

The cubic categorical mechanics is now accounted for by a single, order-matched Noether correspondence: the force law, the integrability boundary, and the variational origin of the abelian truncation all arise from the same Fisher-volume formalism.

This final version also folds in the Constraint 3 cubic Noether audit and its structural promotion. The audit checks that the closed squarefree \(R=\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z\) cubic support remains closed at numerical floor when embedded in \(R=\mathbb Z/12\mathbb Z\) and \(R=\mathbb Z/18\mathbb Z\), rejecting the retired FFT proxy as a diagnostic for cubic Noether charge. The promoted structural lemma says that, under packet-local Fisher factorization and either hard packet preservation or first-order packet pricing, squarefree two-prime support is closed under the cubic Noether force; prime-power depth is therefore not a new independent cubic axis.

## 2. The Cubic Noether Theorem

**Definition 2.1 (Cubic simplex action and energy density).**  
Let \(\eta(t)\) be a smooth path in an \(e\)-affine chart on \(\Delta_R^\circ\), let \(v=\dot\eta\), and let \(h=\eta-\eta_*\) denote displacement from the categorical centroid. The cubic information Lagrangian density is
\[
\ell_3(\eta,v)
= \tfrac12 g_\eta(v,v) + \tfrac13 T_\eta(h,v,v),
\]
where \(g\) is the Fisher metric and \(T=K_{CH}\) is the Amari–Chentsov cubic tensor. The corresponding cubic information action is
\[
\mathcal A_3[\eta]
= \int_0^T \ell_3(\eta,\dot\eta)\,dt.
\]
Because \(\ell_3\) is autonomous, time translation gives an instantaneous Noether energy
\[
\mathcal E_3
= \dot\eta\cdot \frac{\partial \ell_3}{\partial \dot\eta}-\ell_3
= \tfrac12 g_\eta(\dot\eta,\dot\eta)
 + \tfrac13 T_\eta(h,\dot\eta,\dot\eta),
\]
up to the standard Legendre-boundary convention. Thus the integral \(\mathcal A_3\) is the action accumulated along a path, while \(\mathcal E_3\) is the conserved autonomous energy density. Both belong to the cubic truncation: the Fisher term is the quadratic kinetic core, and \(T_\eta(h,\dot\eta,\dot\eta)\) is the first cubic correction.

**Remark 2.1A (Autonomous time and effective-action truncation).**  
The parameter \(t\) is the autonomous flow parameter, not an additional
coordinate on the simplex.  In the effective-action dictionary, the
quadratic Fisher term is the second-cumulant ruler and inertia/mass matrix,
while the cubic term is the third-cumulant Amari-Chentsov source term.  The
Noether energy \(\mathcal E_3\) is therefore the charge of time translation

\(\nabla^{(e)}T\) remains visible, it is recorded here as jerk or projection
residual, not as a new classical force law.

**Theorem 2.2 (Cubic Action-Energy Theorem).**  
Time-translation invariance of the autonomous cubic information action \(\mathcal A_3\) yields conservation of \(\mathcal E_3\). Its first variation, restricted to \(e\)-geodesic variations with fixed endpoints and truncated at third cumulant order, reproduces the cubic force law:
\[
F_i=\ddot\eta_i=K_{CH}(v,v)_i=T_{ijk}(v)v^jv^k.
\]
The next \(e\)-covariant derivative of the cubic tensor is the jerk \(\nabla^{(e)}T=T^{(4)}\), so the variational calculation also identifies exactly where quartic momentum would have to enter.

*Proof (variational source of the force law).*  
For a one-parameter family \(\eta_\epsilon(t)\) with fixed endpoints, the first variation of \(\mathcal A_3\) is
\[
\delta\mathcal A_3
= \int_0^T
\left(
g(\dot\eta,\delta\dot\eta)
+ T(h,\dot\eta,\delta\dot\eta)
+ \tfrac13 \nabla^{(e)}_{\delta\eta}T(h,\dot\eta,\dot\eta)
+ \tfrac13 T(\delta\eta,\dot\eta,\dot\eta)
\right)dt.
\]
Integration by parts on the velocity variations gives the Euler-Lagrange equation
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(g(\dot\eta)+T(h,\dot\eta)\bigr)
= \tfrac13 \nabla^{(e)}T(h,\dot\eta,\dot\eta)+\tfrac13 T(\,\cdot\,,\dot\eta,\dot\eta).
\]
In \(e\)-affine coordinates, at cubic truncation, the surviving third-order term is precisely the Amari-Chentsov force \(K_{CH}(v,v)\). The displayed \(\nabla^{(e)}T\) term is one cumulant order higher; it is the jerk treated in Section 3 as the candidate quartic momentum. Since \(\ell_3\) is autonomous, the usual Noether calculation gives \(d\mathcal E_3/dt=0\) along stationary paths. \(\blacksquare\)

## 3. Higher-Order Momentum Attempt

**Theorem 3.1.** The integrability locus \(\{\nabla^{(e)}T = 0\}\) and the Cartan obstruction locus do **not** coincide, even when the jerk \(\dddot\eta = T^{(4)}\) is treated as a pointer to quartic momentum.

*Proof.*  
- Every nontrivial conductor packet carries nonzero fourth cumulant. Restricting to Cartan-invariant subspaces does not truncate the log-partition to cubic order, nor does it make \(\nabla^{(e)}T \equiv 0\).
- The Cartan fixed locus on \(\Delta_R^\circ\) is essentially the centroid. At the centroid the geometry is purely quadratic; no nontrivial cubic- or quartic-truncated family exists inside the positive cone.
- Any attempt to include a quartic conserved charge built from \(T^{(4)}\) as an additional macro-Hamiltonian requires generators that mix conductor packets nontrivially. Such generators violate real-cone preservation or CRT-tensor compatibility (Paper 14, Theorem 2.2′). The cubic Noether theorem already exhausts the admissible conserved quantities under the classical constraints; higher-order extensions are representation-theoretically forbidden.
- Therefore the intersection of the two loci remains trivial even at quartic order. Polynomial integrability and nonabelian (or higher-cumulant) gauge are independent variational constraints. \(\blacksquare\)

**Remark 3.2 (The Jerk as Pointer to Higher-Order Momentum).**  
The jerk \(\dddot\eta = \nabla^{(e)}T\) naturally suggests that a quartic conserved charge could close the classical system. This intuition is correct in principle — in an unconstrained higher-order mechanics the jerk would correspond to an additional macrovariable. However, on the categorical simplex the only symmetries compatible with positivity, centroid-fixing, and CRT structure are the abelian Cartan ones. Including the jerk as an input macrovariable would demand nonabelian root-space mixing, which is forbidden. Thus the jerk does not open a classical loophole; it confirms that exact prediction is impossible on the real simplex and points toward the quantum lift.

This negative resolution is consistent with the finite-atlas evidence of Papers 10-15. No further computation is required for the formal Cartan-exhaustion argument; Section 5 promotes the Constraint 3 audit to a structural closure lemma showing that prime-power depth does not create a new independent cubic axis under packet-local and packet-respect hypotheses.
