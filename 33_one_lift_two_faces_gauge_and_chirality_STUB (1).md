## 3. The central obstruction: the Chirality Boundary

The one place the internal program *cannot* reach, by its own rules:

> The Fisher/Bures completion is real-cone-preserving and CRT-compatible by
> construction (Paper 14). Real-cone preservation forbids the sign flip that
> distinguishes a commuting KO real-structure sign from the anticommuting
> chirality \(J_F\gamma_F=-\gamma_F J_F\) required by finite Standard-Model
> Dirac operators. Therefore the relative \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) of chirality is not
> in the image of any internal real-cone-preserving lift.

**Chirality Boundary Theorem (target, [C]).** This \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) is the
unique datum that must be supplied externally, and the only external structure
the program admits is the Lorentzian signature of Paper 32 §9. Hence the gauge
lift (internal) and the signature lift (external, Paper 32 Conjecture 9.4) are
coupled by exactly one bit, locked to the spacetime chirality operator
\(\gamma^5\) through the Connes product triple. Falsifier: if the
\(\mathbb{Z}_2\) *can* be produced by a real-cone-preserving internal
construction, the boundary theorem is false — but *that* would itself be a
major result (internal chirality), pre-claimed here so it is not later
presented as unseen.

**Sharpening from the ℂ²-leg real-form *algebraic* identity (Paper 32 ledger
item S′ — algebraic content; geometric realization refuted by §13.2).** Two
distinct content layers, decided differently by the §13.2 audit.

*Algebraic layer (preserved).* On the \(\mathbb C^2\) leg, compact
\(\mathfrak{su}(2)\) and non-compact \(\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)\) are two
real forms of one complexification \(\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb C)_{\mathbb C}\)
— a Lie-algebra fact. The real-form choice has exactly one residual
\(\mathbb Z_2\) (the sign distinguishing compact from non-compact in the
appropriate parameter) that is not determined by the choice of complex Lie
algebra alone. Identifying that residual sign with the KO real-structure
\(\mathbb Z_2\) of \(\gamma^5\) gives a sharper reading of the irreducible-
external bit: chirality is **the one sign the real-form choice does not
fix**, locked to \(\gamma^5\) by construction. This algebraic sharpening
*tightens* Q4 — what was "all of chirality is external" becomes "the one
sign of the real-form choice is external."

*Geometric layer (refuted).* The further claim that *Paper 14 Theorem 2.2′
plus the Bures lift realizes the compact→non-compact passage geometrically
on the lifted block* — i.e. that the lift instantiates the non-compact real
form \(\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C)\) and that gauge and signature collapse into
one geometric face — has been refuted by the Paper 32 §13.2 audit. Paper 14
Theorem 2.2′ selects the *compact* real form on the cone (the gauge face);
no non-compact real form is realized geometrically on the lifted conductor-2
⊕ conductor-3 block. Q4's sharpening to "one sign" remains *algebraically*
sound; the geometric "shared leg" reading falls. Q4's [C] tier and falsifier
are otherwise unchanged.

**Orientation bookkeeping splits — time-orientation is internal, chirality
is external (Paper 32 §9.6 Corollary 9.9 / item W′).** The program carries
*two* distinct orientation data, and they have **opposite status**:
**time-orientation (the arrow of time) is internal** — it is the
diagonal projection channel's monotonicity (Petz monotonicity / DPI /
H-theorem of Paper 32 Corollary 9.9), an in-program structural feature
of the same SLD/Bures metric whose Nambu–Bures flow is unitary
upstairs (Paper 18 §4). **Chirality \(\mathbb Z_2\) (the sign locked to
\(\gamma^5\)) is external** — the one sign the real-form choice does
not fix, supplied by the external Lorentzian datum per the Chirality
Boundary Theorem above and named in Paper 32 §9.5 Corollary 9.8 / V′
as **the** single irreducibly-external bit. One orientation generated by
projection monotonicity, the other irreducibly externally supplied —
the program's two orientation data have opposite epistemic status, and
saying so sharpens Q4's framing without changing its tier or falsifier.
