## Abstract

We explore the interpretation of the jerk term \(\dddot\eta = \nabla^{(e)}T = T^{(4)}\) that emerges from the cumulant tower of the categorical exponential family. Building on the cubic force law and the cubic action-energy theorem (Paper 17), we propose that the jerk functions as an effective **simplex mass term** for conductor modes. More sharply, it is the order-4 projection-closure residual left when time-translation Noether energy bookkeeping is pushed through the diagonal/simplex projection. Its role in the quantum-to-classical projection channel is formally identical to an imaginary part of energy (decay width) or a state-dependent mass gap. We distinguish the accumulated cubic action \(\mathcal A_3\) from the instantaneous Noether energy density \(\mathcal E_3\), then show that the projected curvature/defect of \(\mathcal E_3\) is precisely the jerk. While the analogy is suggestive and internally consistent, it remains purely information-geometric: there is no spacetime, no negative kinetic term, and the leakage is positive semidefinite, hence bounded below by zero inside the finite-jet geometry.

This note does not claim gravity, quantum field theory, or a physical mass term. It offers a clean conceptual bridge between the cubic mechanics of the categorical simplex and familiar notions of mass and complex energy.

## 4. Limitations and Scope

- There is **no spacetime** and no Lorentz structure; the “mass” is purely statistical.
- The simplex mass is **state-dependent** and vanishes at maximum entropy.
- There is **no negative kinetic term** or ghost field; "non-tachyonic" here only means that the projection-closure residual is positive semidefinite and bounded below by zero in the Fisher/cumulant geometry.
- The analogy breaks at higher orders: the fifth and higher cumulants do not fit neatly into a simple mass-term picture.

This exploration therefore remains internal to the information-geometric program. It does not claim a physical mass term, gravity, or a quantum field theory.
