# Note: Prime Cyclic Rows — Correct Statement and Proof

**Status:** Replaces and corrects the brainstorm claim
"[K^{CH}(χ_j), K^{CH}(χ_k)] = 0 at the centroid for prime cyclic groups."
That claim is false.  The correct theorem is Theorem 3 below.

## 1. The Commutator Is Not Zero — and Is the Same for All N

**Proposition 1.1 (K^{CH} commutator is sphere curvature, universal).**
Let R = C_N for any N ≥ 2.  At the uniform centroid p_* = N^{-1}\mathbf{1}, for
u, v, w \in H_{C_N} (the zero-sum tangent space),

$$
[K^{CH}_{p_*}(u),\, K^{CH}_{p_*}(v)](w)
\;=\;
N\bigl(\langle u,w\rangle_{\mathrm{Euc}}\,v
      - \langle v,w\rangle_{\mathrm{Euc}}\,u\bigr).
$$

This is the Levi-Civita curvature of the round (N-1)-sphere, rescaled by N.
It is nonzero for linearly independent u, v, w, and it is **identical for all N**,
prime or composite.

**Proof.**  The explicit formula for K^{CH}_{p_*} in position coordinates is
(Paper 11 §8)

$$
K^{CH}_{p_*}(u)v
\;=\;
N(u \odot v)
- N\!\left(\sum_x u_x v_x\right)\mathbf{1},
$$

where \odot denotes pointwise multiplication and the second term projects the
result to H.  Compute the commutator directly:

$$
K^{CH}(u)(K^{CH}(v)w)
= N\bigl(u \odot (N(v\odot w) - \textstyle\sum_x v_x w_x \cdot\mathbf{1})\bigr)
  - \textstyle N\!\bigl(\sum_x u_x[K^{CH}(v)w]_x\bigr)\mathbf{1}.
$$

Expanding and using \sum_x u_x = 0 (since u \in H):

$$
K^{CH}(u)(K^{CH}(v)w)
= N^2(u\odot v\odot w)
  - N\!\left(\textstyle\sum_x v_x w_x\right) u
  - N\!\left(\textstyle\sum_x u_x v_x w_x\right)\mathbf{1}.
$$

Subtracting the expression with u and v swapped, the symmetric terms
N^2(u\odot v\odot w) and N(\sum u_x v_x w_x)\mathbf{1} cancel (pointwise
multiplication is commutative).  The residue is

$$
[K^{CH}(u), K^{CH}(v)](w)
= N\!\left(\textstyle\sum_x u_x w_x\right) v
- N\!\left(\textstyle\sum_x v_x w_x\right) u.
$$

Writing \langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_{\mathrm{Euc}} for the standard Euclidean
inner product on \mathbb{R}^N, this is
N(\langle u,w\rangle_{\mathrm{Euc}}\, v - \langle v,w\rangle_{\mathrm{Euc}}\, u). \square

**Remark 1.2.**  The formula depends only on N and the Euclidean structure of
\mathbb{R}^N.  The arithmetic of the label group (prime, composite, local ring)
does not appear.  Therefore:

> The prime/composite distinction does **not** appear in the K^{CH}
> operator commutator at the centroid.

The brainstorm argument "convolution in C_p is commutative so the commutator
vanishes" is wrong because K^{CH}_{p_*}(u) is not simply the multiplication-by-ûoperator on H.  The correction term -N(\sum_x u_x v_x)\mathbf{1} breaks the
convolution algebra commutativity at the operator level.

## 2. Conductor Structure of C_N

**Proposition 2.1 (Conductor formula for cyclic groups).**
The conductor of the additive character χ_k in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} is

$$
\mathrm{cond}(\chi_k) = \frac{N}{\gcd(N,k)}.
$$

**Proof.**  χ_k is trivial on the subgroup d\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z} iff N | kd
iff N/\gcd(N,k) | d.  The minimal such d is N/\gcd(N,k). \square

**Corollary 2.2 (Prime implies single conductor).**
For N = p (prime), \gcd(p, k) = 1 for all k \in \{1,\ldots,p-1\}.  Therefore
every nontrivial additive character has conductor p.  The nontrivial tangent
space H_{C_p} is a **single conductor packet**:

$$
H_{C_p} = X_p, \qquad X_p = \{\chi_1, \ldots, \chi_{p-1}\}.
$$

For composite N, multiple conductor values appear: e.g., C_{12} has conductor
packets at conductors 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.

**Verified:**

| N  | Conductor packets |
|----|------------------|
| 7  | {7: [1,2,3,4,5,6]} — **one packet** |
| 12 | {2:[6], 3:[4,8], 4:[3,9], 6:[2,10], 12:[1,5,7,11]} — five packets |
| 4  | {2:[2], 4:[1,3]} — two packets |
| 9  | {3:[3,6], 9:[1,2,4,5,7,8]} — two packets (prime square, not prime) |

The single-packet property is strictly prime: even C_{p^2} for a prime p has
two conductor levels.

## 3. The Correct Theorem: Trivial Ext_{CH} for Prime Cyclic

**Theorem 3.1 (H7A, corrected).**
For R = C_p with p prime:

1. **(Single packet.)** H_{C_p} = X_p is the unique nontrivial conductor
   packet.  There are no cross-conductor quotient-shift operators in
   \mathcal{A}_{C_p}.

2. **(Commutative within-packet algebra.)** The adjacency algebra
   \mathcal{A}_{C_p} is generated by the within-packet shifts
   A_\eta^{p,p} = P_p M_\eta P_p (\eta \in X_p \cup \{0\}).  Since
   A_\eta^{p,p} \circ A_\zeta^{p,p} = A_{\eta+\zeta}^{p,p} and the character
   group is abelian, the algebra is **commutative**.

3. **(Trivial filtration.)** \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z} is a field: its only ideals
   are (0) and the full ring.  The conductor filtration has a single nontrivial
   level X_p.  The tame/wild grading of the local factor \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}
   has trivial wild part (a field has no nilpotent elements, hence no
   principal-unit depth).  Therefore the associated graded of
   \mathcal{A}_{C_p} equals \mathcal{A}_{C_p} itself — the filtration
   splits canonically.

4. **(Ext_{CH} = 0.)** By point 3, the local Carlitz-Hayes extension class
   \operatorname{Ext}_{CH}(C_p) = 0.

5. **(Irreducibility.)** X_p = \{1,\ldots,p-1\} is an irreducible module for
   the Hayes normalizer \Gamma_{C_p} = (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^\times \cong
   \mathbb{Z}/(p-1)\mathbb{Z}.  The action u \cdot \chi_k = \chi_{uk \bmod p}
   is transitive on X_p (the multiplicative group of a field acts transitively
   on nonzero elements).  No proper submodule of X_p is
   \Gamma_{C_p}-invariant.

6. **(No conductor-path holonomy.)** Because there is a single conductor level,
   every path in the adjacency path-groupoid between characters in X_p passes
   through X_p.  There is no choice of intermediate conductor; path-dependence
   is trivial.  This is the **algebraic** holonomy-free statement.  It is
   distinct from, and consistent with, the nonzero Riemannian holonomy of
   Proposition 1.1.

**Proof summary.**  Points 1–4 follow immediately from the ideal structure of a
prime field: no proper nonzero ideals \Rightarrow no intermediate conductor
\Rightarrow single packet \Rightarrow trivial filtration \Rightarrow Ext = 0.
Point 5 is the standard fact that (\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})^\times acts
regularly on \{1,\ldots,p-1\}. Point 6 is a restatement of point 1 in
path-algebra language. \square

## 4. Corollaries for Fisher Geometry

**Corollary 4.1 (Trace sees only the zero quotient).**
For R = C_p, the within-packet operator C_n^{[p,p]}(h) satisfies

$$
\operatorname{tr} C_n^{[p,p]}(h)
=
(p-1)\hat h^{*n}(0).
$$

In particular, the first-order trace vanishes because h \in H implies
\hat h(0)=0, but higher-order traces need not vanish.

Proof.  Tr(A_\eta^{p,p}) = |\{\chi_k \in X_p : \eta + k \equiv k \pmod p\}|.
This fixed-point set is all of X_p when \eta=0 and empty when \eta\neq0.
Therefore only the A_0^{p,p}=I_{X_p} coefficient contributes to the trace.
\square

**Remark 4.2.**  Trace compression of C_n^{[p,p]}(h) is only the zero-quotient
coefficient.  The full operator-valued coefficient carries the Fisher coupling
information between distinct character modes.  In the prime row there is no
finer conductor sub-structure to lose, but scalar trace compression can still
discard off-diagonal cyclic shift data.

**Corollary 4.3 (No within-block invariant lost).**
For R = C_p, the operator C_n^{[p,p]}(h) is completely determined by its
matrix entries

$$
[C_n^{[p,p]}(h)]_{\ell,m} = \hat h^{*n}(m - \ell),
\quad \ell \neq m,\ \ell,m \in \{1,\ldots,p-1\},
$$

(with common diagonal \(\hat h^{*n}(0)\)).  These entries recover every
line-level Fisher coupling.
There is no finer conductor sub-structure within X_p that a coarser compression
could erase, because X_p is irreducible (Theorem 3.1, point 5).

Contrast: in the nilpotent row \mathbb{F}_2[u]/(u^2) (Paper 11 §3.2), the
conductor-u^2 packet has a 2\times 2 block structure, and the scalar trace of the
second-order coefficient misses the swap operator 2bc \cdot X_N.  This loss is
possible precisely because the block is **reducible** — it splits into two
lines under the swap symmetry, and the trace averages them.  No such split
exists for X_p.

## 6. What the Prime Row Controls

The C_p weekday/month row is the **Ext_{CH} = 0 control** in the Paper 12
experimental program, not a "holonomy-free" control in the Fisher-Rao
Riemannian sense (the Riemannian holonomy of the sphere is the same for all N).

> When the output algebra has a single irreducible conductor packet and
> trivial Ext_{CH}, does the measured correlation structure match the
> behavior-manifold prediction with no conductor-filtration residual?

positives at the prime row.  The residual from r = 0.999 (Wurgaft behavior-manifold
reference) for the weekday C_7 task should be attributable entirely to
activation pullback, Hessian of F, and sampling error — not to any
within-conductor Fisher-adjacency structure that the prime row by construction
does not have.
