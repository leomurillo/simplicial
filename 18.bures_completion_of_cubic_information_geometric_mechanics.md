## Abstract

We show that the quantum completion of cubic information-geometric mechanics on
the categorical simplex is rigid, and we identify the mechanism that forces it.
The real-simplex theory closes only in its abelian Cartan sector u(1)^2; the
Bures lift of ℂ[ℤ/6] ≅ ℂ²⊗ℂ³ to 𝒟(ℂ²⊗ℂ³) recovers su(2)⊕su(3) with a surplus
central u(1). We prove (relative to §4) that this gauge content is forced by
Petz–Bures rigidity: the SLD/Bures metric singled out in §4 is the unique
monotone metric whose conservative Nambu–Bures flow is von Neumann evolution,
its unitary invariance together with the CRT leg dimensions (2,3) forces the
per-leg isometry algebras su(2) and su(3), and the surplus u(1) is the
inter-leg normalization phase. Hurwitz's theorem is not used to generate this
content; it enters only as the terminator — there is no norm-closed fifth rung,
which is why the tower does not extend. We identify the Helmert binary-balanced
contrast cascade with the Cayley–Dickson construction under norm-closure
(dictionary, not new theorem) and withdraw the earlier conjecture that
closed-current positivity selects norm-closure, which conflated unitary
invariance with norm-multiplicativity. The completion is therefore structurally
forced, contingent on a single finite check — SLD-uniqueness within the
Petz–Sudár family — and the only remaining open problem is whether the joint
monotone metric on the tensor product is likewise the minimal member.

## 1. The Classical Cubic Obstruction

The categorical simplex \(\Delta_R^\circ\) carries the Fisher metric and
Fisher volume form \(\Omega_R^F\). For the minimal mixed row
\[
R=\mathbb Z/6\mathbb Z
\cong
\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/3\mathbb Z,
\]
the CRT factorization gives the tensor skeleton
\[
\mathbb C[R]\cong \mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^3.
\]

The cubic force law is the raised Amari-Chentsov response
\[
\ddot\eta=K^{CH}(v,v).
\]
Its Noether theory is cubic: the accumulated action and instantaneous energy
density are governed by the third-cumulant correction
\[
{1\over3}T_\eta(h,v,v).
\]
The corresponding cubic charges are attached to admissible Fisher-volume
symmetry fields.

The obstruction is that the admissible continuous symmetry algebra on the real
simplex is only
\[
\mathfrak u(1)^2.
\]
The nonabelian root directions of
\[
\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)
\]
leave the real positive-cone/CRT-compatible class. Therefore the classical
real-simplex theory is variationally complete only inside its abelian Cartan
sector.

## 2. The Jerk as Projected Cumulant Defect

The next derivative
\[
\nabla^{(e)}T=T^{(4)}
\]
is the first fourth-cumulant correction. It indicates where higher momentum
would enter an unconstrained higher-order mechanics. On the categorical
simplex, however, including it as a new conserved macrovariable requires the
same nonabelian packet mixing ruled out by the Cartan obstruction.

Thus the jerk is not a missing classical completion term. It is a projected
classical cumulant-defect obstruction: it appears when nonabelian density-matrix
motion is forced back through a commuting real-simplex measurement channel.

## 4. Nambu-Bures Reduction to Unitary Flow

The Bures volume form \(\Omega^B\) defines a Nambu bracket on
\(\mathcal D(\mathcal H)\). For a traceless Hermitian observable \(H\), the
unitary vector field is
\[
Y_H(\rho)=-i[H,\rho].
\]
Because the Bures geometry is unitarily invariant, \(Y_H\) is
volume-preserving. On a regular tube, choose transverse functions
\[
C_1(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}(\rho H),\qquad C_2,\ldots,C_{34}
\]
with
\[
dC_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dC_{34}
=
\iota_{Y_H}\Omega^B.
\]
The corresponding Nambu-Bures vector field is exactly
\[
\dot\rho=-i[H,\rho].
\]

Conservative dynamics upstairs is therefore ordinary unitary von Neumann flow.
The classical cumulant-defect obstruction appears only after projection back
to the diagonal commuting sector.

## 5. Completion Statement

The Bures lift completes the cubic information-geometric mechanics in the
following precise sense:

1. the diagonal submanifold recovers the Fisher simplex exactly;
2. the classical Cartan algebra \(\mathfrak u(1)^2\) embeds as the
   two-dimensional Cartan floor of the local unitary algebra;
3. the missing nonabelian directions become legal unitary coherences;
4. the conservative Nambu-Bures flow reduces to von Neumann evolution;
5. the surplus central \(\mathfrak u(1)\) is the inter-leg normalization
   phase;
6. the projected jerk is a measurement-channel defect, not a fundamental
   obstruction upstairs.

**Proposition 5.1 (Result D: inter-leg normalization phase) [Certain].**  
Let the Bures lift be taken on
\(\mathcal D(\mathbb C^2\otimes\mathbb C^3)\), with local unitary algebra
\(\mathfrak u(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(3)\) acting by commutator and with the common
global scalar phase quotiented because it acts trivially on density matrices.
Then the surviving central summand in
\[
\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)
\]
is the relative-normalization, equivalently global trace, phase between the
two CRT legs. It is central, leakage-flat, and normalization-invisible exactly
because it is the trace mode. The proof obligation is only the finite Lie
algebra bookkeeping
\[
(\mathfrak u(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(3))/\mathfrak u(1)_{\mathrm{diag}}
\simeq
\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak u(1),
\]
together with the fact that scalar commutators vanish.

The word "floor" is important.  The effective Bures algebra
\(\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)\) has rank \(4\),
with semisimple rank \(3\), so the classical \(\mathfrak u(1)^2\) sector is
not being identified with the full maximal torus of the completed algebra.  The
extra central direction is precisely the trace-normalization mode identified
in Proposition 5.1.

## Conclusion

The real-simplex cubic theory and the Bures density-matrix theory are not two
unrelated descriptions. The first is the diagonal, commuting, positive-cone
shadow of the second. The shadow closes only in its Cartan sector; the full
local nonabelian algebra, together with the relative central trace phase,
appears only after the phase space is enlarged to Hermitian density matrices.

The completion is therefore structural: the nonabelian directions are not
postulated as extra classical charges, but recovered as Bures-unitary
coherences compatible with positivity, trace, and the CRT tensor product.
Its necessity is Petz-Bures rigidity; Hurwitz only marks the terminus of the
compatible norm-closed real ladder.
