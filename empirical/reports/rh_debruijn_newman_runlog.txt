de Bruijn-Newman: the heat-flow margin, the Lehmer-pair collision clock, and the 0.22 gap.

PURPOSE.  Make the de Bruijn-Newman picture QUANTITATIVE and label every claim [P]/[A]/[C].
The de Bruijn-Newman constant Lambda satisfies:
   H_t real-rooted  <=>  t >= Lambda,        RH  <=>  Lambda <= 0,
where H_t(z) = int_0^inf e^{t u^2} Phi(u) cos(z u) du is the heat-flow deformation of the Riemann
xi function (H_0 = (1/8) Xi(z/2) up to normalization; its real zeros are the ordinates gamma_j).
PROVEN bracket: Lambda >= 0 (Rodgers-Tao 2020) and Lambda <= 0.22 (Polymath15, 2019), so
   Lambda in [0, 0.22]   and   RH  <=>  Lambda = 0.

This script computes FOUR things, all numerical (mpmath/numpy), none a proof of Lambda<=0:

  PART A  THE ZERO FLOW (Calogero / Rodgers-Tao dynamics).  The zeros x_j(t) of H_t obey the
          first-order interacting ODE (Csordas-Smith-Varga 1994; Rodgers-Tao 2020 eq. for the
          backward-heat-in-z DBN convention)
                d x_j / dt  =  + 2 sum_{k != j} 1/(x_j - x_k).            (FLOW)
          As t INCREASES the zeros REPEL (stay real); as t DECREASES toward Lambda they ATTRACT and
          a "Lehmer pair" can COLLIDE (real double root) then split into a complex conjugate pair --
          this collision is the ONLY way reality is lost.  We seed x_j(0) = gamma_j (the true first
          ~50 zeros) and integrate the flow BACKWARD (t decreasing) to watch the tightest pair's
          normalized gap shrink, reporting WHERE the margin is thinnest and HOW it scales.

  PART B  THE LEHMER-PAIR COLLISION CLOCK (the rigorous lower-bound mechanism, in miniature).  For
          an ISOLATED close pair the two-body reduction of (FLOW) gives an EXACT collision law:
          the squared separation s = (x_2-x_1)^2 obeys ds/dt = +8, so s(t) = s(0) + 8 t and the pair
          collides at  t_coll = - s(0)/8 = -(gap)^2/8.  A tight pair (small gap) collides at a t_coll
          just below 0 -> forces Lambda >= t_coll, i.e. Lambda is pushed UP toward 0 from below.  This
          is the Csordas-Smith-Varga "Lehmer pair" lower bound in its cleanest form; we evaluate it on
          the live tightest pair and on the historical Lehmer pair near gamma ~ 7005.

  PART C  DIRECT HEAT FLOW ON A TRUNCATED Xi (cross-check of repulsion).  Build a degree-2M real
          even polynomial whose roots are +/- the first M ordinates, evolve it under the genuine
          backward heat equation p_t = -p_xx (the DBN sign) by the exact moment/derivative recursion,
          and confirm the roots stay real and SPREAD for t>0 while the tightest pair CONTRACTS for
          t<0 -- a finite, exact, self-contained witness of (FLOW) independent of PART A's ODE.

  PART D  WHAT CONTROLS 0.22 (the upper-bound anatomy) and the honest residual.  Quantify why the
          0.22 is set by the DYNAMICS/triangle-of-influence constant, NOT by the numerical height X,
          and state precisely what closing 0.22 -> 0 requires (= RH).

HONESTY.  PART A-C seed at t=0 with the TRUE zeros (so reality at t=0 is RH-true by construction, not
proved); they exhibit the mechanism and the scaling, and PART B's collision law is the exact engine of
the proven LOWER bounds.  Nothing here proves Lambda <= 0.  Conservation of difficulty: the only
unconditional inequality the flow yields by itself is Lambda >= (a negative number) -> 0 (the lower
side); the upper side is RH.

================  PART D : what controls the 0.22, and the residual (honest)  ===============
  POLYMATH15 (2019) upper bound Lambda <= 0.22 -- ANATOMY:
    (i)  [analytic] For t>0 fixed, H_t/(its leading approx) is controlled by a Dirichlet-series
         approximation  H_t(x+iy) ~ B_t * (f_t(x+iy) + g_t(x+iy)) + error, where
            f_t(s) = sum_{n<=N} b_n^{-t...}/n^{s'}   (a t-mollified partial Dirichlet sum) and
            g_t is its functional-equation conjugate;  |error| is bounded EXPLICITLY (e_A,e_B,e_C).
    (ii) [barrier] On a rectangle [X, X+H] x [eps, 1] one shows |f_t+g_t| > (the error bound)
         via a winding-number / Rouche 'barrier': no zero of H_t can have Im>0 there, so all
         zeros in the box are real.  The barrier is verified by RIGOROUS interval arithmetic.
    (iii)[dynamics] The Newton/heat-flow zero dynamics (FLOW) PROPAGATE reality from a single
         seed t_0>0 DOWN to t-just-above-Lambda: if zeros are real at t_0 up to height X and the
         barrier holds, the flow forbids a complex pair from forming, bounding Lambda.
    NUMBERS: Polymath15 ran the barrier at height X ~ 6 x 10^10 and seed t_0 chosen so the
    combination yields Lambda <= 0.22 (the writeup's final constant; an intermediate 0.48 then
    0.22 after optimization).  The DOMINANT term in 0.22 is the DYNAMICS bound (how fast the
    flow can be guaranteed not to create a complex pair as t descends), NOT the height X.

  WHY 0.22 IS DYNAMICS-LIMITED, NOT HEIGHT-LIMITED [A]:
    Raising X only certifies reality of MORE zeros at the seed t_0; it does NOT shrink the t-
    interval (0, t_0] over which the flow must be CONTROLLED.  The 0.22 is essentially t_0 minus
    a provable downward propagation margin; that margin is set by a uniform-in-height repulsion
    LOWER bound (a triangle-of-influence / heat-kernel constant), which does not improve by
    computing higher.  So even X -> infinity leaves a POSITIVE residual unless the dynamics
    constant is itself driven to give propagation all the way to t=0.

  RODGERS-TAO (2020) lower bound Lambda >= 0 -- MECHANISM (the opposite inequality):
    Suppose Lambda < 0.  Run the flow FORWARD from t=Lambda(<0) to t=0.  Backward-heat over a
    NEGATIVE time-interval is SMOOTHING in reverse: it would force the t=0 zeros to be ANOMALOUSLY
    REGULAR -- their gap/pair-correlation statistics would be MORE rigid (closer to a perfect
    lattice / lower-energy Calogero state) than the actual zeros are.  But the actual zeros have
    GUE-type fluctuations of the SIZE that the explicit formula + the pair-correlation of zeros
    pins from below (a quantitative LOWER bound on the energy/variance of the gas).  Contradiction:
    Lambda < 0 over-regularizes; the real zeros fluctuate too much.  Hence Lambda >= 0.  [P, cited]
    (Mechanically: a Lehmer-tight pair at t=0 -- which provably EXISTS in the required averaged
    sense -- cannot have been produced by a flow that started real at some t<0, because (FLOW)
    can only SPREAD pairs going forward; so the seed cannot be below 0.)

  THE RESIDUAL (precise):
    To push Lambda <= 0.22  to  Lambda <= 0 one needs a UNIFORM-IN-HEIGHT statement that the flow
    (FLOW) creates NO complex pair for ALL t in (0, 0.22] at EVERY height -- i.e. an individual
    (not merely statistical) repulsion lower bound holding down to t=0.  Equivalently: the phase
    phi_t(x) = -arg H_t(x) has phi'_t(x) >= 0 maintained uniformly as t \downarrow 0.  The current
    obstruction is QUANTITATIVE: the proven downward-propagation margin from a seed t_0 is
    O(1) (~0.22), bounded by the dynamics constant; there is no known mechanism that converts the
    averaged repulsion (GUE / pair-correlation, which gives Lambda>=0) into the per-pair, per-
    height guarantee that would give Lambda<=0.  Lambda<=0 IS RH; conservation of difficulty says
    this last step cannot be cheaper than RH itself.
