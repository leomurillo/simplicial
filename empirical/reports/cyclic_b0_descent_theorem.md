# Cyclic B0 Balance-Transfer Descent Theorem

## Theorem

For C_N and nonempty reciprocal Fourier support S, the B0 balance-transfer closure D_N(S) is the nonzero part of the unique subgroup generated by S, equivalently the modes whose conductors divide lcm(cond(S)).

## Minimality

Any support with zero reciprocal projection for generic S and zero descent-balance cost must contain D_N(S).  D_N(S) has zero projection, closure, and balance costs, so it is the unique minimum-resource zero-structural-cost support.
