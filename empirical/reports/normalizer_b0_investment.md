# B3 Normalizer-Saturated Information Investment

## Theorem

For a finite character group G with normalizer action Gamma, the B3 symmetry-complete support of reciprocal support S is <Gamma.S>\{0}.  It is the unique minimum-resource support that is additively closed, contains S, and is Gamma-invariant.

## Reading

B2 is minimal prediction investment.  B3 is symmetry-complete investment.  The difference is a measurable symmetry premium.
