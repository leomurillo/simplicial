# Finite Abelian B0 Subgroup Descent Theorem

## Theorem

For a finite abelian character group G and nonempty reciprocal support S, the B0 balance-transfer descent closure is D_G(S)=<S>\{0}.  It is the unique minimum-resource support with zero projection, closure, and descent-balance costs.

## Caveat

In noncyclic groups, lcm/order data is insufficient.  The subgroup generated by S is the invariant; conductor or order saturation is a stronger symmetry policy and can overbuy.

## Reading

B1's cyclic lcm formula survives as a special case because cyclic groups
have one subgroup for each divisor.  B2 replaces lcm closure by generated
subgroup closure.  In product and local additive rows, order or conductor
saturation can be a useful symmetry policy, but it is not the minimal B0
information investment.
