## P1/P6 (entire fns / Hilbert-Polya)
- arXiv:2511.23257 Connes & van Suijlekom, "Quadratic Forms, Real Zeros and Echoes of the Spectral Action" (28 Nov 2025; CMP 406:12).
  GENUINE LEVER. Unconditional [P] criterion: real distribution on [0,L] -> even distribution on [-L,L];
  if quadratic form is lower-bounded self-adjoint with SIMPLE ISOLATED lowest eigenvalue + EVEN eigenfunction xi,
  then ALL zeros of xi-hat(z) lie on the REAL line. Proof = C*-Caratheodory-Fejer + Hurwitz. Does NOT mention zeta;
  it is the abstract "real-zeros from a spectral gap" engine. Matrices = spectral-action perturbative expansion structure.
  >>> This is the abstract template Connes-route needs: "simple+even lowest eigenfunction => real zeros". Maps onto memory's
      Connes 6.6a "missing simple+even lowest eigenvalue". Unconditional engine; the RH-hardness is hidden in verifying the hypotheses.
- arXiv:2511.22755 Connes, Consani & Moscovici, "Zeta Spectral Triples" (27 Nov 2025).
  GENUINE LEVER (the live RH strategy). Rank-one perturbations of the scaling-operator spectral triple on [lambda^-1, lambda],
  Euler product over primes p <= x = lambda^2. Numerically [A] spectra -> zeros of zeta(1/2+is) as N, lambda -> inf;
  regularized determinants -> Riemann Xi. Strategy [C]: "a rigorous proof of this convergence would establish RH."
  Status: numerical only; convergence is the open step. This is the explicit successor to 2602.04022's prolate-convergence step.
- arXiv:2602.04022 Connes, "The Riemann Hypothesis: Past, Present and a Letter Through Time" (5 Feb 2026). The PIVOT (already in memory).
  Survey + finite-prime Weil quad form -> Thm 6.1 finite zeros-on-line; §6.5 prolate convergence k_lambda -> Xi.
  v1 does not visibly cite the Nov-2025 trio (it predates wide circulation / they are siblings, same program).
- arXiv:2310.18423 Connes & Moscovici, "Zeta zeros and prolate wave operators" (v2, 4 May 2024). Semilocal prolate operator; low-lying + UV zeros.
- "New eigenfunctions for the negative part of the Connes-Moscovici prolate spectrum" Comptes Rendus Math. 363 (2025) 1065-1081.
  [P] proves the non-classical CM eigenvalues are NEGATIVE (a CM conjecture) + fast computation via naive eigenvalues on imaginary axis.
  Journal-only; I could NOT verify an arXiv id (do not cite an arXiv number for this one).
- Berry-Keating / canonical-systems context: Suzuki canonical-system criterion (de Branges Hamiltonian PSD <=> RH, cond.) is the older frame.
  Bessonov-Denisov 2024: dynamical characterization of Szego-class spectral measures for de Branges canonical systems / Dirac / Krein strings

## P3 (trilinear Kloosterman / level of distribution)
- arXiv:2604.25177 Wright, "Trilinear Kloosterman fractions I: partially fixed moduli and unbalanced convolutions" (28 Apr 2026).
  GENUINE LEVER (incremental, real). Level of distribution Q <= X^{1/2 + 1/66 - delta} (cond. i), and Q <= X^{45/89 - eps} (cond. ii,
  improving prior X^{53/105}). theta_0 ~ 0.515 (1/2 + 1/66). Improves Bettin-Chandee for FIXED denominator factor R: R^{1/4} (not R^{1/2}/R).

- arXiv:2511.07550 Milicevic, Qin & Wu, "Bilinear forms with Kloosterman sums and moments of twisted L-functions" (10 Nov 2025).
  GENUINE LEVER for primorial moduli. Power-saving bilinear Kloosterman bounds for ARBITRARY modulus q with NO FACTORABILITY conditions
  (removes the factorability constraints of Blomer-Fouvry-Kowalski-Michel-Milicevic-Sawin). Both variables below Polya-Vinogradov range.
  RP-independent. >>> Directly relevant: prior level-of-distribution machinery needed factorable q; this removes that for primorial-type q.
