## Abstract

We construct a native Nambu-Fisher mechanics on the categorical probability simplex \(\Delta_R^\circ\) by replacing the symplectic 2-form with the Fisher volume form \(\Omega_R^F\). The resulting \((Q-1)\)-ary bracket is well-defined in any dimension and inherits global existence and long-time classification from the square-root Fisher compactification.

For the minimal mixed ring \(R = \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}\), we prove that the maximal continuous Lie subalgebra of Fisher-volume-preserving symmetries that respects the CRT-tensor structure of \(\mathbb{C}[R]\) is precisely the Cartan subalgebra \(\mathfrak{u}(1)^2 \subset \mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3)\). Thus hypercharge and electromagnetism arise canonically as classical symmetries of the real simplex, while the full nonabelian factors \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\) and \(\mathrm{SU}(3)\) do not act on \(\Delta_R^\circ\).

This obstruction is sharp and representation-theoretic: a generic element of \(\mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3)\) rotates the trivial character into nontrivial characters and fails to preserve the real positive cone. The full nonabelian gauge action is recovered only upon lifting the dynamics to the density-matrix manifold \(D(\mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^3)\), where local unitaries act naturally. Quantization is therefore forced by representation theory.

The framework is empirically grounded by direct measurements in two independent transformer families (Llama-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B-v0.3). Linear summaries of behavior manifolds exhibit statistically significant correlation collapse relative to Fisher/Hellinger coordinates, while activation pullback audits confirm that the measured structure is intrinsic to the output simplex. These results establish that high-dimensional cross-entropy optimization spontaneously realizes conductor geometry.

An exact 5-dimensional volume-dual Nambu-Fisher integrator is supplied as a consistency check. The paper contains no claim of gravity, renormalization-group flow, chirality, three generations, Yukawa couplings, anomaly cancellation, or a complete quantum field theory. It offers a classical information-geometric mechanics whose arithmetic skeleton stops at the Cartan and whose completion to the full Standard Model gauge group requires the quantum density-matrix lift.

*Proof.* Nambu nondegeneracy of the top-degree volume form gives existence and uniqueness. Conservation is immediate. Global existence and classification follow from Paper 13, Theorems 14.9–14.13.

## 2. The Classical Cartan Obstruction (Formalization)

Let \(R = \mathbb{Z}/6\mathbb{Z} \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/3\mathbb{Z}\). The group algebra decomposes by the Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT) as \(\mathbb{C}[R] \cong \mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^3\).

The Fisher tangent space at the centroid \(p_* = \frac{1}{6}\mathbf{1}\) orthogonally decomposes into conductor packets \(X_{\mathfrak{e}}\) in the character basis (as defined in Paper 11). Let \(Q_{\mathfrak{e}}\) denote the orthogonal projectors onto these packets.

**Definition 2.1 (CRT-Tensor-Compatible Derivation).** A derivation \(D \in \mathfrak{gl}(\mathbb{C}[R])\) is *CRT-tensor-compatible* if it satisfies two conditions:

1. **Locality:** It decomposes over the CRT tensor product as \(D = D_2 \otimes I_3 + I_2 \otimes D_3\), where \(D_2 \in \mathfrak{gl}(\mathbb{C}^2)\) and \(D_3 \in \mathfrak{gl}(\mathbb{C}^3)\).
2. **Conductor Preservation:** It commutes with the conductor packet projectors, \([D, Q_{\mathfrak{e}}] = 0\) for all \(\mathfrak{e}\).

**Theorem 2.2′ (The Classical Cartan Obstruction).** Let \(\mathfrak{g} \subset \mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3)\) be the maximal continuous Lie subalgebra of Fisher-volume-preserving symmetries acting on \(\mathbb{C}[R]\) that satisfies the following three classical conditions:

1. **Centroid-fixing:** \(\mathfrak{g} \cdot p_* = 0\).
2. **Real-cone-preserving:** The exponential map \(\exp(t \mathfrak{g})\) preserves the real positive simplex \(\Delta_R^\circ \subset \mathbb{R}_{>0}^R\).
3. **CRT-compatibility:** Every element of \(\mathfrak{g}\) is a CRT-tensor-compatible derivation.

Then \(\mathfrak{g}\) is exactly the Cartan subalgebra:
\[
\mathfrak{g} = \mathfrak{u}(1)^2 \subset \mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3).
\]

**Proof.**  
By Condition 3, the generators of \(\mathfrak{g}\) must respect the tensor decomposition and preserve the conductor filtration.

Consider an arbitrary element \(X \in \mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3)\). To satisfy Condition 2, the unitary flow \(e^{tX}\) must map strictly real positive probability vectors to strictly real positive probability vectors. In the character basis, real probability distributions satisfy specific complex conjugacy constraints.

Generic off-diagonal elements in \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\) and \(\mathfrak{su}(3)\) rotate the trivial character into non-trivial characters, generating imaginary components that push the state out of the real positive cone \(\mathbb{R}_{>0}^R\). One might attempt to construct purely real orthogonal rotations (e.g., the \(\mathfrak{so}(2)\) subalgebra of \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\) acting as \(SO(2)\) on the real components). However, such off-diagonal real rotations explicitly mix distinct conductor packets (e.g., mixing the trivial character with the alternating character), which violates the \([X, Q_{\mathfrak{e}}] = 0\) requirement of Condition 3.

Therefore, the only elements of \(\mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3)\) that simultaneously preserve the real cone and respect the CRT arithmetic filtration are those strictly diagonal in the character basis. The diagonal elements of \(\mathfrak{su}(2) \oplus \mathfrak{su}(3)\) form its Cartan subalgebra. Subtracting the global trace (Condition 1, centroid-fixing) restricts this exactly to \(\mathfrak{u}(1) \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1) \cong \mathfrak{u}(1)^2\). \(\blacksquare\)
