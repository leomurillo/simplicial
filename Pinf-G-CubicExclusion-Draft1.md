## 3. Lemma G.1: one-sided dynamics at a marginal boundary fixed point $[\mathrm P]$

**Lemma G.1.** *Let $P\subset\mathbb R^N$ be a closed convex cone, $x^**\in\partial P$, and $\nu$ a supporting functional at $x^**$ ($\nu\ge0$ on $P$, $\langle\nu,x^**\rangle=0$). *(i) $\langle\nu,DT(x^**)w\rangle\ge0$ for every admissible $w$.*

*(ii) If $v$ is admissible and $\nu$-marginal — $\langle\nu,DT(x^**)v\rangle=0$ — then $\langle\nu,D^2T(x^**)[v,v]\rangle\ge0$.*

*(iii) If $\pm v$ are both admissible (a tangential mode) with $\langle\nu,DT(x^**)v\rangle=0$ and $\langle\nu,D^2T(x^**)[v,v]\rangle=0$, then*
$$\big\langle\nu,\;D^3T(x^**)[v,v,v]\big\rangle\;=\;0:$$
*odd-order pushes along a tangential marginal mode must vanish at a boundary fixed point.*

*Proof.* (i) $0\le\langle\nu,T(x^**+\varepsilon w)\rangle=\varepsilon\langle\nu,DTw\rangle+O(\varepsilon^2)$; divide by $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. (ii) With the linear term dead, $0\le\langle\nu,T(x^**+\varepsilon v)\rangle=\tfrac{\varepsilon^2}2\langle\nu,D^2T[v,v]\rangle+O(\varepsilon^3)$. (iii) With linear and quadratic terms dead, $0\le\tfrac{\varepsilon^3}6\langle\nu,D^3T[v,v,v]\rangle+O(\varepsilon^4)$ for $\varepsilon>0$ gives $\ge0$; replacing $v\to-v$ (admissible) gives $\le0$. $\square$
