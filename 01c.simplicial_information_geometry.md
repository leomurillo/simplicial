## Abstract

The zero-sum hyperplane $H=\{c:\sum_i c_i=0\}$ of the $N$-vertex simplicial frame is the tangent space, at every interior point, of the categorical (probability) simplex. We express that simplex's information geometry intrinsically in frame coordinates and find that the simplicial frame *is* the natural arena for it. Five facts, all exact-certified.

**(i) Dual flatness is manifest.** The zero-sum simplicial coordinate $c$ (with $p=\tfrac1N\mathbf 1+c$) is the mixture-affine ($m$-affine) chart, and the Fisher metric is the Hessian of the negative-entropy potential $\varphi(p)=\sum_i p_i\log p_i$. So $(\Delta,g,\nabla^{(\pm1)})$ is dually flat with the frame itself as the flat $m$-chart; the dual $e$-chart is $\theta=\log p$ taken modulo the gauge direction $\mathbf 1$.

**(ii) The cumulant tower is diagonal and factors at every order.** The order-$n$ rung $T^{(n)}=\nabla^n\varphi$ is purely diagonal, $T^{(n)}_{i\cdots i}=(-1)^n(n-2)!\,p_i^{-(n-1)}$, and factors as
$$T^{(n)}=c_n\,\Delta^{(n)}\,D_p^{\,n-1},\qquad c_n=(-1)^n(n-2)!,\ \ D_p=\operatorname{diag}(1/p_i),$$
where the all-equal-index selector $\Delta^{(n)}$ is **position-independent**: the only breathing factor is the diagonal Fisher weight $D_p^{\,n-1}$, and the coefficient $c_n$ is a pure integer. Order 2 is the Fisher metric; order 3 is the moving Amari–Chentsov cubic.

**(iii) The moving cubic is frozen geometry times a diagonal.** The raised order-3 operator is $K^{CH}_p(u)v=D_p(u\odot v)-\langle u,v\rangle_{g_p}\,p$, reducing at $p_*$ to the Corollary 01.3.9 form $N(u\odot v)-(\sum_iu_iv_i)\mathbf 1$. Its cross-product-square realization of the componentwise product $\odot$ — the fixed projectors $I+K(\tau_i)^2$ of Paper 01 — is position-independent: the cross-product geometry of Paper 01 is frozen, and only the Fisher weights move.

**(iv) The $\alpha$-family collapses to the cubic.** In the $m$-affine chart the Levi-Civita connection satisfies $\Gamma^{(0)}=-\tfrac12 T$, so the entire one-parameter family is $\Gamma^{(\alpha)}=-\tfrac{1+\alpha}{2}T$: the $m$-connection is the trivial coordinate derivative, the $e$-connection is $-T$, and dual flatness is the curvature identity $R^{(e)}=0$ on the single cubic.

**(v) The dual chart carries the real cumulants — and the obstruction.** The Legendre dual of $\varphi$ is the log-partition $\Phi(\theta)=\log\sum_i e^{\theta_i}$, whose tower $\nabla^n\Phi$ is the genuine cumulant tower of the categorical distribution, *non*-diagonal with $2{+}2$ Wick structure. The two towers are exchanged by the $\alpha\mapsto-\alpha$ involution, self-dual at order $2$ (Fisher). The entropy tower's diagonality has a consequence: it provably **cannot host the program's quartic obstruction**, which is the $2{+}2$ Wick block of $\nabla^4\Phi$ — localizing that obstruction to the exponential/character chart.

The information geometry here is classical [Amari, Chentsov, Nielsen, Ay–Jost–Lê–Schwachhöfer]; what is new is its simplicial-frame presentation, the cross-product-square factorization that welds Paper 01's algebra to the skewness tensor at every order, and the chart-localization of the quartic obstruction.

**Keywords:** information geometry, categorical simplex, Fisher metric, Amari–Chentsov tensor, skewness tensor, cumulant tower, α-connection, dual flatness, mixture coordinates, exponential family, Legendre transform, negative entropy, log-partition, simplicial coordinates, Quadray coordinates.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The simplex's geometry, native to the frame

The categorical simplex is the basic object of information geometry; its Fisher metric, $\alpha$-connections, Amari–Chentsov tensor, and dual-flat structure are textbook [Amari, Nielsen, Ay–Jost–Lê–Schwachhöfer]. Corollary 01.3.9 gave a single-point glimpse of this from the simplicial frame: at the center $p_*$, the Fisher metric is the integer simplicial inner product $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle_M$ and the Amari–Chentsov cubic is built from the cross-product squares $I+K(\tau_i)^2$. This paper supplies the moving picture across the whole simplex, and the result is that the frame is not just compatible with the information geometry but is its natural chart: **the zero-sum simplicial coordinate is the mixture-affine coordinate**, and the gauge direction $\mathbf 1$ is the normalization constraint $\sum_i p_i=1$.

The organizing object is the **cumulant tower** $T^{(n)}=\nabla^n\varphi$ of the negative-entropy potential. Its order-$2$ rung is the Fisher metric, its order-$3$ rung the Amari–Chentsov cubic, and — read in the frame — every rung has the same shape: a fixed combinatorial object modulated by a diagonal Fisher weight. The cross product of Paper 01 is frozen into the order-$3$ rung's geometry; all connection content collapses to that one cubic; and the dual (log-partition) chart is where the genuine cumulants and the program's quartic obstruction live.

### 1.2 Contributions

1. **Dual-flat structure in frame coordinates (§3).** Frame = $m$-affine chart; Fisher $=\operatorname{Hess}\varphi$, $\varphi=\sum_i p_i\log p_i$; $e$-chart $=\log p\bmod\mathbf 1$.

## 2. The categorical simplex and its tangent frame

Let $\Delta=\{p\in\mathbb{R}^N:p_i>0,\ \sum_i p_i=1\}$ be the open categorical simplex. Its tangent space at any $p$ is the zero-sum hyperplane $H=\{v:\sum_i v_i=0\}$ — the same $H$ that carries the cross product of Paper 01 and the differential operators of Paper 01b. The frame thus globally trivializes $T\Delta$, with $\mathbf 1$ normal to every tangent space.

The **Fisher metric** at an interior $p$ is, for $u,v\in H$,
$$g_p(u,v)=\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{u_iv_i}{p_i}.$$
At $p_*=\tfrac1N\mathbf 1$ this is $N\sum_i u_iv_i=\langle u,v\rangle_M$, the integer simplicial inner product of §01.7.5 (Corollary 01.3.9) — and, by the structural companion paper, the $K_N$ graph Laplacian on $H$. Every $p\in\Delta$ is $p=\tfrac1N\mathbf 1+c$ with $c\in H$ the **simplicial (mixture) coordinate**, $c_i=p_i-\tfrac1N$, the affine translate of §01.2.2.1.

## 3. Dual flatness in mixture coordinates

A statistical manifold is *dually flat* when its metric is the Hessian of a convex potential in some affine chart; that chart and its Legendre dual are the two flat coordinate systems, and $\nabla^{(\pm1)}$ are flat in them [Amari]. For the simplex this is classical; the point is that the frame is the flat $m$-chart.

**Proposition 3.1 (Fisher is the Hessian of negative entropy).** *Let $\varphi(p)=\sum_i p_i\log p_i$. Then $\operatorname{Hess}\varphi=\operatorname{diag}(1/p_i)=g_p$. Hence $(\Delta,g,\nabla^{(\pm1)})$ is dually flat, the simplicial coordinate $c$ (equivalently $p$) is the $m$-affine chart, and the dual $e$-affine chart is $\theta=\nabla\varphi=\log p+\mathbf 1\equiv\log p\pmod{\mathbf 1}$.*

*Proof.* $\partial_i\partial_j\varphi=\partial_i(\log p_j+1)=\delta_{ij}/p_i=g$ (Appendix A). A metric equal to a Hessian in an affine chart is dually flat, that chart $m$-affine, with Legendre dual $\theta=\nabla\varphi$ the $e$-chart [Amari §1.3]. The gauge ambiguity in $\theta$ is the $\mathbf 1$ from $\sum_i p_i=1$. $\square$

The two classical coordinate systems of the simplex are thus the two faces of the simplicial frame: the **mixture coordinate** is the zero-sum simplicial coordinate $c$, and the **exponential coordinate** is its logarithm modulo gauge. The gauge direction the whole series carries is, statistically, the normalization constraint.

## 4. The cumulant tower

The cumulant tower of the $m$-potential is $T^{(n)}=\nabla^n\varphi$. Because $\varphi=\sum_i g(p_i)$ is **separable** with $g(t)=t\log t$, the tower is diagonal at every order.

**Theorem 4.1 (Diagonal cumulant tower).** *For $n\ge2$, $T^{(n)}=\nabla^n\varphi$ has only all-equal-index components,*
$$T^{(n)}_{i_1\cdots i_n}=\begin{cases}(-1)^n(n-2)!\,p_i^{-(n-1)}, & i_1=\cdots=i_n=i,\\[2pt]0,&\text{otherwise,}\end{cases}$$
*the coefficients being $c_n=(-1)^n(n-2)!=1,-1,2,-6,24,\dots$ for $n=2,3,4,5,6$.*

*Proof.* $g^{(n)}(t)=\dfrac{d^n}{dt^n}(t\log t)=(-1)^n(n-2)!\,t^{-(n-1)}$ for $n\ge2$ (induction from $g''=1/t$). Separability kills every mixed partial. Certified exhaustively over all $N^n$ multi-indices for $N=4$, $n=2..6$, and an $N=5$ check, in Appendix A. $\square$

**Theorem 4.2 (Frozen geometry $\times$ breathing diagonal, all orders).** *Write $\Delta^{(n)}$ for the position-independent all-equal-index selector ($\Delta^{(n)}_{i\cdots i}=1$, else $0$) and $D_p=\operatorname{diag}(1/p_i)$. Then for every $n\ge2$,*
$$T^{(n)}=c_n\,\Delta^{(n)}\,D_p^{\,n-1},\qquad c_n=(-1)^n(n-2)!.$$
*The combinatorial object $\Delta^{(n)}$ and the integer $c_n$ are independent of $p$; the sole position-dependent factor is the diagonal Fisher weight $D_p^{\,n-1}$.*

This is the promotion to all orders of the order-3 picture of §5: across the whole tower, the geometry is frozen and only the Fisher weighting breathes. The order-$2$ rung is the Fisher metric ($\Delta^{(2)}=I$ on the diagonal, $c_2=1$, $D_p^1$); the order-$3$ rung is the moving Amari–Chentsov cubic of §5.

## 5. The moving Amari–Chentsov cubic

The **Amari–Chentsov tensor** is the totally symmetric skewness tensor $T_p(u,v,w)=\sum_i u_iv_iw_i/p_i^2$ ($=\nabla^3\varphi$ up to sign), and its metric-raised form is the cubic operator $K^{CH}_p$ with $g_p(K^{CH}_p(u)v,w)=T_p(u,v,w)$.

**Theorem 5.1 (Moving cubic; frozen-geometry factorization).** *For $u,v\in H$ and interior $p$,*
$$K^{CH}_p(u)\,v=D_p\,(u\odot v)-\langle u,v\rangle_{g_p}\,p,\qquad D_p=\operatorname{diag}(1/p_i),$$
*with $\odot$ componentwise multiplication. It lies in $H$, is totally symmetric as a cubic, and reduces at $p_*=\tfrac1N\mathbf 1$ to the Corollary 01.3.9 form $N(u\odot v)-(\sum_iu_iv_i)\mathbf 1$. The componentwise product $\odot$ admits the position-independent cross-product-square realization of Proposition 01.3.8, built from the fixed centered vertex axes $\tau_i=e_i-\tfrac1N\mathbf 1$ via the projectors $I+K(\tau_i)^2$; only the diagonal Fisher weight $D_p$ depends on $p$.*

*Proof.* Raising the index with $g_p$ and a Lagrange multiplier for $\sum_i(\cdot)_i=0$ gives $(K^{CH}_p(u)v)_i=u_iv_i/p_i-\langle u,v\rangle_{g_p}p_i$; the correction lies along $p$ and the multiplier is fixed by $\sum_i p_i=1$. Total symmetry follows since $g_p(K^{CH}_p(u)v,w)=\sum_i u_iv_iw_i/p_i^2-\langle u,v\rangle_{g_p}\sum_i w_i=\sum_i u_iv_iw_i/p_i^2$ for $w\in H$. The $p_*$ reduction and the projector realization are Corollary 01.3.9 / Proposition 01.3.8, both $p$-independent. Certified in Appendix A. $\square$

**Reading.** Paper 01 warned that the cubic "is not represented by a single binary cross-product operator" away from $p_*$. That is correct — but the obstruction is *only* the diagonal reweighting $D_p$. The cross-product geometry (the squares $I+K(\tau_i)^2$ that turn the frame into the componentwise product) is frozen across the simplex; the Amari–Chentsov cubic is the fixed tetrahedral multiplication of Paper 01 heard through a position-dependent diagonal gain.

## 6. The α-connection collapse

The $\alpha$-connections interpolate the metric (Levi-Civita, $\alpha=0$) and the dual flat pair, with Christoffels $\Gamma^{(\alpha)}_{ij,k}=\Gamma^{(0)}_{ij,k}-\tfrac{\alpha}{2}T_{ijk}$ [Amari].

**Theorem 6.1 (Collapse to the cubic).** *In the simplicial ($m$-affine) chart, $\Gamma^{(0)}=-\tfrac12 T$ identically. Hence for all $\alpha$,*
$$\Gamma^{(\alpha)}=-\tfrac{1+\alpha}{2}\,T,$$
*so the $m$-connection $\Gamma^{(-1)}=0$ is the trivial coordinate derivative (manifest flatness), the $e$-connection is $\Gamma^{(+1)}=-T$, and the whole family is carried by the single Amari–Chentsov cubic.*

*Proof.* The mixture chart is affine for the mixture family, so $\Gamma^{(-1)}=\Gamma^{(0)}+\tfrac12 T=0$, giving $\Gamma^{(0)}=-\tfrac12 T$; substitution gives the family. Verified in the $(p_1,\dots,p_{N-1})$ chart in Appendix A: the Levi-Civita Christoffels of $g$ equal $-\tfrac12 T$. $\square$

**Dual flatness as a curvature identity.** In frame coordinates dual flatness reduces to two statements about the one tensor $T$. The first, $\Gamma^{(-1)}=0$, is automatic (the frame is the mixture chart). The second is the nontrivial half: $R^{(+1)}=0$, the curvature of $\Gamma^{(+1)}=-T$ vanishes, with flat coordinates $\theta=\log p\bmod\mathbf 1$. The entire dual-flat package is thus a statement about $K^{CH}$ alone — the same cubic that, by §5, is the frozen cross product modulated by the Fisher diagonal.

## 7. The two charts and the Legendre dual

*Forward pointer.* The reading of $\Phi$ as the program's "one functional" (effective action) is derived in Paper 54 (*The One Functional, Derived*, Theorem 1): $\Phi$ is the generating function of the completely passive states, and its Fenchel–Young gap with $\varphi=\Phi^*$ is the relative entropy.

The mixture potential $\varphi$ has Legendre dual the **log-partition** (free energy)
$$\Phi(\theta)=\log\sum_i e^{\theta_i},\qquad \theta=\nabla\varphi=\log p\bmod\mathbf 1,$$
the canonical potential of the categorical distribution as an exponential family. Its tower $\nabla^n\Phi$ is the **genuine cumulant tower** of the distribution — order $2$ the covariance $\operatorname{diag}(p)-pp^\top$, and higher orders carrying the connected/Wick structure of cumulants. The two towers are dual.

**Proposition 7.1 (Two towers, one involution).** *The diagonal entropy tower $T^{(n)}=\nabla^n\varphi$ ($m$-chart) and the cumulant tower $\nabla^n\Phi$ ($e$-chart) are exchanged by the $\alpha\mapsto-\alpha$ duality; at the uniform point they are sign-opposite at every order, self-dual at $n=2$ (the Fisher metric), and inversely scaled (powers $p^{-(n-1)}$ versus $p$-polynomial) by the order-by-order Jacobian of the Legendre map.* (Certified at the uniform point, Appendix A.)

**The conductor blind spot is order-2 visibility.** An instrument that reads only the second-order structure sees the metric $g=\nabla^2\varphi=\nabla^2\Phi^*$ — the unique rung that is flat at the universal eigenvalue $N$ (structural companion paper) — and is blind to every higher cumulant. The factorization of §4 makes this concrete: order $2$ is the one rung whose value does not breathe with $p$ beyond the metric; orders $\ge3$ carry the genuine state-dependent content.

**The quartic obstruction lives in the exponential chart.** The program's quartic obstruction is, by construction, the $2{+}2$ Wick block of the order-$4$ log-partition cumulant $\nabla^4\Phi$ — a connected-minus-Gaussian quantity that is generically nonzero. The entropy tower $\nabla^4\varphi$ is **purely diagonal** (Theorem 4.1): it has no $i\,i\,j\,j$ component at all. Therefore:

**Proposition 7.2 (Chart localization of the obstruction).** *The quartic obstruction cannot be carried by the entropy ($m$-chart) cumulant tower; it is necessarily a phenomenon of the exponential/character ($e$-chart) tower $\nabla^n\Phi$. The dual entropy chart is provably obstruction-free at the $2{+}2$ level.*

This is an honest negative result: the all-orders factorization (Theorem 4.2) *survives* at order 4 precisely because the tower is diagonal, and that diagonality is exactly why it cannot host the obstruction. The obstruction's home is the conjugate chart.

## 8. Placement and a fenced speculation

**Floor under cubic mechanics.** The program's "cubic information-geometric mechanics on the categorical simplex" builds dynamics whose cubic term is the Amari–Chentsov tensor and whose connection data is the $\alpha$-family; Theorems 5.1 and 6.1 give those papers their geometric object and connection in closed frame-coordinate form. Where Paper 01b is the floor under the vector-calculus layer, this paper is the floor under the connection/curvature layer.

**The cumulant tower is the order axis.** Since $T^{(n)}=\nabla^n\varphi$, the tower is literally the derivative ladder of the convex potential; "cumulant order $n$" is the order axis whose pairing with the Hodge degree axis is treated in the structural companion paper (they are the two coordinates of the FEEC polynomial-form bicomplex). The metric/order-2 versus higher-order split *is* the conductor blind spot.

**Fenced speculation (not load-bearing).** The certified $\varphi\leftrightarrow\Phi$ Legendre pair, with its order-by-order sign involution and self-dual order-2 rung, is the cleanest *finite* model of the slogan "global Legendre $=$ Tate–Poisson $=$ functional equation," and its diagonal/off-diagonal split mirrors the even/chiral channel split of the arithmetic program. We flag this as analogy only: a finite, additive, separable potential cannot contain the archimedean functional equation, the order-2 self-duality is elementary here, and no spectral operator is built. This note advances nothing on the arithmetic spine and is recorded for orientation, not as a claim.

## 9. Conclusion and open problems

In frame coordinates the information geometry of the categorical simplex is transparent: the frame is the mixture-affine chart, the Fisher metric is the Hessian of negative entropy, the cumulant tower is diagonal and factors at every order into a frozen combinatorial object times a breathing Fisher diagonal, the moving Amari–Chentsov cubic is Paper 01's frozen cross product so modulated, every $\alpha$-connection is a multiple of that one cubic, and the genuine cumulants (with the quartic obstruction) live in the conjugate exponential chart.

Open, in convergence-friendly order:

- **D1.** The $e$-flat curvature identity $R^{(+1)}=0$ written explicitly as a quadratic identity on $T$ in frame coordinates (integrability of $\log p\bmod\mathbf 1$).
- **D2.** The metric-raised higher operators $K^{(n)}_p$ generalizing $K^{CH}_p$: does the cross-product-square realization of $\odot$ extend to an $n$-fold componentwise product through the fixed projectors $I+K(\tau_i)^2$?
- **D3.** Geodesics: $m$-geodesics are straight lines in $c$ (manifest); the $e$-geodesics and the dual ("Pythagorean") decomposition in frame coordinates, and their relation to the rotation $R(u,\theta)$ of Paper 01.
- **D4.** The quantum continuation: the moving cubic on the density-matrix manifold (the quantum Amari–Chentsov tensor), where the monotone-metric fork (Bures versus Kubo–Mori) replaces the unique Fisher metric.
