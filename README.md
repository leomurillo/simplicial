## Abstract Summaries

### Identifying 3 — Trilogy

- [ל1](C:/Projects/simplicial/T1-NativeSimplicialChart.md): Presents the native simplicial chart for categorical information geometry. Refuses the standard coordinate elimination, keeps the redundant $N$-th coordinate, treats $\sum p_i = 1$ as gauge fixing on the overcomplete frame $\mathbb{R}^N$, and reads probabilities as squared amplitudes $p_i = a_i^2$. The Fisher–Rao metric becomes the sphere pullback under square-root, the tangent space at any interior point is the zero-sum hyperplane $H_0$, and the isometry group at the uniform is $\mathrm{SO}(H_0)$. For $N = 4$ the chart's intrinsic vector algebra reproduces ordinary 3D Euclidean vector calculus exactly. Walks through worked examples for $N = 2, 3, 4$ and supplies the toolkit ל2 and ל3 share.
- [ל2](C:/Projects/simplicial/T2-CumulantTowerEffectiveAction.md): Identifies the categorical log-partition $\Phi(\eta) = \log Z(\eta)$ as the single scalar functional on ל1's chart whose successive cumulants generate the geometric tower: $\nabla\Phi = p$ (position), $\nabla^2\Phi$ = Fisher information (metric and inertia), $\nabla^3\Phi$ = Amari–Chentsov 3-tensor (the cubic structure that defines information geometry beyond Riemannian geometry), and $\nabla^4\Phi$ and higher = projection-closure defects. The order-2-to-order-3 leap is the load-bearing distinction — the third cumulant is the first invariant Riemannian geometry cannot see — and is the "3" of the trilogy title.
- [ל3](C:/Projects/simplicial/T3-CharacterOrthogonalityConductorLattice.md): Reads ל2's cumulant tensors through Pontryagin duality on $\mathbb{Z}/N$. Decomposes $H_0$ into conductor packets $H_d$ indexed by non-trivial divisors $d \mid N$ via character orthogonality. The Fisher form $\Sigma$ at the uniform is scalar in the character basis (order 2 carries no cross-packet content), while $T$ satisfies the modular selection rule $a + b + c \equiv 0 \pmod{N}$ (order 3 couples packets across divisors). The single line $k + \ell + m \equiv 0 \pmod{N}$ is the load-bearing arithmetic of the trilogy.
