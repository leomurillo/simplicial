## §3. Local units, universal ratios

### §3.1 The classical distinction

Physics distinguishes between:

- **Dimensionful constants** ($G$, $\hbar$, $c$, $e$, particle masses in MeV): require unit conventions; differ across unit systems (SI vs CGS vs Planck).
- **Dimensionless constants** ($\alpha$, $\sin^2\theta_W$, $m_p/m_e$, holographic exponent, generation count): invariant under unit conventions; same in every unit system.

The dimensionful constants are conventionally anchored — meters and seconds are human-scale anchors that mean nothing without a measurement protocol. The dimensionless constants are nature's universal numbers — Edward Witten remarked that any extraterrestrial physicist would compute the same $\alpha \approx 1/137$ regardless of their unit choices.

The simplicial framework's objects are FINITE COMBINATORIAL structures: characters on $(\mathbb{Z}/P_n)^\wedge$, cumulant tensors, Helmert coordinates, conductor packets. These objects have NO native units — they are dimensionless by construction.

- Spatial dim 3 (integer count of admitted dimensions).
- Signature (3,1) (integer count + sign).
- Four forces (integer count of conductor-induced sectors at P_2).

- Three colors (integer count of cubic conductor packets at p=3).
- Two prime sectors at $P_2 = 6$ (integer count of primes in primorial).
- $L(1/2, \chi_3) \approx 0.48085$ (dimensionless special value).
- Capacity_V exponent → −2 (asymptotic log-log slope, dimensionless).

NONE of these has units. ALL of them are universal in the sense that any extraterrestrial mathematician would derive the same number.

### §3.3 The §11.2 firewall, properly read

Paper 32 §11.2 (verbatim, reordered for emphasis):

> *"because the simplex carries no length, time, or mass scale (Paper 20 §4), this program may attempt only dimensionless quantities fixed by conductor/cumulant structure. Anything carrying a unit — $\hbar$, $c$, $G$, the Planck scale, any absolute mass or length — is out of scope by construction"*

The firewall forbids UNITS. It explicitly admits "dimensionless quantities fixed by conductor/cumulant structure." This is precisely what the framework computes.

Paper 32 §11.2 continues:

> *"A subtle but admissible category sits inside the firewall: constants that are unity-by-construction, whose content is the theorem that they are unity and invariant, not a numerical value. Corollary 9.7's $c \equiv 1$ is the archetype: the framework derives that an invariant speed exists, is frame-independent, is the hyperboloid asymptote, and equals 1 in internal units — and explicitly does NOT predict its metre/second value. This is the correct template for any future constant claim."*

T32 generalizes: **any dimensionless ratio the framework derives is admissible, provided the program does not promote it to dimensionful magnitudes by smuggling in unit conversions**.

This is not a relaxation of discipline — it is a sharpening. The proper reading: **the framework's $3/8$ IS the tree-level Weinberg angle**. Both are dimensionless. Both are universal. No unit conversion is involved.
