## Abstract

Linear interpretability tools are usually evaluated by whether they recover a
behaviorally meaningful direction or subspace.  This paper gives a sharper,
falsifiable obstruction.  When the behavior labels carry a finite abelian output
algebra `R`, and a readout is constrained to factor through a quotient
`q: R -> R_k`, the quotient projection is diagonal in the Pontryagin character
basis and retains exactly the characters in the annihilator of `ker(q)`.  For
the canonical cyclic quotient `C_N -> C_k`, this becomes the concrete divisor
rule: the readout retains exactly the conductor packets `H_d` with `d | k` and
annihilates exactly the packets with `d` not dividing `k`.  The equality is
sharp: every excluded nonzero packet contains a unit signal with exactly zero
surviving energy, and every included packet is retained with unit surviving
energy.

The result is then specialized to interpretability readouts.  A logit lens,
tuned lens, or trained affine probe whose codomain is a `k`-resolution quotient,
or whose full scores are post-composed with such a quotient before evaluation,
can be tuned arbitrarily well inside that quotient codomain; after lifting back
to the original label space its image remains inside the quotient subspace and
has zero coefficient in every forbidden conductor packet.  A full-vocabulary
tuned lens is outside this hypothesis until it is binned or otherwise
quotient-tied.  This is not a claim that Fisher coordinates preserve structure
while linear probes lose it; it is a quantitative prediction about which
linear/logit-lens/probe/SAE-style readouts must fail as a function of the
output label algebra, together with explicit escape conditions for full-rank
probes, base-10 shortcut mechanisms, non-equivariant activation maps, and
mismatched token algebras.

For the cyclic month task `C_12`, the preregistered table is:

| quotient k | H_2 | H_3 | H_4 | H_6 | H_12 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 6 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 12 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

On the saved Llama-3.1-8B month task, the non-tautological empirical fact is
that the native final month-token logits are already conductor-structured:
their packet fractions are `(0.050608, 0.066390, 0.059082, 0.166506, 0.657415)`
for `(H_2,H_3,H_4,H_6,H_12)`, so the full-period packet `H_12` carries
`0.657415` of the nontrivial energy while the parity packet `H_2` carries only
`0.050608`.  That number could have come out flat; it did not.  The theorem then
explains exactly what quotient-tied readouts must discard from that native
structure.  The aligned `C_12` certificate and the post-binning `10^-33` floors
are implementation checks of the projector/pullback, not model evidence.
The empirical evidence is instead (i) the native pre-bin packet fractions,
(ii) the held-out accuracies retained by coarse hidden-state probes despite
their packet blindness, and (iii) the shuffled-label and same-rank
random-subspace controls, which show that the divisor law is not generic to
rank.  A live hidden-state shortcut audit further shows that result-month
accuracy survives after linearly residualizing source month and offset number:
on raw layer-28 BF16 activations the full result probe rises from `0.958` to
`1.000` after residualization, while source-month and offset probes collapse to
`0.000` and `0.042` held-out accuracy.  This falsifies the linear input-shortcut
alternative.  The stronger nonlinear base-10 alternative remains real at the
hidden-state layer: a targeted decimal sum/carry ablation drops raw-BF16 result
accuracy to `0.458` and reduces the full-label hidden-probe `H_12` fraction to
`0.065`.  Thus the empirical discovery is localized: intermediate hidden-state
probes are substantially entangled with decimal addition features, while the
native final logits concentrate into the full-period cyclic packet.

## Theorem Stack

### T1. Pontryagin obstruction

Let `R` be a finite abelian output label algebra, written additively, and let
`q: R -> R_k` be a quotient with kernel `K` and `|R_k| = k`.  Let `P_q` be the
orthogonal projection in `L^2(R)` onto the subspace of functions factoring
through `q`, equivalently the functions constant on cosets of `K`.

Then `P_q` is diagonal in the Pontryagin character basis.  A character `chi`
survives iff `chi` is trivial on `K`, i.e. iff `chi` lies in the annihilator
`K^perp` and descends to a character of `R_k`.

For general finite abelian `R`, this annihilator statement is the theorem.  The
short divisor form below is the canonical cyclic specialization: when
`R = C_N`, `k | N`, and `q_k: C_N -> C_k` is the quotient of order `k`, it is
exactly:

```text
P_q H_d = H_d  iff  d | k
P_q H_d = 0    iff  d does not divide k.
```

Here `H_d` is the conductor-d packet.  The equality is sharp: for every
`d` not dividing `k`, every nonzero packet-pure signal `h_d in H_d` has
`P_q h_d = 0`, so the surviving packet energy is exactly zero.  For every
`d | k`, every `h_d in H_d` is fixed by `P_q`, so the surviving packet energy
is exactly one.

**Proof.**  Give `L^2(R)` the normalized counting inner product

```text
<f,g> = |R|^{-1} sum_{x in R} f(x) conjugate(g(x)).
```

Let

```text
V_q = {F o q : F in L^2(R_k)}
```

be the quotient-respecting readout subspace.  Since the fibers of `q` are the
cosets `x + K`, the orthogonal projection onto `V_q` is coset averaging:

```text
(P_q f)(x) = |K|^{-1} sum_{a in K} f(x + a).                 (1)
```

Indeed, `(P_q f)(x)` is constant on cosets of `K`, so `P_q f in V_q`.  If
`g = G o q in V_q`, then summing fiber by fiber gives

```text
<f - P_q f, g>
= |R|^{-1} sum_{y in R_k} conjugate(G(y))
    sum_{x in q^{-1}(y)} (f(x) - |K|^{-1} sum_{z in q^{-1}(y)} f(z))
= 0.
```

Thus (1) is the orthogonal projection.

Now let `chi in \hat R` be a character.  Applying (1),

```text
(P_q chi)(x)
= |K|^{-1} sum_{a in K} chi(x + a)
= chi(x) |K|^{-1} sum_{a in K} chi(a).                       (2)
```

If `chi` is trivial on `K`, the final average in (2) is `1`, so
`P_q chi = chi`.  If `chi` is nontrivial on `K`, choose `b in K` with
`chi(b) != 1`.  Writing `S = sum_{a in K} chi(a)`, translation by `b` permutes
`K`, hence

```text
S = sum_{a in K} chi(a + b) = chi(b) S.
```

Since `chi(b) != 1`, `S = 0`, and therefore `P_q chi = 0`.

It remains only to identify the surviving characters.  A character descends to
`R_k` exactly when it is trivial on `K`: if `chi = psi o q`, then `chi(a) = 1`
for every `a in K`; conversely, if `chi|_K = 1`, define
`psi(q(x)) = chi(x)`, which is well-defined because two representatives differ
by an element of `K`.  Therefore

```text
P_q chi = chi  iff  chi in K^perp = q^*(\hat R_k),
P_q chi = 0    iff  chi notin K^perp.
```

The characters of `R` form an orthonormal basis of `L^2(R)`, so `P_q` is
diagonal with only the eigenvalues `0` and `1` in that basis.

For the cyclic specialization, write `R = C_N = Z/NZ` and use characters

```text
chi_a(x) = exp(2 pi i a x / N),  a = 0,...,N-1.
```

The conductor, equivalently the order, of `chi_a` is

```text
cond(chi_a) = N / gcd(a,N).
```

For the quotient `q_k: C_N -> C_k`, the kernel is generated by `k mod N`.
Thus `chi_a` is trivial on the kernel iff

```text
chi_a(k) = 1
iff exp(2 pi i a k / N) = 1
iff N divides a k
iff N / gcd(a,N) divides k
iff cond(chi_a) | k.
```

Let `H_d` be the span of the characters with conductor `d`.  The preceding
line says that every basis character in `H_d` has projection eigenvalue `1`
when `d | k` and eigenvalue `0` when `d` does not divide `k`.  Hence

```text
P_q|_{H_d} = id_{H_d}  iff  d | k,
P_q|_{H_d} = 0         iff  d does not divide k.
```

For any packet-pure signal `h_d in H_d`,

```text
||P_q h_d||_2^2 / ||h_d||_2^2 =
  1  if d | k,
  0  if d does not divide k,
```

provided `h_d != 0`.  Sharpness is immediate: whenever `d` does not divide
`k`, any unit vector in the nonzero packet `H_d` is a unit signal with exactly
zero surviving energy.  Conversely, if `d | k`, every unit vector in `H_d` is
retained with exactly unit surviving energy.  This proves T1.

*FEP-bridge cross-reference (R1 / FEP-1, Paper 32 §12 INSERT 14;
reading-record only).*  The exact `d | k` survival law of T1 is the
**finite-exact arithmetic instance** of the FEP stack's adiabatic-
elimination / renormalisation step: which packets survive the
coarse-graining is fixed *exactly* (binary, parameter-free, no
asymptotics) by Pontryagin character orthogonality on
`(Z/N)^∧`. The FEP version is generic and asymptotic; this T1 is
finite and exact. **Pre-claimed non-correspondence (matched
pair):** the FEP's renormalisation is scale-recursive on any NESS;
the d∣k law is arithmetic and specific (conductor lattice). Bounded
correspondence of *form*, not of derivation; not citable as
external validation. See Paper 31 Remark 5.3.0 for the
load-bearing FEP-1 obligation (\(\Pi_S\) as a *bona fide* Markov
blanket; identify the Paper 11 finite-jet closure defect with the
adiabatic-elimination error bound).

### T2. Logit-lens corollary

Let `q: R -> R_k` be the quotient from T1.  Write

```text
q^*: L^2(R_k) -> L^2(R),      q^*F = F o q
```

for pullback to the original label space.  A layer-`ell` lens with target
algebra `R_k` is any affine map

```text
L_ell(h) = W_ell h + b_ell in L^2(R_k),
```

where `h` is the hidden state at that layer.  Its lifted full-label score
function is

```text
tilde L_ell(h) = q^* L_ell(h) in L^2(R).
```

This definition covers an ordinary logit lens tied to a coarse vocabulary
partition and a tuned lens whose learned affine translator has a quotient
target.  The weights `W_ell` and bias `b_ell` may be arbitrary and trained by
any objective; the only restriction is that the target algebra is `R_k`.

**Corollary.**  For every hidden state `h`,

```text
tilde L_ell(h) in im(q^*) = V_q.
```

Therefore, for every character `chi notin K^perp`,

```text
<tilde L_ell(h), chi> = 0.
```

In the cyclic specialization `R = C_N`, this says that for every conductor
packet `H_d`,

```text
Pi_d tilde L_ell(h) = 0       if d does not divide k,
Pi_d tilde L_ell(h) may be nonzero if d | k.
```

where `Pi_d` is orthogonal projection onto `H_d`.  Equivalently, a lens whose
output algebra has only `k` quotient classes has zero packet-pure score,
correlation, or recovered energy on every annihilated packet `H_d` with
`d` not dividing `k`.

**Proof.**  By definition, `tilde L_ell(h)` lies in `im(q^*)`, the subspace of
functions factoring through `q`.  T1 identifies this subspace as the span of
exactly the characters in `K^perp`; equivalently, it is orthogonal to every
character outside `K^perp`.  Hence all Fourier/character coefficients outside
`K^perp` vanish for every possible choice of `W_ell`, `b_ell`, and `h`.

For `C_N -> C_k`, T1 further identifies `K^perp` with the characters whose
conductors divide `k`.  The projection onto a packet `H_d` with `d` not
dividing `k` is therefore zero.  Tuning changes the affine map into
`L^2(R_k)`, but the lifted image remains inside `im(q^*)`; it cannot create a
component in an orthogonal packet.  This proves T2.

**Softmax/probability version.**  If the lens scores are converted to
probabilities after lifting, the result is still constant on cosets of `K`.
Thus the centered probability vector also lies in `V_q`, and the same packet
annihilation holds.  If a full-vocabulary lens is later binned through `q`, the
same statement applies to the binned/coarse prediction, not necessarily to the
unbinned vocabulary logits.

**Insights.**

1. The obstruction is about the codomain, not the training quality.  A tuned
   lens can improve calibration, causal faithfulness, and layerwise prediction
   while still having an algebraic blind spot inherited from its target labels.
2. The blind spot is packet-specific.  For `C_12`, a `k = 6` quotient can see
   `H_2`, `H_3`, and `H_6`, but it is forced to miss `H_4` and `H_12`.
3. Bias terms do not help.  The bias is also a quotient function, so after
   lifting it has no coefficients outside `K^perp`.
4. This gives a clean diagnostic for logit-lens failures: compare recovered
   packet energy against the divisor table before attributing failure to bad
   tuning or weak linearity.
5. The corollary does not rule out a full-label tuned lens.  If the lens target
   is the full `R` label algebra, then `k = |R|` and no conductor packet is
   removed by this obstruction.
6. The result also does not rule out hidden states containing the missing
   packet.  It says that a coarse lens cannot read that packet out through its
   quotient target, even if the activation representation contains it.

This is the bridge to Belrose et al.'s Tuned Lens result: the obstruction is
not that a trained lens is useless.  It is that a lens with a coarse output
algebra has a named, packet-level blind spot independent of how well the affine
map is tuned.

### T3. Linear-probe selectivity bound

Let a probe class be control-respecting in the Hewitt-Liang sense: the same
rank/output-algebra restriction is applied to the real task and to a matched
control task.  If the task signal is packet-pure in `H_d` with `d` not dividing
`k`, then every quotient-respecting rank-`k` probe has zero packet signal after
readout.  Under balanced labels and no side-channel correlations, its
selectivity on that packet is zero: the restricted probe cannot beat the
matched control by using the annihilated packet.

This is the probe-builder version of T1.  It says not only that a probe can
fail, but exactly which control tasks become uninformative under a given rank
budget and label algebra.

### PCA/rank-truncation reading

PCA inherits the obstruction only when the truncation is quotient-respecting:
for example, when the data are first binned through `R -> R_k`, or when the
allowed principal subspace is constrained to the quotient subspace.  Then the
retained character packets are again exactly the packets with `d | k`.

Unconstrained PCA is a failure condition, not a corollary.  If a high-conductor
packet dominates variance, ordinary PCA can keep it.  This distinction should
be explicit in the manuscript because it prevents the result from being read as
a blanket claim that every rank-limited linear method loses high-conductor
structure.

### T4. SAE/dictionary prediction

For an SAE or dictionary method whose decoder span is constrained, explicitly
or effectively, to a `k`-resolution quotient subspace, a monosemantic feature
cannot lie wholly in `H_d` when `d` does not divide `k`.  Such a packet can only
appear as residual error, dead directions, or superposed combinations outside
the certified quotient span.

This is stated as a prediction for ordinary learned SAEs, not as a theorem about
all SAE training dynamics.  It becomes a theorem only when the learned
dictionary span is certified to equal, or be contained in, the quotient
subspace.  That certification is part of the missing work below.

### T5. C_5 anchor: KCBS noncontextuality as the rank-1 quotient obstruction

T1 has a closed-form quantum-foundations corollary on `C_5`. The
Klyachko-Can-Binicioğlu-Shumovsky (KCBS) five-cycle scenario consists of
five dichotomic observables `A_0,...,A_4` with cyclic compatibility
`{A_i, A_{i+1 mod 5}}`. The KS noncontextuality inequality

```text
sum_{i=0}^{4} <A_i A_{i+1}> >= -3
```

is the rank-1 quotient bound on `C_5`: every classical (noncontextual) joint
assignment factors through the trivial readout `q_1: C_5 -> C_1`, and T1 with
`N = 5`, `k = 1` gives `P_{q_1} H_d = id` only for `d = 1`. The non-trivial
packet `H_5` is annihilated. Quantum violations live exactly in `H_5`.

**Corollary T5.1 (KCBS as forbidden-packet content).** Under a rank-1 cyclic
quotient readout on `C_5`, the KCBS witness sum decomposes as
`<S>_{H_1} + <S>_{H_5}`. The classical (T1-surviving) component obeys
`<S>_{H_1} >= -3`. Any violation `<S> < -3` is entirely supplied by
`<S>_{H_5}`, the T1-forbidden packet.

**Corollary T5.2 (classical-limit threshold from packet geometry).** For a
qutrit `(d = 3)` state `\rho` with KCBS sum `S_\rho` and the depolarizing
channel `D_p(\rho) = p\rho + (1 - p)\mathbb{1}/3`, the centroid `\mathbb{1}/3`
sits at `S_{\mathbb{1}/3} = -5/3`, strictly inside the classical bound. By
linearity, `S_{D_p(\rho)} = pS_\rho + (1-p)(-5/3)`. Violation persists iff
`pS_\rho + (1-p)(-5/3) < -3`, i.e.,

```text
p > p_crit(\rho) = (-4/3) / (S_\rho + 5/3),
```

where numerator and denominator are both negative for any state with
`S_\rho < -5/3`, so `p_crit(\rho) > 0`. At the Klyachko maximally violating state
`S_\rho = 5 - 4\sqrt{5}`, the threshold is

```text
p_crit^{max} = (5 + 3\sqrt{5}) / 20 \approx 0.5854,
```

reproducing Dutra-Baldijão-Terra Cunha (DBT26) §IV.A. The packet reading
is structural: depolarization scales the `H_5` projection of `\rho` linearly
in `p` while leaving the centroid `H_1` content fixed; `p_crit` is the value
at which the surviving `H_5` magnitude no longer overcomes the centroid's
classical-bound margin `(5/3 - 3 = -4/3)`.

**Remark (state-dependent vs state-independent in T5 language).** The KCBS
mechanism is single-packet: only `H_5` carries the witness, and `H_5` shrinks
proportionally to the state's deviation from the centroid. The Peres-Mermin
square is structurally different in the program: its witness is the
cross-prime carry on `Z/6` (Paper [44](44_quantization_forced_cubic_to_bures_bridge.md);
Paper [32](32_one_functional_four_forces_v0.2%20%282%29.md) §closure
certificate), and depolarization at the preparation alone does not move it;
DBT26 §IV.B recovers PM classicalization only by Heisenberg-side
sequential channel application between the three measurements of each
context, with shrinkage `p^2` from the dual-map step in Paper 26 T1 applied
three times. T5 is the C_5 instance of T1; the Z/6 PM instance lands in
Paper [44](44_quantization_forced_cubic_to_bures_bridge.md).

## Failure Conditions

The obstruction is useful only if its escape hatches are explicit.

1. **Unconstrained supervised probes.**  A full-rank or unconstrained linear
   probe can recover a high-conductor packet if the packet is present and
   predictive.  T1-T3 are about quotient-respecting, rank/output-restricted
   probe classes, not arbitrary supervised probes.
2. **Base-10 or external shortcut mechanisms.**  A model can solve month or
   weekday arithmetic by re-encoding the task through a different internal
   algebra.  "Arithmetic in the Wild" is the strongest current warning: Llama
   can use a generic base-10 addition mechanism for cyclic concepts rather than
   native period-`N` modular addition.
3. **Non-equivariant activation-to-behavior maps.**  If the map from activation
   coordinates to behavior labels does not respect `R`-translation symmetry,
   the character diagonalization need not apply.
4. **Wrong label algebra.**  Tokenization, vocabulary bins, or prompt formats
   can change the effective output algebra.  The theorem applies to the
   algebra actually used by the readout, not the algebra intended by the
   experimenter.
5. **Zero-energy packets.**  If a packet has no empirical energy in the
   behavior being tested, the survival fraction is vacuous.  The local
   certificate uses packet-pure components to avoid this ambiguity.
6. **Arbitrary same-rank subspaces.**  The theorem does not say every rank-`k`
   subspace has divisor behavior.  The random-subspace control is included
   precisely because same rank without quotient structure destroys the step
   function.

## Empirical Reading Rule

The forbidden-packet zeros reported after quotient pullback or quotient binning
are analytic consequences of T1/T2.  They certify that the code implemented the
specified quotient map, but they are not evidence that Llama has a cyclic
conductor representation.  The model-facing evidence is confined to quantities
that could have failed before the projector was applied: native pre-bin packet
fractions, held-out accuracy under packet-blind quotient targets, and controls
that deliberately break quotient alignment.
