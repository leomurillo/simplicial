## Headline

**T8.4 Lemma 6.4 as currently drafted in T8.4 §6.4 is empirically FALSE.**

The matrix
$$\mathcal Q(N,n)_{i,j} := R_{i+j+1}\cdot\mathrm{Re}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle,\qquad 0\le i,j\le n,$$
is **not** positive semidefinite at either $N=30$ or $N=210$ for any $n\ge 1$.

The axis entries themselves are all *positive and monotone increasing* — but a positive monotone sequence is generically **not** the diagonal of a PSD Hankel; in fact, a Hankel matrix built from $(\mu_k)$ is PSD iff $(\mu_k)$ is a positive moment sequence, and the sequence $R_k\langle h_k,\chi_1\rangle$ has no such Stieltjes structure on its own.

## Diagnosis — what T8.4 §6.4 got wrong

The argument in T8.4 §6.4 made an over-simplification: after the multiplicative selection rule collapses the joint prime cumulant to the principal coset $m\equiv 1\bmod N$, I claimed that $\chi_{m^{-1}}=\chi_1$ on the support, and that the resulting kernel factor $\sum_{i,j}v_iv_jR_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle$ has a "Stieltjes-positive structure" via a deferred representation.

This collapse is too crude. On the principal coset $m\equiv 1\bmod N$, $m$ takes infinitely many values ($m=1,N+1,2N+1,\ldots$), and the *correct* T9.4 Cor 5.1 sum runs over *all* $m$ coprime to $N$ with $\Lambda(m)$ weight and $\chi_{m^{-1}}$ varying through all residues coprime to $N$ — not just $\chi_1$. The multiplicative selection rule selects the principal *character* (summing $\sum_\chi\chi(m)=\varphi(N)\mathbf 1_{m\equiv 1\bmod N}$), but the *residue index* $m^{-1}$ in T9.4 Cor 5.1 corresponds to a different aggregation that does not collapse to a single $c=1$ Helmert-Fourier evaluation.

The right picture: the kernel $K_v(m,N)$ in T8.4 Theorem D is a sum over the Helmert-Fourier residues $\chi_{m^{-1}}$ as $m$ varies through *all* residues coprime to $N$ (not the principal coset only), with the von Mangoldt weight $\Lambda(m)$ selecting prime powers. The Stieltjes-positivity question is then a *bona fide* additive-Fourier–weighted sum, not a quadratic form in a single direction. My sketch in §6.4 conflated these two distinct aggregations.

## Where the real positivity must come from

The empirical signal is sharp ($J^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(210)\succeq 0$ with margin $\sim 0.84$). The structural mechanism (signed-measure split) under RH/GRH$_N$ proves the result *if* the moment-cumulant identification holds. The analytical work that T8.4 was supposed to do — make the structural mechanism explicit without invoking RH/GRH$_N$ — must come from elsewhere:

1. **Full additive-Fourier Stieltjes representation.** The kernel $K_v(m,N)$ has to be analyzed as a sum over $m$, not factored at the principal coset. The natural object is the additive-Fourier transform of $\Lambda\cdot\mathbf 1_{(\mathbb Z/N)^\times}\cdot w_{\mathrm{shift}}$, paired with the Helmert kernel.

2. **Direct moment-cumulant verification.** Under RH/GRH$_N$, $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ is the positive atomic measure on the zero atoms; the shifted-Hankel form is then directly $\int s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma\ge 0$ without going through the explicit kernel. T8.4 §3.3 already gives this argument cleanly. Theorem E follows from Theorem A alone (Corollary 3.3), without needing Lemma 6.4.

In other words: **Theorem E is provable conditional on RH/GRH$_N$ by ArchStruct §7's argument formalized as T8.4 Theorem A + Corollary 3.3. The detour through Lemma 6.4 was unnecessary, and was wrong.**
