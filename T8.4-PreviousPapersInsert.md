## 5. Lemma 6.4 (kernel positivity on principal coset) — proof sketched, full Stieltjes representation deferred

**Observation (substantive — honest framing).** T8.4 Lemma 6.4 claims that the matrix $(\mathcal Q)_{i,j}=(R_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle)_{i,j=0,\ldots,n}$ is PSD for primorial $N\ge 30$. This is a real gap: Lemma 6.4 as stated is a structural claim whose full proof requires identifying the positive atomic measure $\rho_N^{\mathrm{Helm}}$ on $\{c\in(\mathbb Z/N)\setminus\{0\}\}$ that has $(\mathcal Q)$ as its shifted Hankel. T9 Theorem 2.1's rational-polynomial form at $c=1$ is suggestive but does not immediately yield the Hankel-positive structure; the full proof needs the additive-character Stieltjes lift, which is a clean computation but not yet written down. T8.4 §6.4 sketches the argument and §7.1 invokes Lemma 6.4 — but the lemma is technically *open as written*.

**Honest framing.** Theorem E's proof in §7.2 relies on Lemma 6.4. The proof of Lemma 6.4 is sketched, not full. The empirical observation at $N=210$, $n\le 14$ is consistent with Lemma 6.4 holding at the tested rungs. A direct numerical check of the matrix $(\mathcal Q)$ at $N=30,210$ for $n\le 14$ is a 5-line computation in mpmath; this should be done before T8.4 is locked. Adding this as a probe target:
