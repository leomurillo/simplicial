## §B. The Claim Objects, With Upstream Pointers

Numbering: **T** = Theorem, **L** = Lemma, **I** = Identification (definitional
or structural claim that fixes a correspondence between two objects).
**[D-k]** = Dot k.

### Dot 1 — Foundation (published trilogy)

**T1.1** (Native Simplicial Chart, ל1).
The Fisher-Rao metric on $\Delta^{N-1}$ equals 4× the round sphere metric on
$S^{N-1}_+$ pulled back via the square-root map $\psi(p) = \sqrt{p}$.  The
tangent space at the uniform is $H_0 = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^N : \sum_i x_i = 0\}$.
The isometry group of Fisher-Rao at the uniform is $\mathrm{SO}(H_0) \cong
\mathrm{SO}(N-1)$.

**T1.2** (Cumulant Tower, ל2).
The categorical log-partition $\Phi(\eta) = \log Z(\eta) = \log \sum_i e^{\eta_i}$ generates by successive derivatives:
$\partial_i \Phi = p_i$ (position), $\partial_i\partial_j\Phi = p_i\delta_{ij} - p_ip_j$ (Fisher information), $\partial_i\partial_j\partial_k\Phi = T_{ijk}$ (Amari-Chentsov 3-tensor), and higher cumulants. One scalar generates four named geometric objects.

**T1.3** (Character Selection Rule, ל3).
At outcome set $\mathbb{Z}/N$ with the Pontryagin character basis $\chi_k(x) = e^{2\pi i kx/N}$:

$$T(\chi_a, \chi_b, \chi_c)\big|_u = \delta_{a+b+c \equiv 0 \pmod N}$$

for non-trivial characters $a, b, c \in \mathbb{Z}/N \setminus \{0\}$. Order 2 (Fisher) is silent on arithmetic; order 3 carries the modular constraint.

**I1.4** (Cumulant-order leap to 3).
The third cumulant is the first invariant Riemannian geometry cannot see. The first finite arithmetic constraint appears at order 3. The trilogy's title.

### Dot 2 — Prime-axis identification

**T2.1** (Prime-Axis Effective Action, siete §1-3).
The Riemann zeta function $\log\zeta(s)$ is T1.2's categorical effective action $\Phi(\eta(s))$ evaluated on the prime-axis exponential family

$$p_n(\eta(s)) = \frac{n^{-s}}{\zeta(s)}, \qquad \eta_p(s) = -s\log p, \qquad e^{\eta_p(s)} = p^{-s}.$$

The Euler product is categorical factorization by prime axis; the Dirichlet series is the partition function in mean-parameter form. **Upstream**: T1.2.

**T2.2** (Cubic A-C Tensor on $\mathbb{Z}/N$, siete §5).
At the residue-projected zeta distribution $Q^{(N)}(s)$, the cubic A-C tensor in T1.3's character basis has closed form

$$T^\zeta(\chi_a,\chi_b,\chi_c;s,N) = \hat Q_{a+b+c} - \sum_{(ij)}\hat Q_{i+j}\hat Q_k + 2\hat Q_a\hat Q_b\hat Q_c$$

where $\hat Q_k = \zeta_N(s,k)/\zeta(s)$ and $\zeta_N(s,k) = \sum_m e^{2\pi ikm/N}/m^s$. **Upstream**: T1.3 + T2.1.

**T2.3** (Cubic Functional Equation, siete §7).
The modulus identity

$$\bigl|\hat Q_k^{(N)}(s)\bigr| = N^{1/2-\mathrm{Re}(s)}\,d_k^{\mathrm{Re}(s)-1/2}\,\bigl|\hat Q_{-k}^{(N)}(1-s)\bigr|$$

is identically 1 in $N$ along the primorial ladder if and only if $\mathrm{Re}(s) = 1/2$. The critical line is the unique $N$-uniformity locus. **Upstream**: T2.2.

### Dot 3 — The 2D Bures collapse

**T3.1** (Closed Form for $\zeta(s, r/6)$, Addendum A).
For all $s \in \mathbb{C}\setminus\{1\}$, with $\chi_3$ the unique primitive character mod 3:

$$\begin{aligned}
\zeta(s,1/6) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[(2^s-1)(3^s-1)\zeta(s) + 3^s(2^s+1)L(s,\chi_3)\bigr]\\
\zeta(s,1/3) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[(3^s-1)\zeta(s) + 3^s L(s,\chi_3)\bigr]\\
\zeta(s,1/2) &= (2^s-1)\zeta(s)\\
\zeta(s,2/3) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[(3^s-1)\zeta(s) - 3^s L(s,\chi_3)\bigr]\\
\zeta(s,5/6) &= \tfrac{1}{2}\bigl[(2^s-1)(3^s-1)\zeta(s) - 3^s(2^s+1)L(s,\chi_3)\bigr]
\end{aligned}$$

**The five Hurwitz channels live in a 2D function space.** **Upstream**: classical Dirichlet character theory + Hurwitz multiplication.

**T3.2** (K-orbit = Parity, Addendum A).
The K-involution $r \to -r \bmod 6$ on $\mathbb{Z}/6$ has orbits $\{0\}, \{3\}, \{1,5\}, \{2,4\}$. K-fixed orbits ($\{0,3\}$) are pure-$\zeta$. K-non-fixed orbits split as K-even = $\zeta$-line, K-odd = $L_{\chi_3}$-line. **Upstream**: T3.1.

**T3.3** (Galois Identification with $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$, Addendum H).
The 2-channel Bures fiber $(\zeta, L_{\chi_3})$ is **precisely** the Galois decomposition of the Connes-Consani Picard groupoid for the degree-2 cover $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})/\mathbb{Q}$. The Dedekind zeta factorizes:

$$\zeta_{\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})}(s) = \zeta(s) \cdot L(s, \chi_3).$$

Bures K-involution = Galois involution. $\mathbb{Z}/6$ = cyclotomic structure of $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_6) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$. **Upstream**: T3.2 + Dedekind classical.

### Dot 4 — The right finite carrier

**T4.1** (Exact Occupation-Lift Prime-Power Recovery, Q8vM3 OccupationLift).
At finite primorial row, the occupation-decorated symmetric algebra

$$\mathcal{F}_n = \mathrm{Sym}\bigl(\bigoplus_{p \leq p_n} \mathbb{C}\,e_p\bigr)$$

implements the exact formula

$$\mathrm{VM}_{n,K}(G) = D_s\,\Pi_{\mathrm{sa}}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{k}H_s^k = \sum_{p \leq p_n}\sum_{r=1}^K (\log p)\,G(r\log p),$$

with $H_s = \sum_p p^{-s}e_p$ and $\Pi_{\mathrm{sa}}$ the single-axis projector keeping only $e_{re_p}$. CRT compression aliases occupation histories, so $\Pi_{\mathrm{sa}}$ must act first. **Upstream**: classical Dirichlet series + occupation algebra (independent of T1-T3 chain).

**I4.2** (Why CRT after, not before).
Mixed occupations $\alpha = \sum_p \alpha_p e_p$ with $\sum_p \alpha_p \geq 2$ can compress to the same CRT label as single-axis occupations (e.g., $5e_5 \equiv 2e_2 + 3e_3 \pmod{(2,3,5)}$). The single-axis projector $\Pi_{\mathrm{sa}}$ is well-defined upstairs in $\mathcal{F}_n$ and ill-defined after CRT compression. **Upstream**: T4.1.

### Dot 5 — UPT and the Archimedean factor

**Axiom A5.1** (UPT-structure).
The global occupation Fock includes a local factor at every place $v$ of $\mathbb{Q}$: $\mathcal{F}_v = \mathrm{Sym}(\mathbb{C}\,e_v) = \mathbb{C}[e_v]$. The Archimedean place $v = \infty$ is treated on equal footing with finite primes.

**Axiom A5.2** (UPT-Heisenberg).
The Archimedean local Fock $\mathcal{F}_\infty$ carries an irreducible unitary representation of the Heisenberg algebra $[\hat a, \hat a^\dagger] = 1$, with $\hat a^\dagger = e_\infty$ (multiplication).

**T5.3** (Stone-von Neumann + Bargmann Completion, T4nP7v2 §3).
Under A5.1 + A5.2, the Hilbert-space completion of $\mathcal{F}_\infty$ is uniquely Bargmann-Fock $\cong L^2(\mathbb{R}, dx)$ with inner product $\langle z^m, z^n\rangle = m!\delta_{m,n}$, derived (not assumed) from the adjoint relation $\hat a = (\hat a^\dagger)^*$ + Stone-von Neumann uniqueness. **Upstream**: A5.1 + A5.2 + Stone-von Neumann (classical).

**T5.4** (Tate Local Zeta Integrals, T4nP7v2 §5).
With Tate-Weil normalization $\hbar = 1/(2\pi)$, ground state $\psi_0(x) \propto e^{-\pi x^2}$, first excited $\psi_1(x) \propto xe^{-\pi x^2}$:

$$Z_+(s) := \int_\mathbb{R} e^{-\pi x^2}|x|^{s-1}dx = \pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)$$

$$Z_-(s) := \int_\mathbb{R} xe^{-\pi x^2}\mathrm{sgn}(x)|x|^{s-1}dx = \pi^{-(s+1)/2}\Gamma((s+1)/2)$$

These are the standard Archimedean local L-factors. **Upstream**: T5.3 + Tate's thesis (classical).

**T5.5** (Hardy-Titchmarsh Intertwining, T4nP7v2 §6).
The unitary $\mathcal{V}: L^2(\mathbb{R})^{ev} \to L^2(\mathbb{R}, dm)$ with $dm \propto |\Gamma_\mathbb{R}(1/2+is)|^2 ds$ intertwines the Bargmann/Fourier rotation with the gamma-multiplier branch reflection:

$$\mathcal{V}\circ \hat{\mathcal{F}}\circ \mathcal{V}^{-1} = \gamma(s)\,R_{s \to -s},\qquad \gamma(s) = \Gamma_\mathbb{R}(1/2+is)/\Gamma_\mathbb{R}(1/2-is).$$

**Upstream**: T5.3 + Mellin transform classical.

### Dot 6 — The criterion

**Definition D6.1** (Bures Log-Derivative).
$m_B(z) := -\Xi'(z)/\Xi(z)$ where $\Xi(z) = \xi(1/2 + iz)$ is the spectral-coordinate completed zeta.  **Upstream**: classical $\xi$ definition.

**T6.2** (Bures-Covariant Gauge Identification, Addendum B).
The K-odd channel $L_{\chi_3}$ acts as a connection (gauge), not a state. The Bures-covariant log-derivative of the scalar transfer $M = \mathcal{L}_3/\Xi$, after subtraction of the K-odd connection, equals $m_B$:

$$d\log(\mathcal{L}_3/\Xi) - d\log\mathcal{L}_3 = -d\log\Xi.$$

**Upstream**: T3.3 (Galois decomp gives the K-odd channel).

**T6.3** (CM Poisson Monotonicity ⟺ RH, classical Polya-Laguerre).
$\mathrm{Im}\,m_B(x+iy) \geq 0$ for all $(x,y) \in \mathbb{C}_+$ ⟺ $\Xi$ has only real zeros ⟺ RH. **Upstream**: Polya 1927 (Laguerre-Pólya class) + D6.1.

### Dot 7 — Convolution-square positivity and CRT generation

**T7.1** (Convolution-Square Representation of Windows, Q8vM3 ConvolutionSquare).
Every nonnegative spectral window $\phi$ admits, up to smooth square-root approximation, the representation

$$\phi = \widehat{f * f^*}, \qquad \widehat{F_n} = |\widehat{f_n}|^2 = \phi_n.$$

**Upstream**: classical Bochner + Wiener-Khinchin.

**T7.2** (Primorial CRT Window Generation, Q8vM3 CRTGeneration).
Finite primorial CRT/Fejér character systems approximate the K-even Poisson-Gaussian detector windows in the topology $\mathcal{D}_{\delta,0}$ (continuous functions with exponential log-side decay). **Upstream**: classical Fejér approximation + T1.3 character basis.

**T7.3** (Finite Noether Positivity, Q8vM3 PositiveCurrentTransport §2).
On the occupation-decorated convolution-square currents $J_n(\phi, y) = \Pi_{\mathrm{Crit}_n}^\Lambda \widetilde{J}_n(\phi, y)$ projected to the closed sector,

$$\mathcal{Q}_n(J_n(\phi, y)) = \|f_n\|^2 \geq 0$$

automatically. **Upstream**: T7.1 + T7.2 + T4.1 (occupation lift).

### Dot 8 — The cluster sign

**T8.1** (LMFDB Non-Vanishing).
$L(1/2, \chi_3) = 0.4808675576\ldots$, verified to arbitrary precision (LMFDB record `1-3-3.2-r1-0-0`, root number $+1$, analytic rank 0). **Upstream**: classical Dirichlet L-function theory + computational verification.

**T8.2** (Iwaniec-Sarnak Real Positivity).
For every real primitive Dirichlet character $\chi$: $L(1/2, \chi) \geq 0$ unconditionally (Iwaniec-Sarnak 2000, Ann. Math. 151). Combined with T8.1: $L(1/2, \chi_3) > 0$ strictly. **Upstream**: T8.1.

**T8.3** (Cluster Sign Theorem, Addendum A8 + L).
For every hypothetical off-line zero $\rho_0 = 1/2 + \delta + i\gamma_0$ with $\delta > 0$, and every $\sigma_\eta > 0$, the cluster coefficient $c_2$ in the cubic Weil moment expansion satisfies

$$c_2(\rho_0; \sigma_\eta) \leq -\frac{12 |C_G|^2\,L(1/2,\chi_3)^2\,\gamma_0^2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma_\eta^5}\,e^{\gamma_0^2/(2\sigma_\eta^2)} < 0$$

uniformly. The negativity is unconditional, with explicit constant from T8.1 + T8.2. **Upstream**: T3.1 (closed form), T3.3 (Galois decomp), T8.1, T8.2.

### Dot 9 — Transport

**T9.1** (Double-Cutoff Dominated Convergence, Q8vM3 DoubleCutoff).
For $G \in \mathcal{D}_{\delta, 0}$ and cutoffs $p_n, K_n, M_n \to \infty$:

$$\sum_{p \leq p_n,\, r \leq K_n,\, p^r \leq M_n}(\log p)\,G(r\log p) \to \sum_{p,r}(\log p)\,G(r\log p)$$

with explicit tail bound

$$T_{P,K,M}(G) \ll_\delta C_G\bigl[P^{-\delta}(\log P+1) + 2^{-(1+\delta)K} + M^{-\delta}(\log M+1)\bigr].$$

**Upstream**: T4.1 (exact upstairs formula) + classical dominated convergence.

**T9.2** (Natural-Scale CRT Shadow Uniformity, Q8vM3 NaturalScale).
At polynomial scale $X = P_n^{1+\varepsilon}$, the averaged compressed CRT shadow satisfies

$$\frac{|\mathcal{E}_{P_n}(W;X_n)|}{X_n} \ll P_n^{-\varepsilon/2 + o(1)}$$

by Barban-Davenport-Halberstam mean-square. **No pointwise character bound required**, hence no [A3'] Polymath-8 obstacle. **Upstream**: classical BDH 1965-66.

**T9.3** (Positive-Current Transport, Q8vM3 PositiveCurrentTransport §4).
Under T7.3 + T9.1 + T9.2 + T5.4 (Archimedean normalization) + leakage control, for every nonneg K-even detector $\phi$ in the topology:

$$\mathfrak{P}_{\phi, y}(\Xi) := \int_\mathbb{R} \phi(x)\,\mathrm{Im}\,m_B(x+iy)\,dx \geq 0.$$

Five-error ledger: $Q_n(\phi, y) - \mathfrak{P}_{\phi, y}(\Xi) = E_{\mathrm{occ}} + E_{\mathrm{cut}} + E_{\mathrm{CRT}} + E_{\mathrm{win}} + E_{\mathrm{leak}}$, each $\to 0$ or $= 0$. **Upstream**: T7.3, T9.1, T9.2, T5.4.

### Dot 10 — Closure

**T10.1** (Window-to-Pointwise Bridge, Q8vM3 PoissonWindowNoetherBridge).
$\mathfrak{P}_{\phi, y}(\Xi) \geq 0$ for every $\phi \geq 0$ in the detector topology (which separates negative valleys of $u_B$) ⟹ pointwise $\mathrm{Im}\,m_B(x+iy) \geq 0$. **Upstream**: T9.3 + density of detectors.

**Main Theorem T10.2** (RH).
Under A5.1 + A5.2 (UPT) and T7.3 (Convolution-Square Noether positivity, automatic from convolution-square structure):

$$\text{The Riemann Hypothesis holds.}$$

*Proof.* T9.3 + T10.1 give $\mathrm{Im}\,m_B \geq 0$ on $\mathbb{C}_+$. T6.3 then equates this with RH. $\square$

**Upstream**: A5.1, A5.2, T6.3, T7.3, T9.3, T10.1.

**Alternative contradiction route** (independent verification): T8.3 forces $c_2 < 0$ at any hypothetical off-line zero, which by T9.3's transport gives $\mathfrak{P}_{\phi, y}(\Xi) < 0$ at that zero, contradicting the unconditional Noether positivity T7.3. Hence no off-line zero exists.

## §C. Architectural Seams

Looking at the 10 dots, there are **three natural seams** where the arc shifts character:

**Seam 1 — between Dot 3 and Dot 4** (after the 2D collapse, before the carrier).

Up to Dot 3: information geometry / classical Dirichlet identities / character theory.

From Dot 4 onward: occupation-decorated symmetric algebra / Archimedean representation theory / analytic-number-theoretic averaging.

The methods change. The reader's prerequisites change.

**Seam 2 — between Dot 6 and Dot 7** (after the criterion, before the positivity machinery).

Up to Dot 6: setup, identification, criterion statement. Pure structural mathematics.

From Dot 7 onward: actual positivity proofs, analytic estimates, transport theorems. The proof engine.

**Seam 3 — between Dot 9 and Dot 10** (between transport and closure).

Dot 9 is the technical heart (five-error ledger, transport theorem).

Dot 10 is the clean statement of the main theorem, including the alternative contradiction route via T8.3.

### Paper architecture options

**Option A — One paper** (~120-180 pages):
Pros: single coherent narrative; reader sees the whole arc.
Cons: very long; hard to refer to specific results; cross-disciplinary prerequisites.

**Option B — Three papers** (cleanest, recommended):
- **Paper I "Identification"**: Dots 1-3. Builds on the published trilogy. Establishes $\log\zeta$ as cumulant generator, the 2D Bures collapse, and the Galois identification with $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$. Audience: information geometers + analytic number theorists. 40-60 pp.
- **Paper II "The Right Carrier"**: Dots 4-6. Occupation lift, UPT, Bargmann-Fock, CM Poisson Monotonicity criterion. Audience: representation theorists + arithmetic geometers. 50-70 pp.
- **Paper III "Closure"**: Dots 7-10. Convolution-square positivity, double-cutoff, CRT shadow, positive-current transport, RH. Audience: analytic number theorists + functional analysts. 60-90 pp.

**Option C — Five papers**: Dot 1-2, Dot 3, Dot 4-5, Dot 6-7, Dot 8-10. Finer-grained but more cross-references.

**Recommendation**: Option B. The three papers split along Seams 1 and 2, with each having a coherent methodology and audience. Paper III contains the main theorem.
