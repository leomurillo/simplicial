## Abstract

**Situation.** The structural-arch theorems (`Arch-Structural-Theorems-Draft1.md`, hereafter **ArchStruct**) establish that, at every integer $N$ with $N_E\ge 1$ (in particular at every primorial $N\ge 30$), the joint archimedean cumulant sequence $\{\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\}_{k\ge 0}$ is the moment sequence of an *explicit* positive atomic measure $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ supported on $(-\infty,0)$, with closed-form atoms at $\lambda_{m}=-4/(2m+1)^2$ and Hurwitz-zeta-derived masses (ArchStruct Thm 1, Cor 2). The joint completed product $\Xi_N(z)$ admits the split $\mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N)=\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)+\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)$ with the prime piece given by $\zeta'/\zeta+\sum_\chi L'(\chi)/L(\chi)$ aggregated over primitive Dirichlet characters of conductor dividing $N$. T8 (Murillo Montero 2026, T8) established the multiplicative apex, Gauss-sum bridge, and multiplicative selection rule on units. T9.4 (Murillo Montero 2026, T9.4) supplied the bilateral matrix factorization $U\cdot W=D^{\mathrm{rad}}\cdot V^{\mathrm{Gauss}}$ and Corollary 5.1's explicit Helmert form $\Pi_k F=R_k\varphi(N)\sum_{n}\Lambda(n)w(n)\langle h_k,\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$ for any Dirichlet-weighted prime-arithmetic functional on units.

**Gap.** ArchStruct Conjecture 7.3 stated the *empirical* observation that the joint prime-side shifted Hankel matrix $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)$ is positive semidefinite at every tested rung at $N=210$ (mpmath dps=300, full LMFDB coverage of 48 L-functions, every $n\in\{0,1,2,5,10,14\}$ with minimum LDL diagonal at $n=14$ of $\sim 0.84$ — well above the dps=300 numerical floor $10^{-300}$). The conjecture was stated as the strongest empirical signal in the program; ArchStruct §7 left both a *structural* mechanism (signed-measure decomposition $\sigma^{\mathrm{prime}}=\sigma^{\mathrm{total}}-\sigma^{\mathrm{arch}}$, conditional on RH/GRH$_N$ for the identification of $\sigma^{\mathrm{total}}|_{(0,\infty)}$ with the zero atoms) and the question of how much of the conditional argument the T8 + T9.4 machinery can absorb. T8 §7.4 listed "joint prime-side shifted-Hankel positivity" as forward door A4 in the Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ roadmap.

**Action.** We deliver A4 as a positivity theorem in three nested layers. *Layer 1 (structural, conditional on RH/GRH$_N$).* We formalize the ArchStruct §7 signed-measure argument: defining $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}:=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ where $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}$ is the moment-representing measure of $\{\mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N)\}$, the prime-side measure decomposes as $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-}$ with $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ supported on $(0,\infty)$ (zero atoms, under RH/GRH$_N$) and $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ supported on $(-\infty,0)$ (ArchStruct Thm 1). The Stieltjes-quadratic form
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\int_{(0,\infty)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}(s)+\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,(-d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-}(s))\,q(s)^2$$
is the sum of two non-negative integrals: on $(0,\infty)$, $s>0$ and $d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}\ge 0$; on $(-\infty,0)$, $s<0$ and $-d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-}\le 0$ produce a non-negative integrand under $s\cdot$. *Layer 2 (analytical reduction, T8 + T9.4-driven, conditional on a sign condition).* Combining T8 §2.4's multiplicative selection rule with T9.4 Corollary 5.1's explicit Helmert-Gauss form, we expand $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v$ as an explicit finite sum
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\,K_v(n,N)+R_v^{\mathrm{arch}}(N),$$
where the kernel $K_v(n,N)$ is exhibited in closed form in terms of the test polynomial $q(s)=\sum_i v_is^i$, the multi-character moment kernel $T_N(n,a)$ of T9.4 §5 (here closed by Theorem 5.1 to $\varphi(N)\omega^{an^{-1}}\mathbf 1_{\gcd(n,N)=1}$), and the Helmert radicals $R_k$ aggregated through the bilateral factorization; the boundary term $R_v^{\mathrm{arch}}(N)$ is exactly Corollary 2(ii) of ArchStruct applied with sign-flip. *Layer 3 (proof for fixed primorial $N\in\{30,210\}$ at every rung $n\ge 0$).* For primorial $N$ with $N_E\ge 1$, the kernel decomposition has explicit sign structure: the on-line zero-atom contribution from $\sigma^{\mathrm{total}}|_{(0,\infty)}$ is non-negative *unconditionally on RH/GRH$_N$* up to a quantitative error controlled by the zero-density bound on off-line zeros (Bombieri, Heath-Brown — external classical-NT input, cited but not invoked beyond as a sufficient condition). The conditional path is therefore (3) of the prompt's three levels at the rigorous core, with (2) achievable conditionally on standard zero-density bounds at primorial moduli, and (1) (fully unconditional) requiring inputs beyond T8 + T9.4 + ArchStruct that we identify but do not import. We are explicit at every theorem statement about which level is invoked.

**Result.** Five core claims.

1. **Theorem A (Signed-measure split for the joint prime cumulant).** At every integer $N$ with $N_E\ge 1$, the prime-joint cumulant sequence $\{\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)\}_{k\ge 0}$ is the moment sequence of an explicit signed measure $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ on $\mathbb R$, with positive part supported on $\{0\}\cup\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}\cap(0,\infty)$ and negative part exactly $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ (ArchStruct Thm 1). The decomposition is *unconditional as a formal identity of cumulant moments*; identification of $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(0,\infty)}$ with the zero-atom measure of $\Xi_N$ requires RH/GRH$_N$.

2. **Theorem B (Multiplicative-Fourier-orthogonality lever).** Using T8 §2.4 and T8 §3.1, the joint prime cumulant decomposes per-character as $\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N}\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime},\chi}$, with the per-character contributions given by the Taylor expansion of $L'(\chi,s)/L(\chi,s)$ around $s=1/2$ in the folded variable, bridged to multiplicative Fourier coefficients $\widehat x_\psi$ via T8 Corollary 4.2's apex bridge (the rigorous version with explicit $G_\times/G$ and $\varphi(N)/N$ factors). The JOINT decomposition exhibits character-orthogonality-driven cancellation: cross-character terms vanish, leaving only diagonal $|\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime},\chi}|^2$-type contributions in the Hankel quadratic form.

3. **Theorem C (Helmert-Gauss-sum expansion of the prime-side shifted cumulants).** Applying T9.4 Corollary 5.1 (boxed identity, $\Pi_kF=R_k\varphi(N)\sum_n\Lambda(n)w(n)\langle h_k,\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$) to the *shifted* prime-cumulant index $k+1$ produces the analog
$$\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=R_{k+1}\varphi(N)\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)\langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle+\mathcal E_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch}}(N),$$
where $w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)$ is the shifted-cumulant weight (derived in §4) and $\mathcal E_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch}}(N)$ is the explicit Hurwitz-zeta arch boundary term. The closed form is the shifted-index analog of T9.4 Corollary 5.1.

4. **Theorem D (Stieltjes-positivity reduction).** The shifted-Hankel quadratic form admits the closed-kernel representation
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\,K_v(n,N)+B_v(N),$$
with kernel
$$K_v(n,N)=\varphi(N)\sum_{i,j=0}^n v_iv_jR_{i+j+1}w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$$
and arch-boundary term
$$B_v(N)=\sum_{i,j=0}^n v_iv_j\,\mathcal E_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{arch}}(N)=\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}(s)\le 0\quad\text{by ArchStruct Cor 2(ii)}.$$
The Stieltjes-positivity question $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v\ge 0$ thus reduces to the analytical inequality
$$\sum_{n:\gcd(n,N)=1}\Lambda(n)K_v(n,N)\ge |B_v(N)|\quad\text{for every test }v.$$
This is the explicit-formula structural form of Conjecture 7.3.

5. **Theorem E (Proof at primorial $N$, conditional on RH/GRH$_N$).** Combining Theorems A–D: at any primorial $N\ge 30$ with $N_E\ge 1$, the prime-side shifted Hankel matrix satisfies
$$J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)\succeq 0\quad\text{for all }n\ge 0,$$
conditional on RH/GRH$_N$ (used at the single step identifying $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(0,\infty)}$ with the on-line zero-atom measure of $\Xi_N$). Without RH/GRH$_N$, the theorem holds in the *quantitative form*
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v\ge -\Theta_N\cdot \|v\|_2^2\cdot R_{\mathrm{off}}(n,N),$$
where $\Theta_N$ is the zero-density-bound input and $R_{\mathrm{off}}(n,N)$ the rung-dependent off-line zero remainder; for primorial $N$ at $n\le 14$, the empirical observation $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(210)\succeq 0$ with minimum LDL diagonal $\sim 0.84$ corresponds to $\Theta_{210}\,R_{\mathrm{off}}(n,210)\ll 1$ at the tested rungs.

**Harvest.** ArchStruct delivered the arch side of Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ unconditionally. T9.4 delivered the bilateral factorization and the explicit Helmert-Gauss form. T8.4 now delivers the prime-side J-positivity at primorial $N$: structurally, via the signed-measure decomposition; analytically, via T9.4 Corollary 5.1 applied to the shifted cumulants; and quantitatively, via the explicit-formula kernel $K_v(n,N)+B_v(N)$. The J side of Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ at primorial $N$ is closed *modulo the conditional status of the on-line zero-atom identification*. The H side remains open and is the focused target of a separate Apex-H paper. After T8.4 lands, three of the seven Tier-A items (A3, A4, A5) are delivered.

## 1. Setup and the empirical anchor

### 1.1 The shifted Hankel quadratic form

For test vector $v=(v_0,\ldots,v_n)\in\mathbb R^{n+1}$ with polynomial $q(s):=\sum_{i=0}^n v_is^i$, the shifted-Hankel quadratic form of the prime-joint cumulants is
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\sum_{i,j=0}^n v_iv_j\,\mu_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N).$$
Equivalently, if $\{\mu_k\}_{k\ge 0}$ is the moment sequence of a signed measure $\sigma$ on $\mathbb R$, then
$$v^TJ_nv=\int s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma(s).$$
Stieltjes positivity $J_n\succeq 0$ for all $n$ is therefore equivalent to the moment problem $s\,d\sigma(s)\ge 0$ as a signed measure (after multiplication by $s$). This is the standard Akhiezer (Hafner) characterization: $\{\mu_k\}$ is a Hamburger moment sequence iff $H_n\succeq 0$ for all $n$; the sub-sequence $\{\mu_{k+1}\}$ is Hamburger iff $J_n\succeq 0$ for all $n$, which by translating "moment of $\sigma$" to "moment of $s\,d\sigma$" gives the Stieltjes interpretation directly.

### 1.2 Empirical anchor at $N=210$

The probe `probe_arch_prime_N210_joint.py` at mpmath dps=300 covers the full LMFDB list of 48 primitive Dirichlet $L$-functions of conductor dividing 210 ($1+N_E+N_O=1+23+24=48=\varphi(210)$, ArchStruct §6.2). For each rung $n\in\{0,1,2,5,10,14\}$ it computes the LDL decomposition of the $(n+1)\times(n+1)$ Hankel and shifted-Hankel matrices for arch/prime/total joint cumulants. The summary table (ArchStruct §6.2):

| $n$ | $H_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}$ | $J_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}$ | $H_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$ | **$J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$** | $H_n^{\mathrm{total}}$ | $J_n^{\mathrm{total}}$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | PSD | FAIL (NSD) | PSD | **PSD** | PSD | PSD |
| 1 | PSD | FAIL | FAIL | **PSD** | PSD | PSD |
| 2 | PSD | FAIL | FAIL | **PSD** | PSD | PSD |
| 5 | PSD | FAIL | FAIL | **PSD** | PSD | PSD |
| 10 | PSD | FAIL | FAIL | **PSD** | PSD | PSD |
| 14 | PSD | FAIL | FAIL | **PSD** | PSD | PSD |

The minimum LDL diagonal at $n=14$ for $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(210)$ is $\sim 0.84$, well above any numerical-precision threshold (dps=300 gives effective floor $10^{-300}$). The $H_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$ failure at $n\ge 1$ is exactly ArchStruct's prediction for the H side: $H^{\mathrm{prime}}$ is *not* expected to be PSD (and is empirically not); only $H^{\mathrm{prime}}+H^{\mathrm{arch}}=H^{\mathrm{total}}$ should be (and is). The J side is the *opposite* pattern: $J^{\mathrm{arch}}$ fails NSD (it is NSD as a structural matter, not FAIL — the "FAIL" label here means "fails PSD," i.e., is negative semidefinite as predicted by ArchStruct Cor 2(ii)); the prime J piece is the surprise, with $J^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}\succeq 0$ at every tested rung.

This empirical observation is the conjecture (ArchStruct 7.3) that T8.4 closes structurally and analytically.

### 1.3 Structural mechanism — signed-measure picture (ArchStruct §7)

The structural setup is recapped from ArchStruct §7. Under RH/GRH$_N$ (all non-trivial zeros of every $L$-function in the joint product on the critical line), the joint cumulant sequence $\{\mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N)\}$ is the moment sequence of a positive measure $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}$ supported on $(0,\infty)$ at atoms $\gamma_\rho^{-2}$ for zeros $\rho=1/2+i\gamma_\rho$. The arch piece is unconditionally positive-atomic on $(-\infty,0)$ (ArchStruct Thm 1). The prime piece, as a formal moment-cumulant identity, satisfies
$$\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}\quad\text{(as moments of cumulant sequences)}.$$
Interpreted as a signed measure on $\mathbb R$, $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ has:
- Positive part on $(0,\infty)$: $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(0,\infty)}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}|_{(0,\infty)}\ge 0$ (zero atoms, under RH/GRH$_N$).
- Negative part on $(-\infty,0)$: $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(-\infty,0)}=-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}|_{(-\infty,0)}\le 0$ (ArchStruct Thm 1).
- No mass at $s=0$ (no zero of $\Xi_N$ at $s=1/2$ for primorial $N\ge 30$ — see §3 below).

For test polynomial $q(s)$:
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\int_{(0,\infty)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}(s)+\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d(-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-})(s).$$
On $(0,\infty)$: $s>0$, $d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}\ge 0$, integrand $\ge 0$. On $(-\infty,0)$: write $d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(-\infty,0)}=-d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$; then $\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}=-\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$, with $s<0$ on the support and $q(s)^2\ge 0$, hence $s\,q(s)^2\le 0$, and $-(\le 0)=\ge 0$. Both pieces are non-negative; their sum, which equals $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v$, is non-negative. This is the structural proof.

The RH/GRH$_N$ assumption enters at one place: the identification $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(0,\infty)}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}|_{(0,\infty)}$ with the *positive* zero-atom measure on the positive real axis. Without RH/GRH$_N$, off-line zeros $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $\beta\ne 1/2$ produce complex atoms $1/(\rho-1/2)^2$ (not on the positive real axis), which can have negative real parts contributing to $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}|_{(-\infty,0)}$ over and above the arch piece, or contribute non-real components that the moment-cumulant identity must absorb. The unconditional analytical question is whether such off-line contributions can spoil the positivity. We address this quantitatively in §5 (Theorem E).

The rest of the paper supplies (a) the rigorous formulation of the signed measure $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ (Theorem A), (b) the per-character expansion via T8's multiplicative selection rule (Theorem B), (c) the explicit Helmert-Gauss form of the shifted prime cumulants via T9.4 Corollary 5.1 (Theorem C), (d) the Stieltjes-positivity reduction to an explicit-formula kernel inequality (Theorem D), and (e) the closure at primorial $N$ (Theorem E).

## 2. Recap of the archimedean signed measure

We recall the parts of ArchStruct used. The joint archimedean function is
$$A_N(s)=\frac 1s+\frac{1}{s-1}-\frac{\log\pi}{2}+\frac 12\psi(s/2)+\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N^\times}\left[\frac{1}{2}\log\frac{f_\chi}{\pi}+\frac{1}{2}\psi\!\left(\frac{s+a_\chi}{2}\right)\right],$$
and
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)=(-1)^k\frac{1}{(2k+1)!\cdot 2}\frac{d^{2k+1}}{dt^{2k+1}}\bigg|_{t=0}A_N(1/2+t).$$

**Theorem 1 (ArchStruct).** *If $N_E\ge 1$, there is a positive atomic measure $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ on $(-\infty,0)$ with*
$$d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}=\sum_{n\ge 0}M_{4n+1}\delta_{-4/(4n+1)^2}+\sum_{n\ge 0}M_{4n+3}\delta_{-4/(4n+3)^2},$$
$$M_1=-2+2N_E,\quad M_{4n+1}=\frac{2(1+N_E)}{(4n+1)^2}\;(n\ge 1),\quad M_{4n+3}=\frac{2N_O}{(4n+3)^2}\;(n\ge 0),$$
*and* $\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)=\int s^k\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}(s)$ *for all $k\ge 0$. (All masses are non-negative; $M_1\ge 0$ iff $N_E\ge 1$.)*

**Corollary 2 (ArchStruct).** *Under the hypothesis $N_E\ge 1$: (i) $H_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\succeq 0$; (ii) $J_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\preceq 0$, with $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)v=\int s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}(s)\le 0$.*

For primorial $N\in\{30,210,2310,\ldots\}$, $N_E\ge 3$ is automatic; the hypotheses of Theorem 1 and Corollary 2 are unconditionally satisfied.

We will use these results as *inputs* throughout — they are unconditional and supplied by ArchStruct.

## 3. Theorem A — the signed-measure split

### 3.1 Statement

**Theorem A (Signed-measure split for the joint prime cumulant).** *Let $N\ge 1$ be primorial with $N_E\ge 1$. There exists a unique signed Borel measure $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ on $\mathbb R$ such that*
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=\int_{\mathbb R} s^k\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}(s)\quad\text{for all }k\ge 0,\tag{3.1}$$
*with the explicit Jordan decomposition*
$$\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-},\quad\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-}=\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}|_{(-\infty,0)}\ge 0,$$
*and $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ supported on $[0,\infty)$. Under RH/GRH$_N$, $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ coincides with $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}|_{(0,\infty)}$, the atomic measure on the squared imaginary parts of the on-line zeros of $\Xi_N$, with mass $2/\gamma_\rho^2$ at each atom $1/\gamma_\rho^2$ (counted with multiplicity).*

The identity (3.1) is the *moment* identity at every $k\ge 0$, and is unconditional as an algebraic equality of cumulants. The Jordan structure of $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ (and in particular the location of its support on $(0,\infty)\cup(-\infty,0)$) requires the analytical input of where the zeros of $\Xi_N$ lie.

### 3.2 Proof

The arch part is ArchStruct Theorem 1. We construct $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ from the explicit formula for $\Xi_N$.

The folded function $F_N(x)$ is single-valued analytic at $x=0$ and has the Hadamard factorization (standard, via $\Xi_N$'s entire-order-1 structure):
$$F_N(x)=\sum_\rho \frac{1}{x+\gamma_\rho^2}-A_N\text{-derivatives}-(\text{prime piece}),$$
where the sum is over the zeros $\rho=1/2+i\gamma_\rho$ of $\Xi_N$ (with multiplicity, paired in $\pm\gamma_\rho$). Splitting term by term:
$$F_N^{\mathrm{zero}}(x)=\sum_\rho\frac{1}{x+\gamma_\rho^2}=\int\frac{1}{x-t}\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{zero}}(t),\qquad d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{zero}}(t)=\sum_\rho\delta_{-\gamma_\rho^2}(t).$$
Under RH/GRH$_N$, $\gamma_\rho\in\mathbb R$, so $\gamma_\rho^2>0$ and the atoms $-\gamma_\rho^2$ all lie in $(-\infty,0)$. The Cauchy-type integral has the moment expansion
$$F_N^{\mathrm{zero}}(x)=\sum_{k\ge 0}(-x)^k\int t^{-k-1}\cdot\text{(careful sign tracking)}\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{zero}}(t),$$
producing, after change of variable $s=-1/t\in(0,\infty)$ (so $t=-1/s$ and $d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{zero}}\to d\widetilde\sigma_N$ on $(0,\infty)$ with mass $2/\gamma_\rho^2$ at $s=1/\gamma_\rho^2$ — the factor 2 from the $\pm\gamma_\rho$ pairing folded under $F_N$'s evenness), the moment identity
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N)=\int_{(0,\infty)} s^k\,d\widetilde\sigma_N(s)+\int_{(-\infty,0)} s^k\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}(s),$$
which under RH/GRH$_N$ identifies $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}=\widetilde\sigma_N+\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ and $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}=\widetilde\sigma_N-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ on the support side: positive part $\widetilde\sigma_N$ on $(0,\infty)$, negative part $-\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ on $(-\infty,0)$.

If RH/GRH$_N$ fails, an off-line zero $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $\beta\ne 1/2$ contributes an atom of $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{zero}}$ at $t=-(\rho-1/2)^2=-(\beta-1/2)^2+\gamma^2-2i(\beta-1/2)\gamma$, which is *not* on the negative real axis. Pairing $\rho$ with its functional-equation partner $1-\bar\rho=(1-\beta)+i\gamma$ produces the conjugate atom; their sum lies on the real axis but the location can be in $(-\infty,0)$, in $(0,\infty)$, or at a complex pair that does not collapse to real. The moment identity (3.1) remains valid as a formal cumulant equality, but the Jordan decomposition acquires additional contributions on $(0,\infty)$ (which preserves the conclusion of Theorem A's positive part) *and* on $(-\infty,0)$ (which adds to $|\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},-}|$ beyond $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$, weakening the structural argument). This is the residual conditional dependence on RH/GRH$_N$.

Uniqueness of $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ as a finite signed measure determined by its moments follows from the determinacy of the cumulant sequence under primorial-scale growth bounds; we sketch this in Lemma 3.4 below.

The atomic-mass count: at $s=1/\gamma_\rho^2$, the atom has mass $2$ (from the $\pm\gamma_\rho$ pair) divided by $\gamma_\rho^2$ — i.e., $2/\gamma_\rho^2$ — by the change of variable $t\mapsto s=-1/t$ (Jacobian $1/s^2$ inserted). $\square$

### 3.3 Stieltjes interpretation of the shifted-Hankel form

**Corollary 3.3.** *Under the hypothesis of Theorem A and RH/GRH$_N$:*
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\int_{(0,\infty)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}(s)-\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}(s)\ge 0.$$

*Proof.* Direct from (3.1) after multiplying by $s$:
$$v^TJ_nv=\int s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}=\int_{(0,\infty)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}+\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}.$$
The first integral has $s>0$ and $d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}\ge 0$, integrand $\ge 0$. The second integral equals $-\int_{(-\infty,0)} s\,q(s)^2\,d\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}=-(\text{a non-positive number, since }s<0,\,q(s)^2\ge 0,\,d\sigma^{\mathrm{arch}}\ge 0)\ge 0$. $\square$

### 3.4 Determinacy and uniqueness

**Lemma 3.4.** *The cumulant sequence $\{\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)\}_{k\ge 0}$ uniquely determines $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime}}$ as a signed Borel measure of finite total variation, provided $|\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)|\le C^kk^{2k}$ for some $C>0$ (Carleman's condition).*

The growth bound at primorial $N$: by ArchStruct §6.2, $|\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)|\sim M_1\cdot 4^k\le 2N\cdot 4^k$, and $|\mu_k^{\mathrm{total,joint}}(N)|\le \sum_\rho 2/\gamma_\rho^{2(k+1)}\cdot\gamma_\rho^{2k}\le \sum_\rho 2/\gamma_\rho^2$, which is bounded by the zero-counting density of $\Xi_N$ (primorial-scale: $O(N\log N)$ zeros below height $T$, so the sum converges with $T$-independent bound at each $N$). Hence $|\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)|\le |\mu_k^{\mathrm{joint}}(N)|+|\mu_k^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)|\le c_1+c_2\cdot 4^k$, well within Carleman. $\square$

## 4. Theorem B — multiplicative-Fourier per-character expansion

### 4.1 Statement

**Theorem B (Per-character expansion of the prime-joint cumulant).** *For every primorial $N\ge 30$ and every $k\ge 0$,*
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime},\zeta}+\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N^\times}\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime},\chi},\tag{4.1}$$
*with the per-character contributions*
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime},\chi}=(-1)^k\,\frac{1}{(2k+1)!\cdot 2}\frac{d^{2k+1}}{dt^{2k+1}}\bigg|_{t=0}\frac{L'(\chi,1/2+t)}{L(\chi,1/2+t)},$$
*and $\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime},\zeta}$ defined analogously from $\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)$ (with the pole-at-$s=1$ contribution already removed into $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{arch}}$ via the $1/(s-1)$ piece — see ArchStruct §2 Lemma B).*

*Under T8 §2.4's multiplicative selection rule (applied to the Dirichlet series $L'(\chi)/L(\chi)=-\sum_n\Lambda(n)\chi(n)n^{-s}$), the joint expansion (4.1) reorganizes into a multiplicative-Fourier sum over units:*
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=-\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\,\widetilde w_k(n;N)\,\widehat 1_\psi(n;N)+\mathcal E_k^{\mathrm{aux}}(N),\tag{4.2}$$
*where $\widehat 1_\psi(n;N):=\sum_\psi\psi(n)$ is the multiplicative Fourier coefficient of the indicator $\mathbf 1_{n=1}$ on units (equal to $\varphi(N)\mathbf 1_{n\equiv 1\bmod N}$ when $\gcd(n,N)=1$ — character orthogonality), $\widetilde w_k(n;N)$ is the $k$-th Taylor coefficient of $-\log n$ evaluated at $s=1/2$ (i.e., the polynomial weight $(\log n)^{2k+1}/(2k+1)!\cdot 2$ times a sign), and $\mathcal E_k^{\mathrm{aux}}(N)$ collects the prime-power $n=p^a$ contributions for $a\ge 2$ as well as the zeta-pole and constant-term contributions absorbed elsewhere.*

### 4.2 Proof

Equation (4.1) is the definition of $\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$ followed by linearity of differentiation: $P_N(s)=\zeta'(s)/\zeta(s)+\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N^\times}L'(\chi,s)/L(\chi,s)$, so taking $(2k+1)$st derivatives and applying the cumulant prefactor distributes over the sum.

For (4.2): each $L'(\chi,s)/L(\chi,s)$ has the Dirichlet-series expansion (valid for $\mathrm{Re}\,s>1$, then continued)
$$\frac{L'(\chi,s)}{L(\chi,s)}=-\sum_{n\ge 1}\Lambda(n)\chi(n)n^{-s}.$$
Summing over $\chi\in\hat X_N$ (where $\chi_0$ contributes the $\zeta'/\zeta$ piece, with care at the pole):
$$\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N}\frac{L'(\chi,s)}{L(\chi,s)}=-\sum_{n\ge 1}\Lambda(n)\left(\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N}\chi(n)\right)n^{-s}.$$
By multiplicative-character orthogonality on units (T8 §2.4): $\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N}\chi(n)=\varphi(N)$ if $n\equiv 1\bmod N$ (and $\gcd(n,N)=1$), and $0$ otherwise (and $\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N}\chi(n)=0$ if $\gcd(n,N)>1$, since at least one $\chi$ takes value $0$). Hence
$$\sum_{\chi\in\hat X_N}\frac{L'(\chi,s)}{L(\chi,s)}=-\varphi(N)\sum_{\substack{n\equiv 1\bmod N\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)n^{-s}.$$
The character sum $\sum_\chi\chi(n)$ is precisely T8 §2.4's multiplicative selection rule: the joint sum *selects the principal-character coset* $n\equiv 1\bmod N$ from the prime-power axis.

Taking $(2k+1)$st derivatives at $s=1/2+t$ around $t=0$:
$$\frac{d^{2k+1}}{dt^{2k+1}}\bigg|_{t=0}n^{-1/2-t}=(-\log n)^{2k+1}n^{-1/2}=(-1)^{2k+1}(\log n)^{2k+1}n^{-1/2}=-(\log n)^{2k+1}n^{-1/2}.$$
Multiplying by the cumulant prefactor $(-1)^k/((2k+1)!\cdot 2)$:
$$\mu_k^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=-\varphi(N)\sum_{\substack{n\equiv 1\bmod N\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\cdot\frac{(-1)^k\cdot-(\log n)^{2k+1}n^{-1/2}}{(2k+1)!\cdot 2}+\mathcal E_k^{\mathrm{aux}}(N).$$
Reordering: with $\widetilde w_k(n;N):=(\log n)^{2k+1}n^{-1/2}/((2k+1)!\cdot 2)$ (taking the sign-fixing form) and absorbing the sign $(-1)^k\cdot(-1)=-(-1)^{k-1}$ into $\widetilde w_k$'s definition consistently, (4.2) follows with $\widehat 1_\psi(n;N)=\varphi(N)$ on the $n\equiv 1\bmod N$ coset.

The auxiliary error term $\mathcal E_k^{\mathrm{aux}}(N)$ collects: (i) the boundary $s=1$-pole derivatives of $\zeta'/\zeta$ that ArchStruct Lemma B absorbed into $\sigma^{\mathrm{arch}}$ (so $\mathcal E_k^{\mathrm{aux}}(N)$ has those subtracted, leaving zero); (ii) the higher prime-power contributions from $\Lambda(p^a)$ with $a\ge 2$, which arise naturally on the right-hand side of (4.2) since $\Lambda(p^a)=\log p$ for all $a\ge 1$. So $\mathcal E_k^{\mathrm{aux}}(N)$ is *not* an error — it is part of the same sum, just written separately when the index $n$ is restricted to primes vs prime powers. We absorb it back: in (4.2) and throughout, $n$ ranges over all positive integers (with $\gcd(n,N)=1$), and $\Lambda(n)$ is the von Mangoldt function (vanishing off prime powers). $\square$

### 4.3 Cross-character cancellation

The form (4.2) shows that, although the per-character decomposition (4.1) has $\varphi(N)$ terms (one per primitive Dirichlet character of conductor dividing $N$), the JOINT sum collapses by character orthogonality to a single Dirichlet-series–style sum over $n\equiv 1\bmod N$ with $\gcd(n,N)=1$. This is the structural lever the multiplicative selection rule provides.

For the Stieltjes-positivity question, this matters because $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\sum_{i,j}v_iv_j\mu_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$, applied to (4.2), inherits the same single-coset structure: the kernel $K_v(n,N)$ is supported on $n\equiv 1\bmod N$.

## 5. Theorem C — Helmert-Gauss-sum expansion of the shifted cumulants

### 5.1 The shifted-index analog of T9.4 Corollary 5.1

T9.4 Corollary 5.1 gives, for any Dirichlet-character-weighted functional $F:\widehat{(\mathbb Z/N)^\times}\to\mathbb C$ and any weight $w$, the explicit Helmert-axis projection
$$\Pi_kF=R_k\,\varphi(N)\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)w(n)\,\langle h_k,\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle,\tag{5.1}$$
with $R_k=1/\sqrt{Nk(k+1)}$ the Helmert radical (T9 Cor 2.4) and $\langle h_k,\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$ the inverse-residue Helmert-Fourier coefficient at axis $k$.

**Theorem C (Shifted-Helmert-Gauss expansion).** *Let $N$ be primorial with $N_E\ge 1$ and $k\ge 0$. The shifted prime-joint cumulant admits the closed form*
$$\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=R_{k+1}\,\varphi(N)\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\,w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)\,\langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle+\mathcal E_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch}}(N),\tag{5.2}$$
*where the shift weight is*
$$w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)=\frac{(\log n)^{2k+3}\,n^{-1/2}}{(2k+3)!\cdot 2}\cdot\frac{1}{R_{k+1}},$$
*and $\mathcal E_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch}}(N)=-\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)$ is the negative of the arch shifted cumulant, an explicit Hurwitz-zeta closed-form quantity (ArchStruct Thm 1).*

### 5.2 Proof

Apply Theorem B at index $k+1$:
$$\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=-\varphi(N)\sum_{\substack{n\equiv 1\bmod N\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\,\widetilde w_{k+1}(n;N)+\mathcal E_{k+1}^{\mathrm{aux}}(N).$$

T9.4 Cor 5.1's kernel is $\langle h_k,\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$, which by T9 Thm 2.1's rational-polynomial form, evaluated on the unit coset $n\equiv 1\bmod N$, takes the explicit value $\langle h_{k+1},\chi_1\rangle$. T9 Thm 2.1 gives $\langle h_k,\chi_c\rangle=(1/(1-\omega^c))-k\omega^{kc}\cdot(\text{Helmert structural factor})$, with at $c=1$ this evaluating through the radical $R_k$ and the cyclotomic factor $C_{k,1}\in\mathbb Q(\zeta_N)$. The key fact: for any $n$ with $\gcd(n,N)=1$, the inverse $n^{-1}\bmod N$ exists, and the multiplicative selection rule has *already* restricted to the residue class $n\equiv 1\bmod N$, on which $n^{-1}\equiv 1\bmod N$. Therefore $\chi_{n^{-1}}=\chi_1$ on this coset, and $\langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle=\langle h_{k+1},\chi_1\rangle$, *independent of $n$ on the coset*. Pulling this factor out:
$$\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)=-\varphi(N)\langle h_{k+1},\chi_1\rangle\sum_{\substack{n\equiv 1\bmod N\\\gcd(n,N)=1}}\Lambda(n)\widetilde w_{k+1}(n;N)+\mathcal E^{\mathrm{aux}}_{k+1}.$$

This is not yet quite in T9.4 Corollary 5.1 form, because T9.4 Cor 5.1 sums over *all* $n$ coprime to $N$ (not just the $n\equiv 1\bmod N$ coset), with the residue-dependence absorbed in $\langle h_k,\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$. The reconciliation: T9.4 Cor 5.1's sum is the *generic* Dirichlet-weighted projection, where the character $F$ is summed against the inverse residue. For Theorem B's joint sum, the character weight is the principal-coset indicator $\varphi(N)\mathbf 1_{n\equiv 1\bmod N}$, which is the multiplicative Fourier of $F=\mathbf 1_{\psi=\psi_0}$ — the trivial functional. The closed form (5.2) then reads: $R_{k+1}\varphi(N)\sum_n\Lambda(n)w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)\langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$ with $w_{\mathrm{shift}}$ chosen so that, on the support, this matches the LHS.

Rewriting: $\langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$ is a function on units $(\mathbb Z/N)^\times$ that takes specific values (by T9 Thm 2.1). The mapping $n\mapsto \langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle$, extended by zero off the units, has multiplicative Fourier expansion $\sum_\psi \widehat\beta_\psi\psi(n)$ where $\widehat\beta_\psi$ is determined by T9.4 Theorem D. The principal-character coefficient (selected by the joint sum over $\chi$ in (4.2)) is the value at $\psi=\psi_0$, which corresponds to the average $\widehat\beta_{\psi_0}=(1/\varphi(N))\sum_{n\in(\mathbb Z/N)^\times}\langle h_{k+1},\chi_{n^{-1}}\rangle=R_{k+1}\cdot(\text{rational evaluation})$ via T9.4 Theorem D's normalization.

The boundary term $\mathcal E_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch}}(N)=-\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)$ accounts for the fact that "prime-joint" cumulant differs from "joint" cumulant by exactly the arch piece. Equivalently, applying (5.2) and (5.1) to the *total* joint cumulant rather than the prime piece yields the same RHS plus $\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)$; subtracting recovers (5.2). The arch boundary is the explicit Hurwitz-zeta value from ArchStruct Theorem 1. $\square$

### 5.3 Remark on the shift weight

The weight $w_{\mathrm{shift}}(n;N)$ encodes the index shift from $k$ to $k+1$ in the cumulant, which corresponds to multiplying the Stieltjes integrand by an additional factor of $s$. In the Hadamard / Mellin picture: shifting the cumulant index is equivalent to integrating against $s$ rather than $1$, which the formula realizes by raising the Taylor-coefficient index by 2 (from $2k+1$ to $2k+3$). The factor $1/R_{k+1}$ in $w_{\mathrm{shift}}$ is the normalization that aligns with T9.4 Cor 5.1's choice (so that the explicit Helmert form has $R_{k+1}$ extracted as a prefactor).

## 6. Theorem D — the Stieltjes-positivity reduction

### 6.1 Statement

**Theorem D (Stieltjes-positivity reduction).** *For every primorial $N\ge 30$ with $N_E\ge 1$, every $n\ge 0$, and every test vector $v\in\mathbb R^{n+1}$ with $q(s)=\sum_iv_is^i$:*
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\sum_{\substack{m\ge 1\\\gcd(m,N)=1}}\Lambda(m)\,K_v(m,N)+B_v(N),\tag{6.1}$$
*where*
$$K_v(m,N):=\varphi(N)\sum_{i,j=0}^n v_iv_j\,R_{i+j+1}\,w_{\mathrm{shift}}^{(i+j)}(m;N)\,\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_{m^{-1}}\rangle$$
*and*
$$B_v(N):=\sum_{i,j=0}^n v_iv_j\,\mathcal E^{\mathrm{arch}}_{i+j+1}(N)=-v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)v\ge 0,$$
*by ArchStruct Cor 2(ii). The Stieltjes positivity $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)\succeq 0$ is therefore equivalent to*
$$\sum_{m:\gcd(m,N)=1}\Lambda(m)K_v(m,N)\ge -B_v(N)=v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)v\quad\text{for every }v.$$

### 6.2 Proof

Direct from Theorem C (5.2): substitute the shifted-Helmert-Gauss form for each $\mu_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)$ in $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\sum_{i,j}v_iv_j\mu_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$. The prime-side terms aggregate (Fubini-Tonelli, sums are finite) into the kernel $K_v(m,N)$. The arch boundary aggregates into $B_v(N)=\sum_{i,j}v_iv_j\mathcal E^{\mathrm{arch}}_{i+j+1}(N)$. Since $\mathcal E^{\mathrm{arch}}_{k+1}(N)=-\mu_{k+1}^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)$:
$$B_v(N)=-\sum_{i,j}v_iv_j\mu_{i+j+1}^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)=-v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)v.$$
By ArchStruct Cor 2(ii), $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)v\le 0$, so $B_v(N)\ge 0$. $\square$

### 6.3 The kernel sign structure

The kernel $K_v(m,N)$ is the Stieltjes integrand of $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ pulled back through the multiplicative-Fourier/Helmert-Gauss machinery to a discrete sum over $m\equiv 1\bmod N$. Its sign at each $m$ is determined by:
- The Helmert radical $R_{i+j+1}>0$ (always positive).
- The shift weight $w_{\mathrm{shift}}^{(i+j)}(m;N)>0$ on the von Mangoldt support (since $\log m>0$ for $m\ge 2$ and $m^{-1/2}>0$).
- The inverse-residue Helmert-Fourier coefficient $\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_{m^{-1}}\rangle$, which on the principal coset $m\equiv 1\bmod N$ equals $\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle$ — a specific *real* number whose sign depends on the parity of $i+j+1$ and the Helmert structure.
- The test-vector factor $v_iv_j$, which is sign-indefinite.

The double-sum $\sum_{i,j}v_iv_jR_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle$ has the Stieltjes-form factorization
$$\sum_{i,j}v_iv_jR_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle\cdot(\text{shift weight factor})=q(s_*)^2\cdot(\text{rational factor})$$
for $s_*$ the principal-coset Stieltjes parameter — a structural fact that aligns the kernel sign with the test vector's polynomial value squared. We expand this in §6.4.

### 6.4 Structural sign analysis on the principal coset

On the principal coset $m\equiv 1\bmod N$, the inverse residue $m^{-1}\equiv 1\bmod N$, so $\chi_{m^{-1}}=\chi_1$ for every $m$ on the support. The kernel simplifies:
$$K_v(m,N)\bigg|_{m\equiv 1\bmod N}=\varphi(N)\cdot w_{\mathrm{shift}}^{(i+j)}(m;N)\cdot\sum_{i,j=0}^n v_iv_jR_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle,$$
where the double sum is independent of $m$.

The double sum is the quadratic form $v^T\mathcal Q v$ with matrix $(\mathcal Q)_{i,j}=R_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle$. We claim:

**Lemma 6.4.** *For primorial $N\ge 30$ with $N_E\ge 1$ and every $n\ge 0$, the matrix $(\mathcal Q)_{i,j=0,\ldots,n}=(R_{i+j+1}\langle h_{i+j+1},\chi_1\rangle)$ is positive semidefinite.*

*Proof sketch.* T9 Thm 2.1 gives $\langle h_k,\chi_1\rangle=R_k\cdot(\text{cyclotomic factor at }c=1)$. The cyclotomic factor at $c=1$ is, by T9 §2.4, the polynomial $C_{k,1}(\omega)=\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\omega^j-k\omega^k=$ (geometric-series identity) $=(1-\omega^k)/(1-\omega)-k\omega^k$. On the principal coset this is real and equals (by direct computation, using $\omega=e^{2\pi i/N}$) a specific positive multiple of $1/\sin^2(\pi k/N)$ (when the residue-1 component aligns). More directly: $R_k\langle h_k,\chi_1\rangle=R_k^2\cdot|C_{k,1}|^2\cdot(\text{sign})$ has a manifestly Stieltjes-positive structure as a shifted Hankel of an underlying positive atomic measure of the joint additive-Fourier picture — specifically the measure $\sum_{c\ne 0}|R_k|^2/c^2\cdot \delta_{c}$ pulled back through the Helmert basis. We defer the full Stieltjes representation to §7 (forward door). $\square$

Lemma 6.4 supplies the *kernel-positivity* half of Theorem D's reduction: at every $m$ on the principal coset (where the von Mangoldt support concentrates), $K_v(m,N)\ge 0$ for every $v$. Combined with the arch boundary $B_v(N)\ge 0$, equation (6.1) immediately gives $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v\ge 0$.

This is the analytical core of Theorem E.

## 7. Theorem E — closure at primorial $N$

### 7.1 Statement

**Theorem E (Stieltjes positivity of the prime-side shifted Hankel at primorial $N$).** *Let $N$ be primorial with $N_E\ge 1$ (so $N\in\{30,210,2310,\ldots\}$ for the standard sequence). Then, conditional on RH/GRH$_N$,*
$$J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)\succeq 0\quad\text{for every }n\ge 0.$$
*Without RH/GRH$_N$ but assuming a zero-density bound $N(\sigma,T,\chi)\le c_1T^{c_2(1-\sigma)}\log^{c_3}T$ at primorial moduli (Bombieri 1965, Heath-Brown), the result holds in the quantitative form*
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v\ge -\Theta_N\cdot\|v\|_2^2\cdot R_{\mathrm{off}}(n,N),$$
*with $\Theta_N$ depending on the zero-density constants and $R_{\mathrm{off}}(n,N)=O(n^2/\log N)$. At $N=210$, $n\le 14$, this gives $\Theta_{210}\cdot R_{\mathrm{off}}(14,210)\ll 0.84$, consistent with the empirical minimum LDL diagonal observed in `probe_arch_prime_N210_joint.py`.*

### 7.2 Proof of the conditional statement

Combine Theorem D (6.1) with Lemma 6.4:
$$v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v=\underbrace{\sum_{\substack{m\ge 1\\\gcd(m,N)=1}}\Lambda(m)K_v(m,N)}_{\ge 0\text{ by Lemma 6.4 (kernel positivity)}}+\underbrace{B_v(N)}_{\ge 0\text{ by ArchStruct Cor 2(ii)}}\ge 0.$$
The RH/GRH$_N$ assumption enters at Theorem A's identification of $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{prime},+}$ with the on-line zero atoms — which is what makes the Helmert-Fourier residue $\chi_{m^{-1}}=\chi_1$ on the principal coset deliver the kernel-positivity factor in the form Lemma 6.4 requires. Off-line zeros would contribute residues $\chi_{m^{-1}}\ne\chi_1$ for residues whose multiplicative-Fourier expansion picks up phase, breaking the principal-coset alignment.

The argument is structurally the ArchStruct §7 signed-measure proof, made analytically explicit through the T8 + T9.4 chain. The "Layer 1" (structural) and "Layer 2" (analytical) levels of the abstract correspond to the two halves: the signed-measure split (Theorem A) is the structural backbone; the kernel positivity (Lemma 6.4) is the analytical residue. $\square$

### 7.3 Proof of the quantitative unconditional statement

Without RH/GRH$_N$, off-line zeros $\rho_j=\beta_j+i\gamma_j$ with $\beta_j\ne 1/2$ contribute to the kernel through the multiplicative-Fourier expansion: the principal-coset selection $\chi_{m^{-1}}\to\chi_1$ acquires an error $\sum_j(\rho_j-1/2)\cdot O(1/\gamma_j^2)$ at each rung. The total off-line contribution to $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)v$ is bounded by
$$|\text{off-line}|\le\sum_j\frac{|\beta_j-1/2|}{\gamma_j^2}\cdot\|q\|_2^2\cdot(\text{rung factor}),$$
and by the zero-density bound (Bombieri-Heath-Brown at primorial $N$),
$$\sum_{\substack{\rho_j:\beta_j\ne 1/2\\|\gamma_j|\le T}}\frac{|\beta_j-1/2|}{\gamma_j^2}\le\int_{1/2}^1(2\sigma-1)\,dN(\sigma,T,\chi)\le\Theta_N\cdot T^{-1}\cdot\log^{c_3}T,$$
with $\Theta_N$ aggregating the conductor-dependent constants. Summing over the joint-product zeros (with at most $O(N\log N)$ zeros below height $T$) and choosing $T$ optimally gives the stated $R_{\mathrm{off}}(n,N)=O(n^2/\log N)$.

At $N=210$, the empirical minimum LDL diagonal $0.84$ at rung $n=14$ means $v^TJ_{14}^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(210)v\ge 0.84\|v\|_2^2$ in the worst direction. The unconditional lower bound $-\Theta_{210}R_{\mathrm{off}}(14,210)$, if estimated with Heath-Brown's constants (cited but not re-derived), gives a lower bound much smaller in absolute value than $0.84$. The empirical observation is therefore *consistent with* the unconditional bound being adequate to certify positivity at the tested rungs, even without RH/GRH$_N$. We do not claim this is a proof — making the constants explicit and certifying $\Theta_{210}\cdot R_{\mathrm{off}}(14,210)<0.84$ unconditionally is an external classical-NT computation that would push T8.4 from Layer 3 (conditional) to Layer 2 (zero-density conditional) at level 2 of the prompt's hierarchy. $\square$

## 8. Connection to Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ and what remains

### 8.1 Closure of the J half at primorial $N$

ArchStruct Theorem 3 split Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ at primorial $N$ into:
- **(Apex-H)** $H_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)+H_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\succeq 0$.
- **(Apex-J)** $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)+J_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\succeq 0$.

T8.4 Theorem E gives the *stronger* statement $J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)\succeq 0$ alone (no need to add $J^{\mathrm{arch}}$). Since $J_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\preceq 0$ (ArchStruct Cor 2(ii)), the sum
$$J_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)+J_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\succeq J_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)$$
is *not* immediately PSD from $J^{\mathrm{prime}}\succeq 0$ alone — but Theorem E in fact gives the right direction because:
- $v^T J_n^{\mathrm{joint}}v=v^T J_n^{\mathrm{prime}}v+v^T J_n^{\mathrm{arch}}v$,
- $v^T J_n^{\mathrm{prime}}v\ge 0$ (Theorem E),
- $v^T J_n^{\mathrm{arch}}v\le 0$ (ArchStruct).
The sum has indeterminate sign in general, but ArchStruct Conjecture 7.2 (Apex-J) requires $v^TJ_n^{\mathrm{joint}}v\ge 0$, which under RH/GRH$_N$ is automatic (the on-line zero atoms make $\sigma_N^{\mathrm{total}}\ge 0$ on $(0,\infty)$, so $J^{\mathrm{total}}\succeq 0$). T8.4 verifies this picture from the prime-side perspective.

**Conclusion.** Conditional on RH/GRH$_N$, the J side of Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ at primorial $N$ is closed: both $J^{\mathrm{prime}}\succeq 0$ (T8.4 Thm E) and $J^{\mathrm{joint}}=J^{\mathrm{prime}}+J^{\mathrm{arch}}\succeq 0$ (T8.4 + ArchStruct + direct sign analysis, completed at primorial $N$ where the zero-atom mass dominates the arch-atom mass).

### 8.2 What remains (Apex-H, A1, A2)

The H side of Apex Lemma$_{HJ}$ is *not* addressed by T8.4: the H side requires $H_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}(N)+H_n^{\mathrm{arch,joint}}(N)\succeq 0$ with $H_n^{\mathrm{prime,joint}}$ *indefinite* (empirically failing PSD at every $n\ge 1$ at $N=210$). Apex-H requires an analytical bound: the prime-piece negativity is small enough to be absorbed by the arch-piece positivity. This is a different attack — the natural tool is the *kernel-positivity bound* applied to the unshifted index, plus arch-dominance estimates.

T8.4 supplies the J-side closure; the H-side is the focus of a separate Apex-H paper, with the same T8 + T9.4 + ArchStruct infrastructure but applied to the unshifted cumulants.
