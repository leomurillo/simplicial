## 5. Information geometry — FDIG 2025 open-problems collection (Sei & Matsuzoe, arXiv 2509.06989; read in full)

Direct hits, by contributor:

- **S. Ito (§2):** (a) relations between Wasserstein/Benamou–Brenier structure and
  *entropy production rate for Markov jump processes* — adjacent to the IBS package
  (C4–C6: Δ certificate, arrow dimension β₁); our levers answer a sibling question
  (what part of the entropy production is certifiable from symmetric data), worth a
  targeted note. (b) **"In quantum information theory, the variety of the quantum
  Fisher metric is introduced by using the generalized mean. Is there a framework for
  treating this variety in information geometry?"** — this is, almost verbatim, the
  question T24 (B8) answers: the fork is located in the operator-ordering of the apex
  generator, and operational principles select members (additivity/dual-flatness →
  Kubo–Mori; conservative flow → Bures). A named 2025 open problem our material
  addresses directly.
- **T. Kobayashi (§3):** a dually-flat structure for *dynamics* (velocity/force on
  tangent/cotangent pairs over a conventional dually-flat base) — papers 13–15 + 24
  (simplectic/information-geometric mechanics on the categorical simplex, cubic
  mechanics with Fisher kinetic term) are exactly a finite worked construction of
  this; D1's space=mass theorem is the sharpest statement. Strong fit.
- **T. Matsuda (§4):** is the operator Sinkhorn algorithm an alternating minimization
  of some divergence? (open because ∇^(e) has non-vanishing torsion on density
  matrices) — touches B8/B2 (the same operator-ordering torsion is where the T24 fork
  lives); plausible bounded target, not currently claimed.
- **H. Nagaoka (§6):** (1) his remark that the classical coincidence of estimation
  and large-deviation geometry is "a fortunate coincidence due to Chentsov's
  uniqueness" is precisely the program's classical-tower caveat (all monotone metrics
  agree classically; the quantum fork is real). (2) Determine ∇^(e)-autoparallel
  submanifolds of density operators (formal quantum exponential families, nontrivial
  by torsion) — adjacent to T7/T24; the apex generator's ℂ[ℤ/6] ≅ ℂ²⊗ℂ³ lift is a
  worked finite instance.
- **T. Sei (§7):** characterize non-convex expectation parameter spaces (non-steep
  log-partitions) — adjacent to T0's gauge selection and the steepness of the
  categorical Φ; no current claim.

Net: the quantum-metric-selection target (survey item 15) is upgraded — it now has a
named, current, community-posed open problem it answers, removing the main
"synthesis-vs-new" risk flagged in the ranking.
