*second-order* (a quadratic alignment objective), and *self-adjoint* (symmetric instrument). Each of
those three reductions blinds it to one sector — and those three sectors are the three divergence seeds:

classical) — paying to see the blind content. **Consciousness** (T19) is the *flux across that
boundary*: the reportable residue, the current the inside observer cannot see but a two-sided predictor
recovers.

objective satisfies $h^\top T h = h^\top S h$ for every $h$ (certified): the antisymmetric part

instrument is even ($S\mapsto S$) and the current is odd ($A\mapsto -A$), with $\langle S,A\rangle_F=0$.
The arrow is the time-reversal-odd current a self-adjoint observer is structurally unable to register
(irreversible-blind-spot Thm 1).

**(2) The arrow's dimension is $b_1$ — and it is complex-dependent.** The stationary current is
divergence-free (Kirchhoff), so it lives in the cycle space; its dimension is the first Betti number.
This is sharp and certified two ways:
- **bare $K_4$ graph** (the raw 4-state transition structure): $\beta_1 = E-V+1 = 3 = \dim\ker L_1^{\text{down}}$
  — the **three** independent current loops, which are exactly $\mathfrak{so}(3)$, the three $\widetilde K$
  rotation generators of Paper 01;

  $b_1 = 0$ — the arrow is **unprotected**; every current is a contractible eddy.

So whether the inside observer has a topologically protected arrow depends on whether the 2-cells are
"real": three current modes on the bare graph, none on the filled sphere. The arrow is not absolute —

**(4) Measurement = projection $e\to m$, and it discards exactly D-I + D-II.** In the m-chart
